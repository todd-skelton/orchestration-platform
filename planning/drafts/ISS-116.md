## Why

After ISS-102 the review contract is `parseReview` (38 lines) and
`validateLocations` (20 lines). `scripts/dogfood/repair-policy.ts` is still
813 lines: eight interfaces, seventeen hand-written exact-key checks, a 25-key
config validator, a 27-key authority validator, an unused
`validRepairReviewPath`, an unused `ReviewReportClassification`, and a
`prepare`/`acceptDelta` policy that restates what `flow.ts` already pinned.
The reviewer JSON is also already enforced by the Codex `--output-schema`.

## Done when

- `repair-policy.ts` exports `parseReview`, `validateLocations`, the
  `ReviewFinding` and `ValidatedReview` types, and the three checks the queue
  acts on: terminal status, head match, findings within the candidate.
- `validateRepairConfig`, `validateAuthority`, `classifyReview` (callers use
  try/catch), `validRepairReviewPath`, `ReviewReportClassification`, and the
  15-key `prior` and 8-key `launchContext` checks are deleted.
- `repair-adapter.ts` and `queue.ts` callers are updated; no behavior visible
  to a worker or to GitHub changes.
- Net deletion of at least 500 source lines, stated in the PR body.

## Out of scope

Changing the verdict, findings, G0 shape. Changing prompts.
