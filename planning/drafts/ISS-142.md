## Why

The ISS-141 recovery in `m2-jpeg-20260914` preserved hosted failure logs and
partial work, then attempt 4's author and independent reviewer passed exact
candidate `ac2c54ce80865c0501d71aae4676c719b0d1eaf1`. Delivery stopped at
`unreviewed-delivery-source` because the adapter requires exactly four or five
attempt-record keys, but the native retry producer writes `retryContext` as
a sixth key. The recorded incident is
https://github.com/todd-skelton/orchestration-platform/issues/367#issuecomment-5661692847.
The existing attempt remains in delivery with one consumed worker retry; a
fifth implementation attempt or rewritten worker record is not a recovery.

## Done when

- Accept the actual native retry record at the reviewed delivery boundary by
  removing unnecessary defensive shape restrictions, not adding another schema
  or migrating runtime records. Preserve author/reviewer identity, independent
  review, terminal verdict and exact candidate requirements.
- Regression tests use the actual retry-record producer and real
  `githubDeliveryAdapter.source`, not a handcrafted incomplete retry shape or
  mocked source consumer. Prove successful retry delivery and same-state restart
  without replaying workers, rewriting prior records or resetting retry budgets.
- Ordinary invalid source identity, review verdict and candidate-head cases
  remain rejected. All local and hosted delivery gates remain required.
- Document the recorded blocker and stopped-upgrade recovery in docs/loop.md;
  run typecheck, format:check, planning:check and test. Leave the stopped native
  run, product candidate and historical records unchanged during implementation.

## Out of scope

Product edits, imported PASS, new attempts or retry ceilings, runtime migration,
CI waivers, publication, merge, deployment and M2 completion claims.
