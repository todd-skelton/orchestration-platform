## Context and evidence

The round-373 replan found the delivery loop has no feedback phase: release
and review produce receipts, but no discovery surface consumes them, so intake
of new platform work is permanently operator prose. Once the kernel journal
(`ISS-010`) emits cycle facts and the module packaging seam (`ISS-011`)
exists, measured telemetry — cycle outcomes, refusals, review dispositions —
is the cheapest honest discovery input. Until those land, intake remains
operator judgment and no anticipatory intake machinery is built; this
registration names the consuming moment instead of freezing surface early.

## Scope fence

In: an advisory intake-record contract bound to the journal facts that
produced it; a deterministic, idempotent reduction from journal telemetry to
candidate intake records shaped as thin registrations (context, outcome,
proposed dependency edges); an operator disposition transcript that accepts a
record toward ordinary draft registration or rejects it with a reason; and
replay fixtures.

Out: automatic registration or any mutation of `planning/roadmap.json`
outside the ordinary reviewed-change path, priority scoring models, reading
consumer backlogs or boards, altering PASS/BLOCK or breaker semantics, and
any authority claim — intake records are evidence, never authority.

## Decisions already made

The platform proposes; the reviewed change disposes. Intake records are
advisory only and carry provenance to specific journal facts. Reduction is
deterministic: the same journal yields byte-identical records, and reruns
create no duplicates. Prediction: after the first healthy self-hosted routine
cycle, at least one registered slice originates from a measured intake record.
Review-by horizon: the `ISS-015` first stable-predecessor release, with a
recorded keep/revise/delete result.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. Every intake record binds to the journal facts that produced it; a record
   with missing, dangling, or unmeasured provenance refuses. Evidence: schema
   fixtures with one negative control per provenance field.
2. Reduction over a fixture journal is deterministic and idempotent: repeated
   runs yield byte-identical records and no duplicates. Evidence: repeat-run
   transcript comparison and a duplicate-detection canary.
3. A disposition transcript answers every intake record exactly once, as
   accepted with its registered draft key or rejected with a non-empty
   reason; omission, duplication, or an answer for an unknown record refuses
   completion. Evidence: transcript mutants.
4. An accepted record produces a thin registration that passes
   `planning:check` through the ordinary reviewed-change path; nothing writes
   the roadmap or board directly. Evidence: fixture registration transcript
   and a direct-write canary.

## Operator actions

None at registration; the implemented module adds one bounded disposition
step per intake batch.

## Independent review packet seed

Attack intake records without measured provenance, reduction that differs
across replays or hosts, silent registration without a disposition,
disposition answers duplicated or skipped, intake smuggling consumer-backlog
policy through telemetry, and advisory records acquiring authority language.
Use the replay transcripts and the direct-write canary.
