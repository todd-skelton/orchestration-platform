## Decision

New recovery must use one ordinary reviewed planning path without corrupting retained grants.
Deleting every grant reader immediately is simpler but would strand saved runs and misread historical receipts.
Choose removal of fresh grant issuance/admission while isolating only the compatibility needed to observe and finish already-reserved work.
Accept a bounded historical reader path until a read-only census proves no unfinished consumer remains.
Revisit if a second real lineage requires a capability ordinary planning cannot express; do not prebuild a general grant schema.
