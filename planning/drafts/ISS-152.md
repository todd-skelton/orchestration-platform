## Done when

1. **AC1: Attribute before correction.** The trusted terminal gate result
   distinguishes candidate-caused, host/executor/tooling and unknown failure.
   Nonzero exit or author opinion alone is insufficient. Admit only an exact
   reviewed head with a compiler/formatter/test diagnostic tied to
   committed candidate code. Startup/tool absence, log I/O, install, drift and
   cleanup failures are not candidate defects. Missing/incomplete diagnostics,
   resource/timeouts without a discriminator, mixed causes and unrecognized
   results stay stopped/unknown without correction or publication. Known host
   failures stop without parking. Evidence: delivery-adapter/supervision tests
   hold command/head fixed while varying each cause.
2. **AC2: Correct from the failed artifact once.** After author and independent
   review PASS, one local correction starts at the exact failed head in the
   same implementation attempt, never main or arbitrary HEAD on resume. The
   commit descends from that head; retain the delivery main base/full diff.
   Prompt: exact executable/argv/cwd, full diagnostic artifact path, failing
   test identities (or non-test diagnostics), failed head, original acceptance
   and preserved author/reviewer records/traces. Capture full output before
   dispatch; a tail is insufficient. Limit correction to the failure/direct
   causes. Evidence: native queue fixture checks actual launch input/base and
   Git ancestry.
3. **AC3: Renew review authority.** Require fresh independent DELTA review at
   the corrected exact head, with predecessor complete review, failed artifact,
   correction execution evidence, both bases and changed hunks/direct callers.
   Prior PASS is context, not changed-code authority; never falsify a failed
   source review to enter repair. Only current PASS supplies delivery's review
   ID/source evidence. Blocking DELTA review stops, without another pair.
   Evidence: native tests reject predecessor-only/wrong-head PASS and
   observes distinct corrective author/reviewer identities.
4. **AC4: Deliver normally.** Rerun all ordinary local gates at the resulting
   head, including previously green and after-mirror gates; old receipts remain
   history. Current-main refresh retains fresh review without renewing repair.
   Reconcile applied mirrors/publication intents normally; do not rewrite an
   in-flight publication. Publication, hosted checks, merge and applicable
   deployment bind the final reviewed head. Evidence: delivery integration
   reaches publication/completion and rejects stale receipts after
   refresh.
5. **AC5: Keep the ceiling finite.** Share the existing one local correction
   allowance across gate names and original/refreshed delivery, not another
   allowance beside typecheck/format. Second candidate failure, correction FAIL
   or blocking DELTA review yields a typed work stop/learning note and ordinary
   parking, not another pair or automatic new implementation. Preserve the
   implementation count/ceiling, native launch ceiling, routing and bounded
   retries. Failed/refused/dead launches stay charged; smaller authority
   wins, including ISS-145's no-repair-pair restriction. Evidence: queue tests
   exhaust across different gates/refresh, native launches and accepted replan.
6. **AC6: Resume without erasure.** Records distinguish failed-gate capture,
   author, DELTA review, corrected gates and terminal delivery/stop. Observe the
   in-flight worker/reconcile completion; never restart unrelated authorship,
   reuse old PASS or reset counts. Count each actual launch once; retain all
   participant IDs/placements/outcomes/usage. First log, source/review and stop
   evidence stay unchanged. Completion and stop are steady:
   replay launches nobody and repeats no completed publication/merge. Evidence:
   interrupted/completed replay compares original bytes and participant IDs.
7. **AC7: Document in `docs/loop.md`.** Explain typed stops, shared correction
   limit, fresh DELTA review and resume. #457 remains unready until ISS-152
   lands reviewed/hosted-green and the host installs the stable executor.
   Landing permits readiness restoration, not preserved-run restart/edit.
   Evidence: planning/format checks and semantic review of this unpark text.
