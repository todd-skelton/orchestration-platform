## Why

ISS-103 landed the one-retry rule as about 430 lines: a second `gate-retry-`
record namespace with prefix plumbing through `flow.ts`, `delivery.ts`,
`delivery-adapter.ts` and `queue.ts`, a `gate-retry-stop` record, deferred
delivery records, restart shortcuts, and a configurable
`exitReceiptWindowMs` validated at 23 sites. All of it exists so a crash
mid-retry never repeats the retry. Rule 9 accepts one repeated launch instead.

## Done when

- Gate failure: inside `runGates` in `delivery.ts`, a typecheck or format
  failure launches one corrective author on the same branch and re-runs the
  gates once; the count lives in the attempt record's `retries` field. The
  `gate-retry-` records, `correcting-gate` status, `recordPrefix` and
  `artifactPrefix` plumbing, and the queue restart shortcuts are deleted.
- Malformed verdict: the reviewer branch of `step` in `flow.ts` is a
  two-iteration loop over launch and observe; the second malformed result
  stops. `selectedSourceReview`, `normalizeReviewer` and the seven
  `reviewer-retry-*` reasons and records are deleted.
- Delayed exit: on a missing exit receipt the adapter returns `running` until
  `now - attempt.launchedAt` exceeds a module constant, then fails.
  `exitReceiptWindowMs` leaves the config and every validator.
- `flow.ts` throws `QueueBlocked` (or a shared typed error) so `queue.ts`
  stops matching `error.message` strings.
- Tests cover the three retries with fake attempts and one restart mid-retry
  that repeats the retry without a duplicate PR or merge.

## Out of scope

New retry categories. Changing the four-attempt ceiling.
