## Context and evidence

Repeated v1 review proved that unsigned rows cannot authenticate producer
epochs, current pointers cannot assert their own predecessor, recovery journals
cannot be unselected side channels, bootstrap genesis cannot name a nonexistent
self epoch, and bounded arrays/counters cannot represent lifetime history. The
approved architecture in the four contract documents is authoritative.

## Scope fence

In: platform-neutral schemas, total parsers, canonical bytes, compatibility,
framed digest domains including `DECIMAL_ASCII`, closed record/array snapshots,
thirteen runtime pointer families, external destination-owner/anchor contracts,
stable physical identity and versioned observations, sparse authority history,
commit runs/checkpoints/packets, release/gate/fence/cleanup, authorization,
reservation/descriptor/summary/accumulator, and pure transition validators.

Out: filesystem mutation, locks, private capabilities, process launch, broker
operations, release policy, and adapter/project vocabulary. This issue defines
contracts but never makes a candidate or receipt its own authority.

## Decisions already made

- The acyclic graph is `Dv=value`, `Dr=pointer-cas-proposal-receipt/v2(prior
  triple,Dv,closed producer)`, and `Dt=tip(Dv,Dr)` under exact framing.
- Proposal intents are `VALUE_PROPOSED|TOMBSTONE_PROPOSED`; classifications are
  `PENDING|SELECTED|LOST_CONFLICT|COMPACTED|UNKNOWN`; timestamps are create-once.
- Pointer instance and mutation identity bind kind, canonical path, identities,
  transaction, source, position, prior triple, successor `Dv`, and outcome.
- The registry has exactly thirteen kinds, including authority rotation, the
  generic `POINTER_MUTATION_RUN_CURRENT` META_LEAF, and one
  `AUTHORITY_DP`-scoped singleton `AUTHORITY_NODE_MATERIALIZATION_RUN`.
- Terminal removal retains a selected tombstone; no post-genesis bare absence
  is authority. v1 authority-path records are diagnostic-only and refused.
- `Dphys` is stable physical identity; helper/profile/custody/readback facts are
  versioned `Dobs` observations. `Ddest` frames raw `Dphys` alone. A FULL_REQUIRED
  destination owner and external anchor serialize E0/reinstall/teardown.
- E0 uses external ACTIVE/CONSUMED evidence and an immutable core that excludes
  proposal/`Dr`/tip/`Dt`; its bootstrap producer has no selected epoch. Runtime
  and external post-selection receipts complete the graph without a cycle.
- `state-mutation-authority-value/v3` binds `historyRootKind=EMPTY|NONEMPTY`
  plus selected FULL_REQUIRED node-inventory `Dnir`/count. E0 selects
  `authority-history-empty-root/v2` and empty inventory roots; E1 proves
  `EMPTY→NONEMPTY`, later rotations prove
  `NONEMPTY→NONEMPTY`, and producer membership against EMPTY refuses. Exact
  256-sibling update/membership proofs provide fixed per-call verification
  without lifetime caps or self-authenticating tables. Lifetime-stable `G`
  excludes rotating helper/profile/ABI/lock/state-component facts.
- Each rotation precomputes `Dplan`, materializes the exact derived node set,
  separates filesystem disposition from inventory membership action, and
  selects a batch/root/count. Bounded authenticated directory pages/terminal
  prove exact selected set equality without a lifetime cap or self-assertion.
- The singleton coordinator lifecycle is
  `IDLE|PREAUTHORIZED|STARTED|FINISHING|TERMINAL|REVOKED_BEFORE_START` with
  unbounded decimal ordinal and one active plan. STARTED is finish-only.
- Authority rotation alone splits a commit run: checkpoints 0–5 E(n), target
  CAS, checkpoints 6–8 E(n+1). Fresh E(n+1) reproduces `Drh`; after terminal
  checkpoint 8 it creates `Dhand` for coordinator FINISHING.
- Checkpoint cores exclude their selecting run-current graph; a downstream
  post-selection observation feeds the next core. `PROPOSED` is live-only;
  durable resolution is `SELECTED|LOST_CONFLICT|UNKNOWN_TERMINAL`.
- `pointer-evidence-packet/v3` separates `HISTORICAL_READ` from
  `MUTATION_COMMIT`; pure structural success never grants a live capability.
- Recovery authorization uses the exact acyclic core projection and ordered
  native-consume/select/post-receipt and revoke sequence.
- Attempts use reservation, descriptor, attachment, terminal summary, and a
  rolling accumulator with bounded verification and no lifetime cap.
- Closed arrays accept exact same-realm mutable/sealed/frozen arrays and reject
  reflective or structural extras exactly as `contract-decisions.md` states.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. Every public parse/serialize/migrate call is total and snapshots every
   envelope, record, and nested array before semantic reads. Executable mutants
   cover transparent/throwing proxies, symbols, accessors, non-enumerables,
   subclasses, exotic prototypes, holes, custom iterators, and frozen arrays.
2. Canonical serialization, `DECIMAL_ASCII`, and every digest domain have
   exact-byte goldens, cross-domain negatives, raw-digest framing tests, values
   far above safe integer range, and cross-OS vectors.
3. The registry census is exactly thirteen kinds and exact path constructors,
   schemas, roots, archives, source tokens, retention, and genesis rules.
   Unknown/case/encoding/cross-family paths and source-token collisions refuse.
4. Proposal/conflict tests prove same mutation+bytes idempotence, different
   bytes conflict, real-winner lost classification, malformed `UNKNOWN`, retry
   timestamp reuse, and no self-asserted predecessor.
5. Cleanup validates exactly ten states and twelve mutation edges, no write
   self-loops; fence validates only its one forward edge. Full ordered history,
   ordinal, predecessor, root, path, digest, and terminal-head mutants execute.
6. Auth core tests mutate every common and mode-union field and prove the gate
   binds selected CREATED `Dt/Dv/Dr` without a digest cycle. Consume, attach,
   revoke, native-receipt, and coordinated-substitution matrices fail closed.
7. Reservation/descriptor/summary/accumulator goldens prove UUID uniqueness-only,
   predecessor reuse, first/later rolling formulas, first in-progress absence,
   attachment-to-descriptor binding, and unbounded linked lifetime with a fixed
   per-call packet.
8. Retention tests prove full-required refusal, selected checkpoint/plan/delete/
   completion order, pending protection, and the exact `AUDIT_DEGRADED` allow/
   deny operation matrix.
9. Physical identity/observation, destination-owner, successor review-core/
   post-receipt, external anchor/use/consumption, E0 core/post, expiry, race,
   teardown, and exact reinstall matrices prove one owner per physical target.
10. Authority v3/history-v2 tests prove E0 `Dhe`+empty `Dnir`, first
    EMPTY→NONEMPTY and later NONEMPTY
    updates, nonempty `count-1` latest binding, exact 256-sibling absence/update/
    membership, empty-membership refusal, lifetime-stable `G`, fixed proof cost
    across long histories, stale roots, and FULL_REQUIRED lineage refusal.
11. Commit tests prove all nine stages, selected generic META_LEAF paths,
    acyclic core/value/proposal/tip/post graph, crash/restart runs, ambiguous
    post-CAS recovery, exact terminal states, and purpose/capability separation.
12. Node-inventory tests cover exact Dplan/batch/root, split disposition/action,
    singleton lifecycle, 10k rotations/pages, all missing/injected/stale/crash
    mutants, and the sole E(n)→E(n+1) checkpoint-6 handoff.
13. Compatibility declares superseded authority values, roots, append/core/run
    records, and twelve-slot packets diagnostic-only and refused at current
    authority paths. No deployment migration is claimed.
