## Context and evidence

Portable implementation requires contracts that do not encode operating-system
paths or consumer policy. See `docs/architecture/vision.md` and the classified
manifest produced by `ISS-001`.

## Scope fence

In: schemas for platform configuration, adapter declaration, installed release,
session lease, worker ownership, journal events, review receipts, and promotion
receipts; closed worker-role enum, resource ownership/reclamation, breaker
authority, import transactions; compatibility and canonical serialization
rules; and the provider-neutral module descriptor/input/result/action schemas
fixed in `docs/architecture/module-abi.md`; `supervisor-shim/v1`,
`active-release/v1`, `broker-active-client/v1`, and successor-admission records.
Also in: closed `repository-protection-receipt/v1` and
`bootstrap-verifier-anchor/v1` schemas/parsers predeclared for the `ISS-036`
producer and `ISS-038` consumers.

Out: filesystem locking, process launch, GitHub API calls, or consumer-specific
fields.

## Decisions already made

The encoding, time, numeric, identity, path, unknown-field, and exact bootstrap
version-transition matrix in `docs/architecture/contract-decisions.md` and
`docs/architecture/bootstrap-manifests.md` are authoritative.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. Each authority-bearing record has a discriminated version and total parser.
   Evidence: mutation tests for missing, extra, malformed, future, and legacy
   fields.
2. Canonical serialization produces the exact golden bytes defined by the
   contract decisions. Evidence: local golden tests here and cross-OS execution
   owned by `ISS-006`.
3. Compatibility rules distinguish readable, migratable, and refused versions.
   Evidence: table-driven tests with a negative control per transition.
4. No schema contains an absolute host path or project-policy vocabulary.
   Evidence: structural guard over schemas and fixtures.
5. Dispatch plans require exactly one valid `implementation`, `review`, or
   `observer` role; missing, malformed, or unknown roles fail parsing before
   ownership. Evidence: omission and enum mutation tests proving no launch.
6. Generic breaker, owned-resource, export, import-plan, and import-receipt
   schemas bind exact identities and refuse partial/unknown authority. Evidence:
   total parser and transaction-identity mutation fixtures.
7. `module-descriptor/v1`, `module-plan-input/v1`, `module-plan-result/v1`,
   `module-action-plan/v1`, and `module-no-action/v1` are closed, canonical,
   provider-neutral schemas with exhaustive compatibility rules. Evidence:
   parser/golden fixtures and forbidden host/provider/policy field mutants.
8. Supervisor-shim, active-release, broker-active-client, pending-successor, and
   activation records bind installation/release/executable/operation-manifest/
   generation digests with exact compatibility and transition fields. Evidence:
   total parsers and stale/moved/mixed-generation mutation fixtures.
   `activation-recovery-fence-root/v1` is immutable at its transaction-derived
   root path; create-once `activation-recovery-fence-head/v1` records form an
   ordinal/previous-digest history, and canonical
   `activation-recovery-fence-current/v1` at
   `<state-root>/installation/activation-recovery-fence.json` points to exactly
   one head. Together they bind the
   promotion/recovery transaction, predecessor/successor and operation-manifest
   digests, pending broker admission, old/expected active-record digests and
   adjacent generations, lifecycle, and times; malformed/extra/absent-at-required
   fields fail closed. `activation-recovery-launch-current/v1` has the sole
   canonical current-pointer path
   `<state-root>/installation/activation-recovery-launch.json`; create-once
   `activation-recovery-launch/v1` transition records live under the bound
   transaction/source-token/generation/ordinal path and form a previous-digest
   chain. The exhaustive path-token mapping is `recovery-fence/v1` →
   `recovery-fence-v1` and `cleanup-gate-pre-fence/v1` →
   `cleanup-gate-pre-fence-v1`; alternate encodings and source/token mismatch
   refuse.
   Together they bind immutable gate/fence roots, observed head ordinals/digests,
   fence/transaction/cycle, predecessor/active-record/argv digests, generation,
   attempt, process tree, times, `LIFECYCLE`/`AUTHORITY_REBIND` transition kind, and the closed
   `READY`/`LIVE`/`TERMINAL_RETRYABLE`/`TERMINAL_HANDOFF`/
   `TERMINAL_ABORTED`/`TERMINAL_COMPLETE`/`UNKNOWN` lifecycle;
   `TERMINAL_HANDOFF` is pre-fence only, while `TERMINAL_ABORTED` permits either
   source only under gate `ABORTING` with active record N.
   `READY`/`LIVE` process fields are lifecycle-conditional;
   `activation-recovery-launch-archive/v1` binds the full transition chain and
   canonical-pointer cleanup. `recovery-authorization/v1` binds the non-secret
   transaction/capability/current-launch authority and closed
   `CREATED_UNCONSUMED`/`CONSUMED_BOUND`/`REVOKED`/`UNKNOWN` lifecycle at its
   canonical transaction path. Evidence: total parsers plus create-once,
   child-adoption/absence/ambiguity, immutable three-plus-attempt chains,
   adjacent-generation pointer CAS, stale-attempt, concurrent-live, authority
   consume/restart/revoke, archive/cleanup, second-transaction, and unknown-state
   mutation fixtures. Golden fixtures require canonical `null` prior pointer/
   state digests and CAS-from-absence only for generation-zero ordinal-zero;
   generation-zero `LIVE`, later-generation ordinal-zero `READY`, and every
   other later record require lowercase SHA-256 digests and reject null/zero/
   empty; generation-zero ordinal-zero rejects a digest.
   `AUTHORITY_REBIND` retains source/lifecycle/attempt/process/roots and may
   advance only one gate or fence head whose immutable record names the prior
   digest; while the launch is one head behind, every further authority-head
   write is blocked. Nonadjacent/skipped/changed-root variants refuse. Recovery-launch current/state/archive schemas discriminate
   `recovery-fence/v1` from `cleanup-gate-pre-fence/v1`; the latter requires gate
   root/current-head plus expected fence root but canonical-null fence-authority
   fields, and must terminalize/archive/
   remove before a fresh fence-backed generation-zero chain. Evidence includes
   simultaneous, missing, mismatched, cross-source transition, handoff crash,
   and record-path collision mutants.
   `activation-cleanup-gate-root/v1` is immutable at its transaction-derived
   root path; create-once `activation-cleanup-gate-head/v1` records form an
   ordinal/previous-digest history, and canonical
   `activation-cleanup-gate-current/v1` at the sole installation path points to
   exactly one head. Root/head together bind mode/transaction/candidate/release, recovery-authorization identity,
   created/expected-consumed authorization digests, expected active record/
   generation, deterministic archive path, prior cleanup-head digest, optional
   fence plus `NOT_PUBLISHED`/`PUBLISHING`/`PUBLISHED`/`CLEARED` discriminator,
   `PENDING`/`ACTIVATING`/`ABORTING`/`COMPLETE` lifecycle, abort/terminal revocation
   receipt, and current-pointer CAS removal. `activation-cleanup-gate-archive/v1` has the exact
   transaction-derived path and binds the transition chain, proofs, active-
   record digest, and prior head; canonical `activation-cleanup-head/v1` points
   to the latest verified archive. `active-release/v1` binds its activation
   cleanup transaction/archive path so the shim never scans.
   Evidence: N0, null-launch successor, attached successor, abort, crashes on
   both sides of `PUBLISHING`/fence-create/`PUBLISHED`, abort-vs-`ACTIVATING`,
   pre-activation fence-backed `TERMINAL_ABORTED`, and fence clear,
   missing/deleted/substituted/ambiguous gate/archive/head, aborted
   later transaction retaining active release, and second-activation fixtures.
   Gate and fence ordinal-zero heads encode canonical-null predecessor and their
   current pointers use CAS-from-absence. The shared initialization reducer
   recognizes only all-absent, exact-root-only, exact-root-plus-initial-head, and
   fully current prefixes and resumes one missing suffix. Head-without-root,
   current-without-both, non-null ordinal-zero predecessor, wrong/multiple/extra
   history, and mixed-transaction fixtures reduce `UNKNOWN`.
9. `repository-protection-receipt/v1` binds repository/ruleset numeric identity
   and semantics, protected path-policy/actor/review behavior, ISS-036 synthetic
   probe workflow identity/triggers/permissions, complete paginated API provenance, protected-environment identity plus pre-run
   `VERIFIER_ANCHOR_SHA256` variable name/value/API ETag/update time, producer run/attempt
   and intended artifact name, verifier-anchor digest, issued/expiry, and
   disposition. Enclosing artifact ID/archive digest are deliberately absent
   because they are assigned after serialization. `bootstrap-verifier-anchor/v1` binds operator-confirmed official
   verifier assets/checksums, repository/issuer/signer identity, creation time,
   and forbids custom-root/candidate-supplied trust. Evidence: canonical golden
   parsers plus missing/extra, stale, moved artifact/ruleset/workflow, combined
   root/verifier/bundle, and candidate-created-anchor mutants.
