## Context and evidence

Repeated review proved that unsigned rows cannot authenticate producer epochs,
current pointers cannot assert their own predecessor, recovery journals cannot
be unselected side channels, and bootstrap genesis cannot name a nonexistent
self epoch. The proportionality review (pressure round 34) then proved the
opposite failure: sparse authority trees, a node-inventory census, a
materialization coordinator, an epoch-split commit run, and arbitrary-precision
ordinals defended threats the single-writer topology already excludes, and the
never-deployed schema generations they superseded had accumulated into a frozen
diagnostic namespace with no consumer. The approved architecture in the four
contract documents, as amended by the `Proportionality and schema lifecycle`
decisions, is authoritative.

## Scope fence

In: platform-neutral schemas, total parsers, canonical bytes, compatibility,
framed digest domains over safe-integer ordinals, closed record/array
snapshots, eleven runtime pointer families, the hash-chained linear
`authority-history/v1` log with reviewed-operation/successor-core/genesis-
selection contracts, external destination-owner/anchor contracts, stable
physical identity and versioned observations, the closed ordinary/rotation
single-epoch commit evidence and packet unions, release/gate/fence/cleanup, authorization,
reservation/descriptor/attempt-log records, the closed
`dispatch-brief/v1` schema, the engine-vocabulary lint, and pure transition
validators.

Out: filesystem mutation, locks, private capabilities, process launch, broker
operations, release policy, and adapter/project vocabulary. This issue defines
contracts but never makes a candidate or receipt its own authority.
