## Context and evidence

Repeated review proved that unsigned rows cannot authenticate producer epochs,
current pointers cannot assert their own predecessor, recovery journals cannot
be unselected side channels, and bootstrap genesis cannot name a nonexistent
self epoch. The proportionality review (pressure round 34) then proved the
opposite failure: sparse authority trees, a node-inventory census, a
materialization coordinator, an epoch-split commit run, and arbitrary-precision
ordinals defended threats the single-writer topology already excludes, and the
never-deployed schema generations they superseded had accumulated into a frozen
diagnostic namespace with no consumer. The approved architecture in the four
contract documents, as amended by the `Proportionality and schema lifecycle`
decisions, is authoritative.

## Scope fence

In: platform-neutral schemas, total parsers, canonical bytes, compatibility,

## Decisions already made

- The acyclic graph is `Dv=value`, `Dr=pointer-cas-proposal-receipt/v1(prior
  triple,Dv,closed producer)`, and `Dt=tip(Dv,Dr)` under exact framing.
- Proposal intents are `VALUE_PROPOSED|TOMBSTONE_PROPOSED`; classifications are
  `PENDING|SELECTED|LOST_CONFLICT|COMPACTED|UNKNOWN`; timestamps are create-once.
- Pointer instance and mutation identity bind kind, canonical path, identities,
  transaction, source, position, prior triple, successor `Dv`, and outcome.
- The registry has exactly twelve kinds, including authority rotation and the
  generic `POINTER_MUTATION_RUN_CURRENT` META_LEAF. No materialization
  coordinator kind exists.
- Terminal removal retains a selected tombstone; no post-genesis bare absence
  is authority.
- `Dphys` is stable physical identity; helper/profile/custody/readback facts are
  versioned `Dobs` observations. `Ddest` frames raw `Dphys` alone. A FULL_REQUIRED
  destination owner and external anchor serialize E0/reinstall/teardown.
- E0 uses external ACTIVE/CONSUMED evidence and an immutable core that excludes
  proposal/`Dr`/tip/`Dt`; its bootstrap producer has no selected epoch. Runtime
  and external post-selection receipts complete the graph without a cycle.
- `state-mutation-authority-value/v1` binds the selected `authority-history/v1`
  head ordinal and record digest. The closed record union is `GENESIS`, with
  ordinal zero/genesis predecessor/external bootstrap facts/no retiring epoch,
  or `ROTATION`, with ordinal greater than zero/exact prior record digest/
  retiring `Dp/Dt/Dv/Dr`/deterministic independently reviewed rotation
  identity. Both bind successor core facts and exclude successor value,
  proposal, tip, and selected head. Verification walks the complete chain from
  genesis; missing, forked, reordered, or truncated chains refuse. Lifetime-
  stable `G` excludes rotating helper/profile/ABI/lock/state-component facts.
- Ordinals and counts are canonical decimal strings bounded by the safe-integer
  range. Grammar, length, and lexicographic comparison refuse above
  `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` before conversion. No arbitrary-precision type exists.
- Every commit run is single-epoch. Rotation is an ordinary single-epoch run
  under the old capability that appends the chain record and then performs the
  authority CAS as its final action; its run-current journal legitimately
  rests at CAS-armed. Prior selected plus exact matching head-plus-one is
  resumable under the old epoch; successor selected plus its exact chain record
  and the old CAS-armed checkpoint derives `SELECTED`; otherwise rotation is
  `UNKNOWN`. No post-CAS new-epoch write or rotation receipt exists.
- Checkpoint cores exclude their selecting run-current graph; a downstream
  post-selection observation feeds the next core. `PROPOSED` is live-only;
  durable resolution is `SELECTED|LOST_CONFLICT|UNKNOWN_TERMINAL`.
- `pointer-evidence-packet/v1` separates `HISTORICAL_READ` from
  `MUTATION_COMMIT`. Mutation outcome cross-binds top authority and identities
  to the exact registry slot: selected target, real winner, or empty for
  `SELECTED`, `LOST_CONFLICT`, or `UNKNOWN`. Unknown evidence is a fixed-size
  closed `UNREADABLE|MALFORMED|IMPOSSIBLE` union with closed enums, digest, and
  safe length—never arbitrary JSON, native text, paths, or arrays. Pure
  structural success never grants a live capability.
- Recovery authorization uses the exact acyclic core projection and ordered
  native-consume/select/post-receipt and revoke sequence.
- Attempts use a CAS-selected reservation that is durable before process
  creation and carries the descriptor inputs, a LIVE descriptor binding that
  reservation, attachment, and an append-only `attempt-log/v1` whose records
  chain by predecessor digest and safe-integer ordinal; the TERMINAL record
  carries the folded terminal facts, and each operation verifies the full
  log, which stays small because attempts are rare.
- `dispatch-brief/v1` is the closed structure a planning module emits for a
  worker; every human-readable section derives from typed fields, and the
  host-rendered bytes are digest-bound in the dispatch plan.
- Closed arrays accept exact same-realm mutable/sealed/frozen arrays and reject
  reflective or structural extras exactly as `contract-decisions.md` states.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. Every public parse/serialize/migrate call is total and snapshots every
   envelope, record, and nested array before semantic reads. Executable mutants
   cover transparent/throwing proxies, symbols, accessors, non-enumerables,
   subclasses, exotic prototypes, holes, custom iterators, and frozen arrays.
2. Canonical serialization and every digest domain have exact-byte goldens,
   cross-domain negatives, raw-digest framing tests, and cross-OS vectors.
   Ordinal goldens cover zero, one, `2^53 - 1`, and refusal above the bound
   before numeric conversion.
3. The registry census is exactly twelve kinds and exact path constructors,
   schemas, roots, archives, source tokens, retention, and genesis rules.
   Unknown/case/encoding/cross-family paths and source-token collisions refuse.
4. Proposal/conflict tests prove same mutation+bytes idempotence, different
   bytes conflict, real-winner lost classification, malformed `UNKNOWN`, retry
   timestamp reuse, and no self-asserted predecessor.
5. Cleanup validates exactly ten states and twelve mutation edges, no write
   self-loops; fence validates only its one forward edge. Full ordered history,
   ordinal, predecessor, root, path, digest, and terminal-head mutants execute.
6. Auth core tests mutate every common and mode-union field and prove the gate
   binds selected CREATED `Dt/Dv/Dr` without a digest cycle. Consume, attach,
   revoke, and native-receipt matrices fail closed with one mutant per bound
   field plus one coordinated all-field mutant per matrix.
7. Reservation/descriptor/attempt-log goldens prove UUID uniqueness-only,
   predecessor reuse, chained ordinal continuity, first in-progress absence,
   attachment-to-descriptor binding, and the TERMINAL record carrying the
   folded terminal facts with no separate summary document.
8. Retention tests prove full-required refusal, selected checkpoint/plan/delete/
   completion order, pending protection, and the exact `AUDIT_DEGRADED` allow/
   deny operation matrix.
9. Physical identity/observation, destination-owner, successor review-core/
   post-receipt, external anchor/use/consumption, E0 core/post, expiry, race,
   teardown, and exact reinstall matrices prove one owner per physical target.
10. Authority-history tests prove the closed GENESIS/ROTATION record union,
    including GENESIS external bootstrap facts with no retiring epoch and
    ROTATION's prior digest, retiring `Dp/Dt/Dv/Dr`, reviewed identity, and
    successor core facts; both reject successor value/proposal/tip/head. They prove append verification,
    and the full-chain walk over constructed canonical ordinal paths against
    the selected head with no directory enumeration: fork/gap/reorder/
    truncation and head-ordinal/digest mismatch refuse; constructed-path
    absence probes prove head-plus-one is absent or matches the armed
    rotation intent and head-plus-two is absent. Scale evidence references
    the ISS-006-published 1,000-record, five-seconds-per-OS golden and budget;
    checkpointing stays parked unless that measured gate fails.
11. Commit tests prove all stages single-epoch, selected generic META_LEAF
    paths, acyclic core/value/proposal/tip/post graph, crash/restart runs,
    ambiguous post-CAS recovery, exact terminal states, and purpose/
    capability separation. Rotation appends the chain record, performs the
    authority CAS as its final action, and legitimately rests at CAS-armed.
    Tests derive resumable-old, selected-successor, and UNKNOWN without any
    post-CAS new-epoch write. Packet tests cross-bind top authority, all identity
    fields, and selected/winner/empty registry slots, and reject unknown union
    extras, open enums, unsafe lengths, native text, paths, JSON, and arrays.
    A crash between append and CAS resumes only via the same transaction under
    the old capability re-driving the same CAS.
12. `dispatch-brief/v1` tests prove closed parsing, no free-form prose field,
    deterministic rendering goldens, and digest binding between emitted
    structure and rendered bytes.
13. Superseded pre-deployment schema generations are deleted from the package
    and its tests; no diagnostic or archive namespace exports. The shipped
    census is `v1` for every family, compatibility refuses unknown and future
    versions, and the API-surface test proves no superseded symbol is
    reachable. No deployment migration is claimed.
14. A generated vocabulary lint proves no engine contract field name or closed
    enum value uses adapter or consumer vocabulary from the `ISS-001` denylist;
    one seeded-term mutant fails the contracts build.
