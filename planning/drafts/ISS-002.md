## Context and evidence

Repeated review proved that unsigned rows cannot authenticate producer epochs,
current pointers cannot assert their own predecessor, recovery journals cannot
be unselected side channels, and bootstrap genesis cannot name a nonexistent
self epoch. The proportionality review (pressure round 34) then proved the
opposite failure: sparse authority trees, a node-inventory census, a
materialization coordinator, an epoch-split commit run, and arbitrary-precision
ordinals defended threats the single-writer topology already excludes, and the
never-deployed schema generations they superseded had accumulated into a frozen
diagnostic namespace with no consumer. The approved architecture in the four
contract documents, as amended by the `Proportionality and schema lifecycle`
decisions, is authoritative.

## Scope fence

In: platform-neutral schemas, total parsers, canonical bytes, compatibility,
framed digest domains over safe-integer ordinals, closed record/array
snapshots, eleven runtime pointer families, the hash-chained linear
`authority-history/v1` log with reviewed-operation/successor-core/genesis-
selection contracts, external destination-owner/anchor contracts, stable
physical identity and versioned observations, the closed ordinary/rotation
single-epoch commit evidence and packet unions, release/gate/fence/cleanup, authorization,
reservation/descriptor/attempt-log records, the closed
`dispatch-brief/v1` schema, the engine-vocabulary lint, and pure transition
validators.

Out: filesystem mutation, locks, private capabilities, process launch, broker
operations, release policy, and adapter/project vocabulary. This issue defines
contracts but never makes a candidate or receipt its own authority.

## Decisions already made

- The acyclic graph is `Dv=value`, `Dr=pointer-cas-proposal-receipt/v1(prior
triple,Dv,closed producer)`, and `Dt=tip(Dv,Dr)` under exact framing.
- Proposal intents are `VALUE_PROPOSED|TOMBSTONE_PROPOSED`; classifications are
  `PENDING|SELECTED|LOST_CONFLICT|UNKNOWN`; timestamps are create-once.
- Pointer instance and mutation identity bind kind, canonical path, identities,
  transaction, source, position, prior triple, successor `Dv`, and outcome.
- The registry has exactly eleven kinds, including authority rotation and the
  generic `POINTER_MUTATION_RUN_CURRENT` META_LEAF. No materialization
  coordinator or retention/compaction kind exists.
- Terminal removal retains a selected tombstone; no post-genesis bare absence
  is authority.
- `Dphys` is stable physical identity; helper/profile/custody/readback facts are
  versioned `Dobs` observations. `Ddest` frames raw `Dphys` alone. A FULL_REQUIRED
  destination owner and external anchor serialize E0/reinstall/teardown.
- E0 uses external ACTIVE/CONSUMED evidence and an immutable core that excludes
  proposal/`Dr`/tip/`Dt`; its bootstrap producer has no selected epoch. Runtime
  and external post-selection receipts complete the graph without a cycle.
- `state-mutation-authority-value/v1` binds the selected `authority-history/v1`
  head ordinal and record digest. Both history arms bind the exact shared
  successor core `Dsc`: `G`, authority `Dp`, successor ordinal, reviewed release
  manifest/bytes/subject/review, derived BOOTSTRAP_INSTALL/STABLE_PROMOTION
  operation `Dop`, helper/profile/ABI/lock/state-component, and custody facts.
  GENESIS binds ordinal zero, genesis predecessor, and `Dgb` over selected
  owner/anchor ACTIVE, use intent, and bootstrap identity/transaction/grant;
  downstream selection evidence binds `Dbg`, E0 selection/readbacks/`Dgp`, and
  owner/anchor CONSUMED without entering the record. ROTATION binds exact prior
  head, retiring `Dp/Dt/Dv/Dr`, and `Drot`, recomputed from rotation
  transaction, retiring authority/head, successor ordinal, `Dop`, and `Dsc`.
  Both exclude successor value/proposal/tip/head and downstream artifacts.
- The normative simplified-authority schema ledger in
  `docs/architecture/supervisor-contract.md` fixes every JSON member name,
  canonical member order, scalar/nullability/enum rule, persistence status,
  digest domain, ordered framed part, and excluded field for `Dop`, `Dsc`,
  `Dgb`, `Dgse`, `Drot`, both history arms, selected authority value, and both
  `Dcommit` arms. Symbolic labels are not implementer-selected field names.
  History records serialize as `authority-history-record/v1`; only their digest
  uses domain `authority-history/v1`.
- Ordinals and counts are canonical decimal strings bounded by the safe-integer
  range. Grammar, length, and lexicographic comparison refuse above
  `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` before conversion. No arbitrary-precision type exists.
- Every commit run is single-epoch. `pointer-mutation-commit-evidence/v1` is a
  closed `ORDINARY|AUTHORITY_ROTATION` union. ORDINARY retains checkpoints 0–8
  and `SELECTED|LOST_CONFLICT|UNKNOWN_TERMINAL` resolution. Rotation is a special single-epoch arm
  under the old capability that appends the chain record and then performs the
  authority CAS as its final action; its run-current journal legitimately
  rests at CAS-armed. Prior selected plus exact matching head-plus-one is
  resumable under the old epoch; successor selected plus its exact chain record
  and the old CAS-armed checkpoint derives `SELECTED`; otherwise rotation is
  `UNKNOWN`. It binds selected old checkpoint 5, expected successor/history,
  and old/successor/empty authority slot; checkpoints 6–8, ordinary resolution,
  later selector artifacts, post-CAS new-epoch writes, and rotation receipts
  refuse.
- Checkpoint cores exclude their selecting run-current graph; a downstream
  post-selection observation feeds the next core. `PROPOSED` is live-only;
  ordinary durable resolution is `SELECTED|LOST_CONFLICT|UNKNOWN_TERMINAL`;
  rotation has only the derived `Dcommit` arm above.
- `pointer-evidence-packet/v1` separates `HISTORICAL_READ` from
  `MUTATION_COMMIT`. Mutation outcome cross-binds top authority and identities
  to the exact registry slot: selected target, real winner, or empty for
  `SELECTED`, `LOST_CONFLICT`, or `UNKNOWN`. Unknown evidence is a fixed-size
  closed `UNREADABLE|MALFORMED|IMPOSSIBLE` union with closed enums, digest, and
  safe length—never arbitrary JSON, native text, paths, or arrays. Pure
  structural success never grants a live capability.
- Recovery authorization uses the exact acyclic core projection and ordered
  native-consume/select/post-receipt and revoke sequence.
- Attempts use a CAS-selected reservation that is durable before process
  creation and carries the descriptor inputs, a LIVE descriptor binding that
  reservation, attachment, and an append-only `attempt-log/v1` whose records
  chain by predecessor digest and safe-integer ordinal; the TERMINAL record
  carries the folded terminal facts, and each operation verifies the full
  log, which stays small because attempts are rare.
- `dispatch-brief/v1` is the closed structure a planning module emits for a
  worker; every human-readable section derives from typed fields, and the
  host-rendered bytes are digest-bound in the dispatch plan.
  The normative ledger in `contract-decisions.md` fixes its closed action,
  directive, and opaque-resource records; nine directive kinds cover scope,
  decisions, constraints, non-goals, acceptance, verification, operator
  actions, and review attack surface without a free-form prose field.
- Closed arrays accept exact same-realm mutable/sealed/frozen arrays and reject
  reflective or structural extras exactly as `contract-decisions.md` states.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. Every public parse/serialize/validate call is total and snapshots every
   envelope, record, and nested array before semantic reads. Executable mutants
   cover transparent/throwing proxies, symbols, accessors, non-enumerables,
   subclasses, exotic prototypes, holes, custom iterators, and frozen arrays.
2. Canonical serialization and every digest domain have exact-byte goldens,
   cross-domain negatives, raw-digest framing tests, and cross-OS vectors.
   Ordinal goldens cover zero, one, `2^53 - 1`, and refusal above the bound
   before numeric conversion.
3. The registry census is exactly eleven kinds and exact path constructors,
   schemas, roots, archives, source tokens, and genesis rules.
   Unknown/case/encoding/cross-family paths and source-token collisions refuse.
4. Proposal/conflict tests prove same mutation+bytes idempotence, different
   bytes conflict, real-winner lost classification, malformed `UNKNOWN`, retry
   timestamp reuse, and no self-asserted predecessor.
5. Cleanup validates exactly ten states and twelve mutation edges, no write
   self-loops; fence validates only its one forward edge. Full ordered history,
   ordinal, predecessor, root, path, digest, and terminal-head mutants execute.
6. Auth core tests mutate every common and mode-union field and prove the gate
   binds selected CREATED `Dt/Dv/Dr` without a digest cycle. Consume, attach,
   revoke, and native-receipt matrices fail closed with one mutant per bound
   field plus one coordinated all-field mutant per matrix.
7. Reservation/descriptor/attempt-log goldens prove UUID uniqueness-only,
   predecessor reuse, chained ordinal continuity, first in-progress absence,
   attachment-to-descriptor binding, and the TERMINAL record carrying the
   folded terminal facts with no separate summary document.
8. Record-class tests prove every class is FULL_REQUIRED and no compaction
   path exists; loss of any required record is `UNKNOWN` and blocks mutation,
   and the deleted retention kind, classification, and audit mode are
   unreachable.
9. Physical identity/observation, destination-owner, successor review-core/
   post-receipt, external anchor/use/consumption, E0 core/post, expiry, race,
   teardown, and exact reinstall matrices prove one owner per physical target.
10. Authority-history tests prove the exact closed formulas for `Dop`, `Dsc`,
    `Dgb`, branch-specific `Dh`, derived `Drot`, and downstream genesis-
    selection evidence. GENESIS covers owner/anchor ACTIVE, use intent,
    bootstrap identity/transaction/grant, E0 core/selection/readbacks/`Dgp`,
    owner/anchor CONSUMED, consumption, and no retiring epoch without a cycle.
    ROTATION covers exact prior selected head, retiring `Dp/Dt/Dv/Dr`, reviewed
    operation/release/subject, and successor helper/profile/ABI/custody. Both
    reject successor value/proposal/tip/head and downstream-artifact insertion. They prove append verification,
    and the full-chain walk over constructed canonical ordinal paths against
    the selected head with no directory enumeration: fork/gap/reorder/
    truncation and head-ordinal/digest mismatch refuse. A head-plus-one record
    is accepted only when its ordinal is head plus one, its predecessor digest
    equals the head record digest, and its rotation identity and successor
    authority facts equal the selected armed rotation intent, the CAS-armed
    run-current journal plus its create-once intent record; when no armed
    intent is selected, any head-plus-one file refuses, and head-plus-two is
    absent. Scale evidence references the ISS-006-published 1,000-record,
    five-seconds-per-OS golden and budget; checkpointing stays parked unless
    that measured gate fails.
    Schema-census mutants remove, add, rename, null, reorder, or cross-type every
    literal ledger member and substitute `authority-history/v1` as a record
    `schemaVersion`; every mutant refuses before digest comparison.
11. Commit tests prove the closed ORDINARY/ROTATION evidence union. ORDINARY
    retains all nine stages, selected generic META_LEAF paths, acyclic graph,
    and selected/lost/unknown resolution. Rotation binds old E(n) intent and
    selected checkpoint 5, expected successor `Dv`/target mutation/head/
    record/`Drot`/`Dsc`, then exact RESUMABLE old slot, SELECTED successor slot,
    or UNKNOWN empty slot. Tests reject checkpoints 6–8, ordinary resolution,
    later selector artifacts, and every post-CAS new-epoch write. Packet tests
    cross-bind `Dcommit`, top authority, all identity fields, and exact slots,
    and reject unknown union extras, open enums, unsafe lengths, native text,
    paths, JSON, and arrays.
    They also execute the exact outcome-to-slot matrix: ordinary selected and
    lost require their selected target/real winner slot, ordinary unknown is
    empty, and rotation resumable/selected/unknown require old/successor/empty.
    Empty-at-positive, wrong-kind, stale, and nonempty-at-unknown slots refuse.
    A packet-census mutant proves exactly eleven total registry-ordered slots,
    exactly ten non-target slots, one target occurrence, and no omitted kind.
    A crash between append and CAS resumes only via the same transaction under
    the old capability re-driving the same CAS.
12. `dispatch-brief/v1` tests prove its closed structural parser and that no
    free-form prose field exists. Tests cover the exact three nested schemas,
    all nine required directive kinds, bounded identifier/schema grammars,
    paired reference nullability, subject cross-binding, duplicate refusal,
    opaque resource identity/access, and proxy/symbol/accessor/hole mutants.
    `ISS-011` emits the structure, `ISS-021` owns the versioned host template
    and rendering goldens, and `ISS-008` owns the rendered-byte digest binding.
13. Superseded pre-deployment schema generations are deleted from the package
    and its tests; no diagnostic or archive namespace exports. The shipped
    census is `v1` for every family, compatibility refuses unknown and future
    versions, and the API-surface test proves no superseded symbol is
    reachable. No deployment migration is claimed.
14. A generated vocabulary lint proves no engine contract field name or closed
    enum value uses adapter or consumer vocabulary from the `ISS-001` denylist;
    one seeded-term mutant fails the contracts build.
