## Scope and observed failure

Align the accepted repository delivery result, the persisted queue item
completion and its closed restart reader. The ISS-079/#341 queue completed four
controller gates, actual hosted Windows/macOS/Linux execution, merge and native
cleanup, then emitted a production-shaped completion containing `run` and
`checks`. Its completed restart rejected those same fields as
`malformed-completed-item`. The original receipts and external failure evidence
remain immutable.

ISS-086 is registered as #350 under EPIC-KERNEL/M2 and is blocked by
ISS-078/#338. Pilot `iss086-queue-completion-a1-r2-20260909` starts semantic
implementation attempt 1/4 at reviewed source base
`8f1ebf6c0c952307d2410393e74e6fcb4648fb44`, with fresh author 1 and reviewer 2
reserved inside the initial eight native admissions. Stable executor
`c0247761ccb7514a15a1fbe7cbbf2b35a02434c8` remains the only mutation authority.
ISS-079's four native participants, two failed outcomes and every earlier
history remain externally preserved and are not reset or imported as new facts.

## G0 and smallest complete shape

G0: PASS. One explicit completed-delivery result, one closed queue completion
validator shared by ordinary restart and a read-only completed-state reader,
and focused production-composition/compatibility probes meet every criterion.
New writes retain the delivery run and the ordered passing hosted-check
evidence. Those fields are validated as diagnostic evidence and never grant
setup, publication, merge, cleanup or executor authority.

Not built: receipt rewrite or deletion; field-stripping shim; permissive parser;
general migration framework; retry engine; executor rebinding; live mutable
historical replay; new queue or publisher; automatic release proof; candidate
self-certification; promotion path; test-location repair #348; public package,
compiler, environment, workflow or native-permission change.
