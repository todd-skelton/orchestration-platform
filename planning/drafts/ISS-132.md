## Why

The M2 run `m2-payout-fees-replanned-20260913` authored, independently reviewed,
and passed both committed-candidate gates for chase-sets#7821, then published
PR #7973 at `b3bd4bedf14be41eda8e836c891701ebe26704dc`.
The loop immediately stopped with `missing-or-duplicate-check:PR Required`:
https://github.com/chase-sets/chase-sets/issues/7821#issuecomment-5654011363.

Platform PR run 34763956714 was still running Change Scope, then its static,
typecheck, unit, and DB jobs. The final aggregate `PR Required` job had not
been created yet. Missing final-check evidence during an observed live
workflow is a wait, not a terminal missing-check failure.

## Done when

- When a required check is absent while the candidate's hosted workflow is
  verifiably queued or running, the loop returns `observing-hosted-checks`
  and later proceeds once the real required check appears and passes.
- The observation is bound to the current publication and candidate head;
  another head's workflow cannot authorize waiting or success.
- No synthetic passing check, omitted required check, duplicate required
  check, failed/cancelled/skipped check, or head drift can authorize a merge.
  A required check still missing after terminal workflow observation remains
  a typed stop; unknown workflow state is not success.
- Tests reproduce the actual intermediate-jobs / absent-aggregate sequence,
  its transition to the real passing aggregate, and terminal-missing,
  duplicate, failure, and wrong-head negative cases.
- The existing M2 publication and receipts can be resumed after this host
  fix without reauthoring or rewriting failed stop records.

## Out of scope

Changing Chase Sets CI, weakening PR Required, bypassing the merge queue,
adding a second CI runner, changing the product patch, or broadening attempts.
