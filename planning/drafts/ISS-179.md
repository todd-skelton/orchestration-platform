## Decision

Constraint: keep ISS-161 parking, ISS-160's delivery-seed transition, the attempt
ceiling, spent allowances and every retained record unchanged. Simplest
alternative: have the park path write `phase: "failed"` at stop time, rejected
because `retainedSourceFailure` and resume replay depend on the retained
`source` phase and that would change ISS-161's steady state. Chosen: before
scanning failed attempts, composition observes a retained `source`-phase
record whose pinned config and matching author terminal show a failed author at
the pinned base, and advances it to a failed receipt once (`head` = base,
`reviewId` `""` as the existing no-review convention at queue.ts line 1762,
findings empty, history and `authorFailures` retained), exactly as ISS-160 does
for the delivery seed. Two consumers then need to read that receipt honestly:
composition must not emit the "Start from rejected candidate" prescription
(line 923) when the receipt carries no findings, and ISS-166's
`priorFailedAttempt` (line 2194), which today finds a predecessor only through
a reviewer participant, must also accept the latest failed author participant
of a different item for the same issue so attempt 2 receives the predecessor
records. Accepted downside: attempt 1 counts as a consumed attempt without a
review, which is already the ISS-161 contract.
Revisit when a recorded run shows a source-phase record that should not advance
(an in-flight author, a non-FAIL terminal, or a mismatched pin); those cases
must keep returning to the existing break.
