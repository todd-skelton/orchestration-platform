## Decision

A passing verification-only candidate must integrate later compatible main before unpublished delivery without renewing implementation authority.
The simplest alternative is to stop for another host decision on every main movement, but the recorded PASS and 52 ms replay show that normal moving-main delivery cannot progress.
Reuse the existing native refresh lifecycle for this narrow post-PASS transition, including its retained-state entrypoints and reviewer accounting.
Accept another charged integration DELTA and fresh exact-head gates within existing native bounds while preserving all other terminal stops.
The choice is wrong if a failed or unrelated continuation can re-enter, an old PASS authorizes a new head, or saved publication is rewritten.
