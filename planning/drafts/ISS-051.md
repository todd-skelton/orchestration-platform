## Registration context and outcome

Recovery observation and adapter-owned reclaim have literal representations but
no consumer with a complete attempt log, selected terminal state, supervisor
checkpoint, physical mapping, and ownership evidence. Register implementation
after `ISS-030`, which produces those inputs. This issue is blocked and thin
until the supervisor has exercised them.

## Scope, non-goals, and acceptance evidence

In: elaborate the existing recovery callback and reclaim-owner observation,
bind complete breaker/attempt/resource preimages, implement adapter-defined
recovery predicates and owned-resource deletion/read-back, and run two fixture
policies. Out: clearing a hold from `NO_TRIP`, reclaiming DISPATCH-owned or
unknown resources, generic filesystem authority, release cleanup, credentials,
and consumer policy.

Acceptance evidence must prove recovery cannot clear a hold on stale or partial
facts; reclaim requires exact current ADAPTER ownership and physical mapping;
crash/retry selects one result; and absence without retained deletion/read-back
never completes. Exact commands are due before the edge becomes runnable.

## Verification plan

Registration is checked by the repository planning checker. Before `ISS-030` clears the edge,
a reviewed elaboration must name exact recovery, reclaim, crash, and consumer
tests; planning success is not behavior evidence.

## Predicted footprint and review attack surface

Expected ownership is adapter-sdk recovery/reclaim callbacks and supervisor-led
fixtures/tests, reusing existing public representations. Review should attack
stale recovery, changed policy, mixed attempts, ownership forgery, path aliases,
partial deletion, unknown-to-absent conversion, and reclaim as release cleanup.
