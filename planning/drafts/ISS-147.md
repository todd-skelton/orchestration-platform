## Why

Chase Sets #7766 run `m2-jpeg-corrective-20260914T1402` passed its resumed static gate at independently reviewed candidate `42bac8439e31b8354447f2675edfaea72890d4b5`, published that head to PR #8005, then stopped `publication-outcome-unknown`. Native stop: https://github.com/chase-sets/chase-sets/issues/7766#issuecomment-5666729434. Read-only reconciliation found the PR CONFLICTING/DIRTY against main `78848d23d610d3eba6c8f980fef81e6d82b56b42`; `git merge-tree` showed one route-inventory conflict in `deployables/platform-api/__tests__/route-collision.test.ts`. The queue stops on any rebase conflict without a resolution path. Todd accepted milestone 158 rollback and ordered this bounded native capability: https://github.com/chase-sets/chase-sets/issues/4388#issuecomment-5666826318. Main advancing while a reviewed candidate waits is ordinary delivery, not grounds for another product design or an unknown publication result.

## Done when

- A reviewed candidate that encounters a current-main rebase conflict receives one bounded conflict-resolution author restricted to the actual conflicting hunks. Preserve the reviewed feature and current-main changes; do not reset the implementation lineage or start a fresh full implementation. Use the existing author model, native worktree and lifecycle mechanisms rather than a parallel queue or new authority framework.
- An independent delta review checks the resolved hunks and inherits the retained source review and execution evidence. Bind the result to the exact resulting candidate. No self-review, full review sweep restart or inference that conflicted rebasing is patch-equivalent. Actual semantic expansion or a failed bounded resolution remains a named stop, not an unbounded repair loop.
- Persist enough existing attempt context that restart preserves the in-flight author, delta reviewer, consumed bounded resolution and original participants. One reviewed candidate's conflict cannot mint unlimited resolution rounds across restarts. Prior attempt ceilings and failed evidence remain intact.
- Reconcile publication outcome against actual remote/PR identity. A published exact candidate on a DIRTY/CONFLICTING PR is classified and routed to the bounded conflict path rather than `publication-outcome-unknown`; a wrong or genuinely unknown remote head never permits a blind repeated mutation. Keep ordinary final-head checks, publication, hosted CI, landing and deploy verification.
- Regress the recorded single-list-hunk case, conflict-free path, one-cycle exhaustion, restarts during resolution/review/publication, semantic scope escape, failed delta review and published-DIRTY versus genuinely unknown publication. Document the narrow behavior and preserved historical evidence. The same mechanism must not reopen or restart the rolled-back JPEG run.

## Out of scope

Repairing JPEG or PR #8005 in the platform, touching any preserved M2 runtime/worktree, resetting attempts or reviews, repeated full source sweeps, unconditional force pushes, bypassing hosted gates, new merge authority, changing milestone ownership, or dispatching proposed milestone 159 without Todd's separate assignment. Milestone 158 is now incumbent-owned; only milestone 155 remains platform-owned.
