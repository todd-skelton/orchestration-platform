## Context and evidence

Migration of the selected consumer in `docs/planning/first-consumer.md` requires
representative inputs and incumbent decisions without copying its policy into
the engine. See the classification from `ISS-001` and adapter boundary.

## Scope fence

In: sanitized histories for work discovery, readiness, dispatch, review,
breakers, deployment observation, replan, decisions, and recovery; expected
authoritative decisions; provenance and redaction report.

Out: credentials, source code, live mutation, raw transcripts, and claims that
advisory telemetry must match byte-for-byte.

## Decisions already made

Parity compares authoritative decisions and normalized state transitions.
Advisory timing, cost, and diagnostic text are compared only for declared
health semantics.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. Every behavior family and authority-timing row has a representative success,
   known-empty/unavailable, and failure history or an explicit evidence-backed
   exclusion. Evidence: complete coverage matrix.
2. Expected decisions are derived from the incumbent reducer, not hand-written
   after comparison. Evidence: provenance hashes and reproducible capture.
3. Fixtures contain no secrets, personal paths, repository-private content, or
   mutable external URLs. Evidence: redaction and entropy scans.
4. Every timing-sensitive case is captured at the exact lifecycle moment in
   `docs/planning/first-consumer.md`. Evidence: timestamped, schema-bound
   fixtures; post-hoc observations fail validation.
5. The fixture includes a complete sanitized incumbent state-family census for
   active cursors, breaker histories, worker terminals, discrepancies, and
   recovery evidence, with unknown families explicit. Evidence: coverage report
   consumed by `ISS-024`; omissions fail validation.

## Predicted footprint and dependencies

## Operator actions

in `docs/planning/first-consumer.md` and inspect redaction before commit.

## Glossary impact

None; parity and capture terms are registered already.

## External authority probe and evidence timing

Capture each code-host or deployment observation at the exact pre-dispatch,
pre-review, pre-enqueue, or post-deploy moment where the incumbent consumes it.

## Independent review packet seed

Attack missing failure histories, hand-authored expected results, secrets or
private content, post-hoc observations standing in for live timing, and parity
defined as log-string equality. Use the behavior-family coverage and redaction
reports.
