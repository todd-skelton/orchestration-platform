## Why

`queue.ts:2161`, :3844, :3912, :3993 and :4068 implement that stop;
:654 and :2530 also bind parsing/admission to four. History scans use the
ceiling at `supervision.ts:181`, :287, :451, :567, :933 and
`queue.ts:2797`, :2996. Raising a constant leaves the same failure.
Native launch admission separately stops at `queue.ts:4836`, :4919 and :5099.

retained four-attempt ceiling and terminal issue-local launch cutoff are
**interim behavior this set supersedes**. Do not modify its draft or PR #752.

## Decision

Recovery must continue when evidence improves while remaining bounded between judgments.
Removing all counters is simpler but loses the required independent checkpoints and finite native work steps.
Choose one judged next-step transition in the existing queue using retained outcomes and a small checkpoint in the shared recovery record.
Accept independent step-back latency at checkpoints instead of terminal parking or arbitrary count extensions.
Revisit if a parallel scheduler, copied incumbent ledger or per-site continuation grant is necessary.
