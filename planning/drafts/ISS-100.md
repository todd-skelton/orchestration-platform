## Why

After 26 days the loop under `scripts/dogfood/` works end to end but has
completed zero useful cycles without hand-written host scripts, and every fix
to it has spawned new "extract this manual handoff" issues. The kernel packages
were placeholders, the contracts package had no consumer, and the self-hosting
release program gated Chase Sets behind certification machinery a single
operator does not need. This issue records the 2026-09-10 replan: keep the
loop, delete everything it does not use, and restate the goal as unattended
self-improvement followed by Chase Sets delivery.

## Done when

- `main` is tagged `pre-replan-2026-09-10` and the tree contains only the loop,
  its tests, the planning checkers, the CI workflow, and `docs/loop.md`.
- `planning/roadmap.json` registers milestones M1..M3 and issues ISS-100..111;
  `pnpm planning:check` and `pnpm planning:board-check` pass.
- Old issues, epics and decision records are closed as not planned with a
  comment pointing at the replan PR.
- The three-OS `bootstrap` workflow is green on the replan PR.

## Out of scope

Any behavior change to the loop itself. Those are ISS-101 through ISS-105.
