## Scope fence

In scope: a closed `gate-stop-successor/v1` packet in the self loop config, its
read-only pre-reservation evaluator, one exclusive reservation, composition of
one successor implementation attempt in the same run, retained stop/evidence
bindings, replay/refusal behavior, focused regression fixtures, and one
post-PASS sentence in `docs/loop.md` documenting the admission boundary.

`LoopConfig.gateStopSuccessor` is optional, self-adapter-only, and has the
exact top-level keys `schemaVersion`, `repository`, `run`, `issueKey`,
`issueUrl`, `attemptDirectory`, `stopMarker`, `stopReason`, `gate`,
`candidateHead`, `priorAbsoluteAttempt`, `nextAbsoluteAttempt`,
`historyDigest`, `attemptCeiling`, `authorityUrl`, `authorityAuthor`,
`authorityBodySha256`, `briefRevision`, `cause`, and `evidence`. The literal
schema version is `gate-stop-successor/v1`; `cause` is the literal
`candidate-internal`; no unknown keys are accepted. `briefRevision` has exactly
`sha256` and `updatedAt`; `sha256` is SHA-256 of the raw UTF-8 current issue
body and `updatedAt` is the exact timezone-bearing issue update instant.
`evidence` has exactly `terminalPath`, `terminalSha256`, `logPath`,
`logSha256`, and `changedPathsSha256`. Both evidence paths are absolute,
normalized, regular files beneath the bound stopped attempt directory with no
symlink or parent escape; the terminal is `candidate-terminal.json` and the
complete log is `candidate.log`. Their digests are SHA-256 of their complete
raw bytes. `changedPathsSha256` is SHA-256 of
`JSON.stringify(sorted repository-relative changed paths)` using the existing
`changedFiles` result. `historyDigest` is SHA-256 of
`JSON.stringify(retained history)`; `authorityBodySha256` is SHA-256 of the
complete raw authority-comment body. `attemptDirectory` is the normalized
`stateRoot/run/<issue-key>-attempt-<priorAbsoluteAttempt>` directory; the
marker, reason, issue URL, run, key, candidate head/base and attempt records
must agree, and `nextAbsoluteAttempt` is exactly prior plus one.

The packet is mutually exclusive with `gateStopAuthorization`,
`terminalAttemptAdmission`, `acceptedReplan`, `integrationContinuation`, and
`verificationOnly`; validation rejects any coexistence before composition.
The sole reservation is an exclusive-create
`stateRoot/gate-stop-successor-<sha256(canonical({repository,issueKey}))>.json`
record with schema `gate-stop-successor-reservation/v1` and exactly these
top-level keys: `schemaVersion`, `packet`, `selectedIssue`, `configSha256`,
`stopSha256`, `attemptSha256`, `historySha256`, `terminalSha256`, `logSha256`,
`changedPathsSha256`, `authorityUrl`, `authorityAuthor`, `authorityBodySha256`,
and `observedAt`; no unknown keys are accepted. `packet` is the exact packet
object above. `selectedIssue` has exactly `key`, `number`, `url`, and `base`.
All `*Sha256` values are lowercase 64-hex SHA-256 digests. `configSha256` is
SHA-256 of canonical UTF-8 JSON for the effective config with
`gateStopSuccessor` omitted; `stopSha256` and `attemptSha256` hash the
complete raw retained `gate-stop.json` and `attempt.json` bytes;
`historySha256` hashes canonical UTF-8 JSON of retained history; and the
terminal/log/changed-path digests equal the packet evidence digests.
`canonical` recursively sorts object keys and applies JSON.stringify UTF-8
bytes. `observedAt` is the exact UTC ISO-8601 observation instant. The file is
created with an atomic exclusive create; a pre-existing record is replay only
when every reservation byte and binding is identical. A changed packet, config,
selected issue, later stop, missing or changed authority/evidence, path escape,
changed ceiling, landed-repair grant, conflicting census record, or any other
admission object refuses before reservation and writes no replacement bytes.

The successor starts from the current selected `base` in a distinct ordinary
attempt directory; it never reuses or rebases the stopped candidate. The old
stop, attempt, source/review records, gate terminal/log, publication state,
history and accounting remain byte-identical. The evaluator reads the latest
completed same-key stop census and the complete authority probe immediately
before the exclusive reservation.

Non-goals: `gateStopAuthorization`, terminal-attempt or accepted-replan
semantics; source-stage, repair-stage, verification-only, base-failed,
workspace, hosted, malformed, or historical-stop admission; reset/transfer of
attempts, charges, native launches, reviewer ladders, ceilings, or evidence;
automatic retry, parking, readiness, publication, merge, deployment, provider
credentials, TCGplayer integration, or product behavior.

## Glossary impact

`gate-stop successor` means one same-run next implementation attempt admitted
from the latest candidate-internal ordinary delivery gate stop by a complete
authority packet. `Inert` means the ordinary queue refuses the retained stop
without that packet; it does not mean the stop is forgotten or non-evidence.
No existing public term changes.
