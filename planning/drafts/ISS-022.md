## Question

Can the exact Node 24 candidates in the `Portable primitives probe and
capability ledger` on hosted macOS, Windows, and Linux prove the physical,
helper/ABI/custody, create-once, lock/owner-death, replace, CAS/readback,
same-lock absence, process, probe-handle, and parser facts required before
`ISS-004`, `ISS-005`, `ISS-020`, and `ISS-031` select production mechanisms?

Rounds 243 and 244 blocked the former packet. This repair is limited to their
named gaps: literal physical/custody and observation/decision digest formulas,
one authenticated diagnostic terminal chain, an honest direct-child process
boundary, and default lock-handle non-inheritance. It adds no helper, principal
isolation, reboot lane, or production service.

Rounds 245 and 246 closed those literal gaps but found one new cycle: decision
publication named an installed release that cannot exist before ISS-020/N0.
This slice replaces only that unavailable writer with
`DstableHarnessSubject`, derived from the already-authenticated ISS-006 stable
harness/test/registry/contract inputs and executed from their exact protected-
main checkout.

## Probe method and lifecycle timing

Use the stable `ISS-006` harness with suite `iss022-portable-primitives` and one
required job per hosted OS. Candidate children mutate only one stable-parent
runner-temp custody root. The authority ledger fixes the Node calls, 21 vector
IDs and order, physical component and `Dphys/Ddest` formulas, helper/ABI/custody
receipts, fixed bytes, barriers/crash prefixes, errno reduction, observation and
capability-decision records, PASS provider join, and BLOCK_REPLAN diagnostic
provider/terminal join.

This issue measures process-crash/reopen behavior only. Reboot, cross-volume
mount mutation, persistent-host re-entry, native process enumeration, and
production custody remain later selections. The direct-child handle is
authority only for that child; a grandchild named by candidate IPC/stdout is
UNSUPPORTED, not inferred identity. Probe-local `WeakMap`/nonce behavior does
not claim production ISS-004 equivalence.

   and `Dabi`; filesystem/statfs/case/Unicode inputs derive each OS's

10. The parent and three fresh children parse identical ISS-002 canonical and

11. Vector, observation, custody, decision-core, provider-review, independent-

## Disposition rule

reviewed architecture selection backed by the measured row; single-OS inference
or silent weakening is forbidden.
