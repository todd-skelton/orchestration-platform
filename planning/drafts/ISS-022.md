## Question

Can the exact selected helper/profile on current macOS, Windows, and Linux prove
stable physical destination identity/observations, one destination-owner lock,
kernel-exclusive custody of the runtime state-mutation lock, atomic CAS/
readback, owner-death release, create-once/readback existence plus reliable
absence observation at constructed canonical paths, and nonserializable
state-service guarantees required by `ISS-004`, `ISS-005`, and `ISS-020`?

## Probe method and lifecycle timing

Use the `ISS-006` harness with independently launched processes and controlled
barriers. Probe lock acquisition before any authority observation, concurrent
target mutations, two installation IDs targeting one destination, rotation
versus target mutation, owner death at every step, handle inheritance/
duplication, symlinks/reparse points, exact-byte create-once/replace/readback,
process identity/termination, and clean reboot/re-entry. Derive stable
`Dphys/Ddest` separately from versioned helper/path/case/Unicode/custody
observations and bind all selected profile facts to the test bundle.
For authority-history records, probe create-once and exact-byte readback
existence semantics at constructed canonical ordinal-derived paths, including
reliable absence observation of unwritten canonical paths as needed by the
head-plus-one and head-plus-two absence probes, on every supported
filesystem/profile.

## Required durable evidence

1. Exactly one kernel-exclusive holder across unrelated processes and children;
   no dual holder, borrowed/duplicated handle, or writer after release.
2. Owner death releases through the kernel and the next holder observes exactly
   predecessor or successor—not PID/age/lease inference.
3. Rotation versus every other pointer kind has one linear order: target fully
   under old epoch or after selected/read-back new epoch, never mixed.
4. Atomic replace/create-once/CAS/readback and directory durability hold for the
   exact canonical path/profile or report unsupported.
5. Complete positive/negative matrix per OS, helper/profile, crash point, and
   hostile lock/symlink/permission/handle shape.
6. Same physical destination across project/install/helper/custody tuples and
   admitted aliases yields one `Dphys/Ddest`, current pointer, lock, and winner;
   ambiguous/symlink/junction/reparse aliases refuse.
7. ISS-004 WeakMap/nonces and read/mutation handles cannot be cloned,
   transferred, serialized, or reused after release, owner death, or rotation.
8. Under the selected lock, create-once and readback at constructed canonical
   ordinal-derived paths observe exactly the written record bytes, and a
   probe of an unwritten canonical path reliably observes absence, sufficient
   for the full `authority-history/v1` chain walk and its head-plus-one/
   head-plus-two absence probes to observe every retained record.
9. After authority CAS, old E(n) has no callable capability. Fresh E(n+1) can
   walk the persisted chain and verify the selected head from
   CAS_ARMED/readback/lock/custody facts; cross-process or stale-context
   verification attempts refuse.
10. Receipts emit the exact closed `physical-destination-identity/v1` and
    `physical-destination-locator-observation-receipt/v1` ledger records:
    canonical one-component leaf bytes, exact OS/profile/kind enums, admitted
    all-non-null readbacks, unsupported/unknown all-null readbacks, validity,
    unchanged `Dphys/Ddest` across helper/profile/custody rotation, and the
    exact same-lock `external-destination-absence-observation/v1` branches used
    by E0 and both unused and consumed retirement. Every member, alias, profile, Unicode
    version, absence reason, branch, and coordinated substitution mutant
    refuses.

## Disposition rule

PASS selects the exact physical identity/observation, destination/runtime lock,
helper/ABI/custody, and handle-confinement facts consumed by `ISS-004` and
`ISS-020`. Any unproved target returns `BLOCK_REPLAN`; a global sequencer or
other fallback requires a new architecture decision. No timeout, PID, lease,
stale-age, unsigned lock, single-OS inference, or silent weakening is allowed.

## Scope fence

implementation, project policy, or granting a candidate authority.
