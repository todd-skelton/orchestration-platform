## Question

Can the exact selected helper/profile on current macOS, Windows, and Linux prove
kernel-exclusive custody of the fixed non-symlink state-mutation lock, atomic
pointer CAS/readback, owner-death release, and process guarantees required by
`ISS-004` and `ISS-005`?

## Probe method and lifecycle timing

Use the `ISS-006` harness with independently launched processes and controlled
barriers. Probe lock acquisition before any authority observation, concurrent
target mutations, rotation versus target mutation, owner death at every step,
handle inheritance/duplication, symlinks/reparse points, exact-byte create-once/
replace/readback, process identity/termination, and clean reboot/re-entry. Bind
OS image, filesystem, Node/helper bytes, ABI, lock profile, custody principal,
and test-bundle digest.

## Required durable evidence

1. Exactly one kernel-exclusive holder across unrelated processes and children;
   no dual holder, borrowed/duplicated handle, or writer after release.
2. Owner death releases through the kernel and the next holder observes exactly
   predecessor or successor—not PID/age/lease inference.
3. Rotation versus every other pointer kind has one linear order: target fully
   under old epoch or after selected/read-back new epoch, never mixed.
4. Atomic replace/create-once/CAS/readback and directory durability hold for the
   exact canonical path/profile or report unsupported.
5. Complete positive/negative matrix per OS, helper/profile, crash point, and
   hostile lock/symlink/permission/handle shape.

## Disposition rule

PASS selects the exact OS profile/helper/ABI/custody facts consumed by
`ISS-004`. Any unproved target returns `BLOCK_REPLAN`; a global sequencer or
other fallback requires a new architecture decision. No timeout, PID, lease,
stale-age, unsigned lock, single-OS inference, or silent weakening is allowed.

## Scope fence

In: read-only capability probing and durable receipts. Out: production state
implementation, project policy, or granting a candidate authority.
