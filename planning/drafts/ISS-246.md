continues ordinary delivery without an author, a new attempt or a ceiling
change. This changes the instrument, not the brief, the budgets or the gates.

level: ISSUE - one new prerequisite defect slice; no sibling re-cut and no

## Decision

A reviewed head stopped only by an executed hosted failure must stop with a self-describing diagnostic and have exactly one closed, host-bound re-observation route.
The simplest alternative is to let the host re-run the job and edit nothing, but the installed queue replays the failed record as terminal and the ceiling admits no attempt 5.
Extend the existing gate-stop grant family with one mutually exclusive `hostedExecutedFailure` kind that reuses the ISS-230/245 reservation, observation and resume path.
Accept that admission requires an already executed green run on the exact unchanged head and the unchanged base's green control, never an inference from error text, duration or a passing subset.
The choice is wrong if any automatic retry, timeout increase, gate weakening, second grant per lineage, new attempt, author launch or ceiling change becomes possible.
