## Why

Trade-offs are decided in draft prose. When a draft states the constraint,
the alternative and what was not built (ISS-162, ISS-166), reviewers cite
those lines and G0 answers are specific; when it does not, G0 degrades to
taste. Nothing in the Planning section says what a consequential draft should
contain beyond Why, Done when and Out of scope.

Eleven self drafts carry a `QUALITY_PROFILE:` line borrowed from the Chase
Sets delivery skill, where it selects weights for a twelve-key Quality Packet
that the product-side author fills and the independent reviewer verifies. The
platform has no packet, no weights and no consumer; the tag reaches the self
reviewer as prompt text with nothing to apply it to. The platform must not
grow a competing packet, and the self reviewer needs one sentence on how to
treat a Chase Sets packet it sees.

## Decision

Constraint: no new checker, schema or prompt mechanism; AGENTS.md admits a
mechanism only when a cycle records the blocker it removes, and none has.
Simplest alternative: say nothing and rely on example drafts. Chosen: a short
optional `## Decision` paragraph in the draft grammar, described in the
Planning section, plus a one-sentence rule on Chase Sets packets and removal
of the stray tag from open self drafts. Accepted downside: the paragraph is
unenforced and can be skipped. Revisit when: a review FAIL or a parked issue
traces a wrong shape to a draft that had no Decision paragraph; that is the
recorded blocker for enforcement.

## Done when

- The Planning section of `docs/loop.md` documents an optional
  `## Decision` section between Why and Done when with exactly five
  sentences: the constraint, the simplest viable alternative, the option
  chosen, the downside accepted, and the observation that would show the
  choice was wrong. It states that the section is expected for changes to
  persisted records, published names, prompts or process rules, and that
  `planning:check` does not validate it.
- The same section states that a `QUALITY_PROFILE` line or Quality Packet
  belongs to the Chase Sets delivery skill, that self drafts do not carry it,
  and that a platform reviewer verifies every packet claim independently and
  never adopts one as evidence.
- Every open self draft that carries `QUALITY_PROFILE` at the time of
  authoring has the line removed; closed drafts are history and unchanged.
  `pnpm planning:board-check` semantics are unchanged; the operator syncs
  the GitHub issue bodies for edited open drafts after landing.
- No file under `scripts/`, `adapters/` or `test/` changes. Run
  typecheck, format:check, planning:check and test.

## Simplest shape and not built

| Not built                                   | Reason                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `planning:check` validation of the section | Proves presence, not reasoning; no incident recorded the need       |
| A one-way/two-way door field                 | Prompt text is fingerprinted and can carry authority; label misleads |
| A platform Quality Packet                    | Chase Sets owns it; the bounded JSON verdict is the platform's form |

## Out of scope

Changing reviewer prompts or the verdict schema (ISS-172), compacting the
document (ISS-173), editing closed drafts, any Chase Sets skill file, or a
periodic shape review.
