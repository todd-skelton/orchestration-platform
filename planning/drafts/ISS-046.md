## Context and evidence

The round-373 replan measured the review corpus against the work model's own
first value: if it can be deterministic, it should be. The retained
pressure-test corpus holds 166 round records against 46 registered issues, and
recurring finding classes — scope-fence drift, authority language in
non-authoritative surfaces, missing negative controls, evidence cheaper than
the criterion it discriminates — are re-litigated in prose on each slice. The
planning checker family (`scripts/planning/check.mjs`,
`scripts/planning/board-check.mjs`) already demonstrates that structural
finding classes are cheap to enforce deterministically once, instead of
repeatedly in prose.

## Scope fence

In: a machine-readable finding-class census over `planning/pressure-tests/`
recording each class, its recurrence count, and its source rounds; the Round
373 codification rule (a class recurring in three or more rounds is codified
into a deterministic check or records a concrete non-codifiable reason); the
deterministic checks for codified classes, added to the existing planning
checker family; and fixtures drawn from the historical offending artifacts.

Out: the packaged portable planning standards (`ISS-040`), review/repair
module finding transport (`ISS-039`), independent review discrimination
calibration (`ISS-043`), retroactive edits to past round records, any new
authority service, and runtime package changes.

## Decisions already made

Codification extends the existing checker family rather than introducing a new
service or scorekeeper. The census is reviewed data in the repository, not a
dashboard. A class whose discrimination requires judgment records why and
remains prose; taste alone is not a non-codifiable reason. Prediction: a
codified class stops recurring in subsequent rounds. Review-by horizon: thirty
further pressure rounds or the M2 exit, whichever comes first, with a recorded
keep/revise/delete result.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. The census enumerates every finding class with its recurrence count and
   source-round references, and a census row that cites a missing round or
   misses a cited class refuses. Evidence: census fixtures with removed-round
   and uncounted-class negatives.
2. Every class with three or more recurrences carries either a deterministic
   check or a concrete non-codifiable reason; a class with neither refuses.
   Evidence: census completeness check with a single-row mutant.
3. Each codified check fails on at least one historical offending artifact and
   passes the repaired form of the same artifact. Evidence: paired
   before/after fixtures from the corpus.
4. Re-running the census against an unchanged corpus is deterministic and
   byte-identical. Evidence: repeat-run transcript comparison.

## Predicted footprint and dependencies

Implementation owns the census artifact under `planning/pressure-tests/`, the
census and codified checks inside `scripts/planning/`, their fixtures, and
exact capability slot
`test/capability-slots/ISS-046/planning%3Afinding-census-check.mjs`. This
planning registration uses the approved shared-scaffold amendment to add its
root placeholder and command census together. Blocked directly by `ISS-000`
(repository toolchain and executable verification surface). It blocks nothing:
codified checks reduce review cost but no slice's correctness depends on them.

## Independent review packet seed

Attack census counts that cite no source rounds, codified checks that pass
their own historical offender, non-codifiable reasons that are taste,
census drift from the corpus after new rounds land, checks that duplicate
`ISS-040` module scope, and codification used to widen checker authority
beyond structure and identity. Use the paired before/after fixtures and the
repeat-run transcript.
