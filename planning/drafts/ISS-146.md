## Done when

- Author scratch and evidence writes are confined to the existing attempt temp root (`author-temp`), outside the source tree. Apply the same existing-path rule on initial, corrective and resumed launches; do not add a separate authority framework.
- Delivery gates run against the committed candidate tree, or explicitly refuse source-tree contamination with the offending untracked/ignored path. An empty ignored scratch directory cannot become an unexplained committed-product failure. Preserve tracked edits and unrelated paths; do not silently clean the source worktree.
- Persist the complete failing command and diagnostic and expose its artifact path in the stop note and native recovery inputs. A 500-character excerpt is not the only retained evidence. Keep ordinary exact-head independent review and all local/hosted gates.
- Regress the observed empty ignored directory, clean committed tree, actual committed structure failure and nonempty contamination cases. Cover resumed delivery without minting another author, implementation attempt or corrective cycle; retain old runtime, reviews and consumed attempts unchanged.
- Document the small native behavior and its limits. This implementation supplies no permission to restart #7766 or to mutate its preserved worktree/runtime.
