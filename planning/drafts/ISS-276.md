## Decision

The complete quiet-verification protocol grows the default-author prompt by 475 to 637 characters, while the unchanged `< 8000` corrective-prompt assertion leaves about 47 characters of legacy-retry headroom at fixture root 120 on current main and is already red at root 134. The simplest alternative was to keep `< 8000` and normalize fixture root bytes out of the measured prompt, which would hide the real composed length that reaches a worker. This slice adopts the host D3 cap of 8700 characters (`< 8700`) as the single numeric change to the four-mode queue-adapter assertion, appends the complete protocol to the default-author default-gate branch only, and keeps the exact full-prompt pin with deletion and narrowing mutants plus role negatives. The accepted downside is about 110 characters of legacy-retry margin at the widest supported fixture root, so any future default-author wording growth must be re-measured against this cap before landing. The choice is wrong if any supported root or mode or any required inherited evidence measures at or above 8700, or if the four-mode regression can stay green only by normalizing production bytes; either observation returns to host planning with no further increase.
