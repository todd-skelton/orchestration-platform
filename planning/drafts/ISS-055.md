artifact), 5 (`modules/manifest.json` as the sole ordered four-row registry),
and the row-dependent half of 7 (the generator's literal static imports, frozen

This issue follows the repository's definition-then-source pattern used by
Rounds 434, 443, and 446: an independently reviewed definition round fixes the
exact literals and vectors before any source packet, and each following source
packet lands one consumer-backed tranche with its own independent exact-head
review. No tranche closes this issue by itself.

Three recorded frontier risks are declared inputs to this issue and must be
resolved or explicitly re-recorded by its definition round:

- R2: `modules/build/generate-registry.mjs:24-27` (`owns non-empty module rows`)
  is unreachable, because `:21` already requires byte equality with `"[]\n"`.
  Round 453 recorded the unreachability and renamed the misleading test; the

- R3: `docs/architecture/module-abi.md:128-131` requires extra or partial

- R8: the `ISS-011` draft previously carried no narrative for Rounds 448 and 450
  and still predicted creating `modules/delivery/` and `modules/repair/`. The

Out: any change to the landed `modules/planning/src/index.ts` or

## Decisions already made

The closed ABI, manifest row grammar, ordered five-stage generator, source
census, and static-admission rules are already fixed in landed, independently
reviewed text at `docs/architecture/module-abi.md:52-139`; the sole-generator
and generated-output rules are at `docs/architecture/module-abi.md:423-433`.
`docs/architecture/bootstrap-manifests.md:45-52` records that `ISS-000` created
the empty manifest and the deterministic placeholder generator and that
`ISS-011` replaces the manifest/generator contract and adds the four
entrypoints; `docs/architecture/bootstrap-manifests.md:106-112` fixes that the
root script runs the exact generator contract before the `self-host` target
only, that esbuild receives the resulting literal static imports through the
composition entrypoint, and that no module-list plugin, dynamic import, or
hand-maintained registry is permitted.

`docs/architecture/module-abi.md:70` states that N0 has exactly four rows in the
table order `planning, delivery, review, repair`. An interim one-, two-, or
three-row manifest contradicts that landed text and would require its own ABI
amendment round with independent review; option B of Decision #275 was not
ruled.

Round 450 fixed that there is no zero-consumer exemption: a surface is
admissible only when a real executable consumer exercises it. That rule is why
this issue is blocked rather than partially implemented.

## Consumer-backed manifest and ordered build definition

Round 434 fixed the exact private manifest row and build order for this surface.
That definition moved verbatim from `ISS-011` under the Decision #275 split and
is reproduced here unchanged:

> The manifest remains one canonical ordered private array. Each row binds fixed
> source/emitted paths, normalized tracked-source census, exact emitted bytes,
> the existing descriptor identity, and direct ABI/schema/compatibility/
> capability projections. The sole generator seals all inputs, denies ambient or
> effect imports, emits serially with fixed options, rechecks identities, and
> publishes each ignored output atomically with the registry last only after the
> complete four-row verification. A final census gates the self-host build; build
> output grants no installed or release authority.

Rounds 434 through 453 remain cumulative `ISS-011` lineage for this surface and
are not reset by the split: 434 (manifest and planning definition), abandoned
435 and 437 with their heads, reviews, and hosted failure, 436, 438, 439, 443,
444, 446, 448 with PR #266, 450 with PR #270, and 453 with PR #277. Round 434
also fixed that final manifest and generator activation waits until all four
baseline source entrypoints have consumer-backed semantics and executable
evidence; that condition is still unmet and is why this issue is parked.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. One host-neutral module artifact is byte-stable on all targets; concrete host
   discovery is tested by `ISS-021`. The `ISS-001` source-term map is test input
   only and is absent from the bundle. Evidence: packaging hashes, file census,
   and manifest/package inspection.
2. `modules/manifest.json` is the sole ordered module registry and binds source,
   emitted, descriptor, ABI, schema, compatibility, and capability digests for
   exactly the four baseline entrypoints. Missing, extra, duplicate, moved,
   dynamic-path, and digest-mutated rows refuse. Evidence: manifest/entrypoint
   matrix and clean three-OS packaging hashes.
3. `modules/build/generate-registry.mjs` emits `modules/.generated/registry.ts`
   with literal imports and frozen digests in manifest order. Repeated three-OS
   generation over those rows is byte-identical; stale, extra, dynamic-import,
   path-escape, and changed-descriptor output mutants fail before bundling.
   Evidence: the row-dependent generator matrix over the activated four-row
   manifest. The row-independent half of this sentence — sole generator, sole
   emitted output, dirty, missing, and hand-edited mutants — stays with
   `ISS-011` and is already evidenced by
   `test/module-registry-placeholder.test.ts`.
4. The row-level refusal branch is reachable and asserted rather than shadowed
   by a whole-file byte comparison (R2), and extra or partial ignored output and
   temporary residue refuse or are removed as
   `docs/architecture/module-abi.md:128-131` requires (R3). Evidence: one mutant
   per refusal category with its complete message, plus a killed-run temporary
   residue vector.

four-row `modules/manifest.json`, the replacement
`modules/build/generate-registry.mjs`, the emitted per-module
`modules/<moduleId>/dist/index.mjs` ignored artifacts and the regenerated
`modules/.generated/registry.ts`, plus manifest, source-census, generator, and
packaging tests and its own registered verification command. It changes no
public contract package, no root configuration outside the shared-scaffold
amendment its first source packet declares, and no landed `planning` or
`review` module semantics.

Blocked directly by `ISS-011`, which owns the module ABI, the landed `planning`
and `review` entrypoint semantics, the dispatch brief and catalog structures,
and the row-independent generator invariants this issue extends.

**Parked, with an explicit un-park condition.** No registered issue owns the
delivery and repair executable consumers. The planning lane checked every
candidate named in the ruling and found none: `ISS-026` owns the step-4
`module.plan` invocation of an already-admitted registry entry
(`planning/drafts/ISS-026.md:96-102`) and is itself downstream of module
packaging through `ISS-012`, so it consumes this issue rather than preceding it;
`ISS-041` explicitly excludes module registry admission and invokes "one inline
trivial plan function" and a stub review
(`planning/drafts/ISS-041.md:21-32`); `ISS-008` invokes no module at all and
excludes review semantics (`planning/drafts/ISS-008.md:20-25`); and `ISS-009`
records that "repair/replan taxonomy belongs to planning modules rather than the
engine" (`planning/drafts/ISS-009.md:31-33`) and consumes only an opaque module
disposition payload. `ISS-039` owns future review and repair module *content*
and its manifest rows, not an executable consumer, and depends on this issue.

Un-park condition: a registered issue lands an executable consumer that invokes
a `delivery` entrypoint and a `repair` entrypoint with real inputs, so that both
have consumer-backed semantics under the Round 450 no-zero-consumer rule. Option
A of Decision #275 — a bounded walking-skeleton fixture-runtime extension — is
the eventual route named by the ruling and was expressly not admitted by it; it
needs its own registered slice and ruling. When that consumer exists, this issue
gains a direct `blocked_by` edge on its owner and leaves the parked state. Until
then this issue is registered and visible on the board and takes no source
tranche.

Consumers of this issue, each by its own consuming text: `ISS-039`
("Implementation owns the future review/repair module content, manifest rows"),
