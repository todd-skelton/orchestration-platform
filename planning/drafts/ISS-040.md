## Context and evidence

The incumbent often created complexity before implementation: acceptance
criteria prescribed test architecture instead of observable properties,
replans promoted a reviewer's mechanism into a requirement, and surfaces with
no consumer were frozen for hypothetical use. Chase Sets issue `#7000`
registered the planning-side correction after the 2026-08-17 proportionality
audit. The portable planning module must supply counter-pressure without a new
authority service or an over-engineering scorekeeper.

## Scope fence

In: planning standards with a registered prediction and review-by horizon;
an adversarial deletion test that constructs the strictly smaller credible
alternative; observable-property acceptance criteria paired with the cheapest
discriminating evidence; replan intake that treats a prescribed mechanism as a
floor of properties to preserve, not an implementation mandate; and a
consuming dependency or explicit parked unpark condition for every
zero-consumer surface.

Out: engine contracts, review/repair finding transport (`ISS-039`), consumer
rubric wording, runtime enforcement services, package/runtime/contracts
changes, and adapter implementations.

## Decisions already made

Each retained planning standard is experiment-shaped: it states a falsifiable
prediction and the event or date by which review must keep, revise, or delete
it. An adversarial pass has explicit authority to propose deletion or the
smallest credible alternative. Acceptance criteria name observable properties;
their evidence is the cheapest check that distinguishes the named defect from
the control. A replan may retain a reviewer's mechanism only when the mechanism
is the demonstrated minimum for the properties to preserve.

Zero-consumer surface is ready only when a registered consumer depends on it,
or when it is parked with an evidence-based unpark condition. Future-consumer
prose is neither.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. Every packaged proportionality standard registers a prediction and a
   review-by event or horizon, and fixtures refuse either field missing or an
   elapsed horizon without a recorded keep/revise/delete result. Evidence:
   golden records and single-field mutants.
2. The adversarial plan pass records the deletion test and a concrete strictly
   smaller alternative for each retained mechanism. A plan whose smaller
   alternative still satisfies every observable property yields a shrink
   finding and bounded remedy. Evidence: paired minimal/oversized briefs.
3. Acceptance criteria bind observable properties to the cheapest
   discriminating evidence for the named defect class. Mechanism-only criteria
   and more expensive evidence with no additional discrimination yield rewrite
   findings. Evidence: property/evidence fixtures with cost-order controls.
4. Replan intake extracts the properties a prescribed mechanism demonstrates
   and treats that mechanism as a floor only when no smaller alternative proves
   them. Verbatim mechanism transcription without that comparison yields a
   property-preserving rewrite finding. Evidence: replan transcript mutants.
5. A zero-consumer surface is ready only with a registered consuming dependency
   or an explicit parked state naming its unpark condition. Hypothetical
   consumer prose, an epic-only owner, or a blank condition refuses readiness.
   Evidence: dependency and parked-state negatives.
