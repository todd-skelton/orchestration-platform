## Context and evidence

The incumbent authored most of its measured over-engineering upstream of
implementation: acceptance criteria mandating test architecture instead of the
property to prove, replan rounds transcribing reviewer prescriptions verbatim
into amended criteria, and zero-consumer surface frozen ahead of any consumer
(chase-sets 2026-08-17 thirty-PR audit; planning-side fix registered as
chase-sets issue 7000). Its plan-time adversarial pass could only add
requirements — no rubric item recognized an oversized plan as a finding. The
portable planning module must ship the counter-pressure as content rather than
inherit the ratchet. Module packaging is `ISS-011`; the review-side channel is
`ISS-039`.

## Scope fence

In: planning-module artifact standards requiring an adversarial plan pass with
deletion authority (construct the strictly smaller plan; a plan whose smaller
variant still satisfies the outcome and defect constraints is a finding with a
shrink remedy), acceptance criteria that bind observable properties with the
cheapest discriminating evidence, refusal of mechanism-mandating criteria, a
consuming dependency or explicit parked state for any zero-consumer surface,
and repair/replan intake that maps findings to properties-to-preserve rather
than prescribed mechanisms.

Out: engine contracts, review/repair module semantics (`ISS-039`), consumer
rubric wording, and any machine gate beyond fixture validation.

## Decisions already made

Plan-size judgment stays inside the module's adversarial pass; the platform
ships no over-engineering police tooling. A finding of excess carries a shrink
remedy and the smaller alternative considered. Standards are experiment-shaped:
each carries a registered prediction and a review-by horizon.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. The planning module's artifact standard includes both adversarial checks
   with explicit oversized-plan finding authority and the shrink-remedy shape.
   Evidence: golden standard text in the packaged module bundle.
2. A fixture brief whose criterion mandates a test architecture instead of an
   observable property yields a finding with a property-rewrite remedy.
   Evidence: single-field-negative fixtures.
3. A fixture replan brief transcribing a prescribed mechanism verbatim yields a
   property-preserving rewrite finding. Evidence: replan transcript fixtures.
4. A fixture brief freezing zero-consumer surface without a consuming
   dependency or parked state refuses readiness. Evidence: dependency-negative
   fixtures.
