## Decision

The constraint is that Q, Q2 and R recur past the unchanged 30 s Vitest limit on hosted Windows, on main and on unrelated PRs, which blocks ISS-257 and every later M1 delivery, while ISS-160 forbids raising the limit and ISS-221's r5 ruling fences how lifecycle work may be cut.
The simplest viable alternative is a larger per-case budget or host reruns until green, which the closed 120 s budget PR and `docs/loop.md:1791-1793` already reject because they hide the cost instead of removing it.
This issue measures the timed windows of Q, Q2 and R first, then removes only redundant fixture preparation and r5-fenced duplicate read-only Git probes, and splits R's five-fault group into whole rows on the landed ISS-235 precedent, proving a deterministic child-process reduction and hosted headroom.
The downside accepted is that, if the measured removable work cannot bring Q or Q2 under the bound, this slice returns to planning with its measurements instead of landing a partial fix, and ISS-257 stays parked meanwhile.
A hosted Windows run at or after the final head that times out Q, Q2, R or a row split from R, or a measurement showing no admissible removable work for Q or Q2, would show the choice was wrong.
