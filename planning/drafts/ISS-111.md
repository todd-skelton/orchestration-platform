## Why

Once the loop has delivered a Chase Sets milestone, routine delivery should
run on the platform and the `milestone-orchestrator` host loop should stop
being the default.

## Done when

- Routine Chase Sets issues are delivered by the platform loop for one full
  milestone with the incumbent idle.
- A one-page rollback note in the Chase Sets repository says how to resume the
  incumbent skill if the platform stops.
- The skill's routine-delivery sections are marked superseded; its
  Todd-decision and operator-action sections remain.

## Out of scope

Deleting the incumbent. Any second consumer.
