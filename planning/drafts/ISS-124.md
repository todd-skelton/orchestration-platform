## Why

`hosted-check-failed:<name>` and `merge-queue-removed` are typed stops in
`scripts/dogfood/delivery.ts`. On this repository that was acceptable because
the author runs the whole suite locally. On Chase Sets the local gates are
the scoped static check and typecheck; hosted CI owns the full suite and the
merge group runs it again on the synthetic base. In the sample read on
2026-09-12, 3 of the last 6 pull-request runs and 2 of the last 4
merge-group runs of `platform-pr.yml` failed. The incumbent controller reads
the failing log itself and gives the author one lane with that log.

## Done when

- When delivery observes a required check in the `fail` or `cancel` bucket,
  or the merge queue removed the PR, delivery returns the existing
  `{ status: "failed", head, reviewId, findings }` result instead of
  throwing. The single blocking finding has `file` equal to the check name
  (or `merge-queue` for a removal), `line` 1, and `text` holding the last
  4,000 characters of `gh run view <id> --log-failed` for the run behind the
  check link, or the queue-entry state for a removal. The failed-attempt
  receipt accepts a finding whose `file` names a check rather than a
  candidate path.
- The next attempt starts from the rejected head rebased by the loop onto
  current `origin/main` before the author launches; the attempt record
  stores both the rejected head and the rebased base. A conflicting rebase
  is the typed stop `rebase-conflict`, which ISS-123 parks.
- The repair prompt already applies prescribed findings verbatim; a CI
  finding needs no new prompt text beyond the finding itself. The repair
  review remains a delta review over the changed hunks.
- The four-attempt ceiling is unchanged; a CI repair consumes one attempt.
- `test/dogfood/delivery.test.ts` proves with fake `gh` output that a failed
  check and a queue removal each produce the failed result with the bounded
  log, and `test/dogfood/queue.test.ts` proves the rebased base and the
  `rebase-conflict` stop.

## Out of scope

Retrying a flaky check without a repair. Reading logs from jobs other than
the failing ones. Repairing a red staging deploy; that stays a learning note
for a person.
