## Context and evidence

`ISS-036` must exercise two negative controls that only the binder can judge:
"change the live ruleset after receipt creation" and "set/move the anchor
variable after the producer run"
(`planning/drafts/ISS-036.md`'s negative-control list). Round 456 established
that the binder's third input, `observedProtection`, is "fresh ISS-038
read-back evidence" (`contract-decisions.md:7069-7070`) and that "ISS-036
does not produce `observedProtection`" (`planning/pressure-tests/2026-09-05-round-456.md:685-686`).
Decision #302's readiness audit (§3, CF-6; §4, F4) resolves this boundary
question explicitly: this issue builds an offline six-purpose
`observedProtection` fixture producer sufficient to drive
`bindRepositoryProtectionEvidence` for exactly those two controls, without
becoming ISS-038's live producer.

## Scope fence

In: an offline fixture producer for `observedProtection` covering the six
purposes `bindRepositoryProtectionEvidence` needs to judge the two
binder-only controls.

Out: any live read-back; any claim to own or become the `ISS-038` producer.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. Both binder-judged controls refuse with the binder's exact issue codes
   when fed the fixture's mutated `observedProtection` alongside `ISS-059`'s
   receipt.
2. The positive control (no post-creation ruleset change, no post-run anchor
   move) binds successfully.
3. The fixture's `evaluatedAt` equals `completedAt`, per
   `contract-decisions.md:7081-7082`.
