## Why

Chase Sets is the first real consumer. Its incumbent controller (the
`milestone-orchestrator` skill and `chase-sets/.orchestrator` scripts) already
encodes issue readiness, lane branches, review contracts, merge-queue landing
and deploy verification. The adapter translates those rules; it does not
rebuild them.

## Done when

- `adapters/chase-sets.mjs` implements the ISS-107 seam: selection by the Chase
  Sets ready labels and milestone order, its branch naming, its PR body
  conventions, its required checks, merge-queue enqueue as the landing method,
  and a post-merge deploy verification that reuses the incumbent's existing
  scripts as observed behavior.
- The adapter carries no platform logic and the platform carries no Chase Sets
  vocabulary.
- A dry run against the Chase Sets repository selects the correct next issue
  and produces the expected branch and PR shape without dispatching a worker.

## Out of scope

Running a real Chase Sets issue through the loop (ISS-110). Retiring the
incumbent (ISS-111).
