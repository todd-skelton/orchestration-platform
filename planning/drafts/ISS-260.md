## Decision

An unresolved heavy-gate timeout must not become an author-failed parking decision without a same-profile unchanged-base control tied to the exact test identity. The smallest alternative is to keep the current author-failed path and ask a person to infer load from a focused pass, but that cannot distinguish candidate cost from host contention. Choose a host-owned attribution handoff at the existing retained failure and gate-classification seams, preserving the first output and returning base/load results to host attribution. The downside accepted is one additional host-owned evidence step before a disposition, with no local retry, hidden retry, attempt refund, or timeout change. This choice is wrong if a candidate-only failure can bypass the ordinary work-stop path, or if a base/load result parks, resets, skips, or synthesizes a PASS.
