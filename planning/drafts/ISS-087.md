## Scope and observed need

Preserve one externally authorized COMPLETE or DELTA scope through source
dispatch, classification, malformed-report replacement, selected-review
validation, repair handoff and queue continuation. DELTA binds the exact run,
source configuration fingerprint, source author, candidate head, comparison
base, adopting executor revision and an independently completed sweep. The ISS-084 reviewer5 report remains
malformed because of invalid pair codes and an oversized finding; its DELTA
label neither causes nor cures that malformed classification.

## G0 and smallest complete shape

G0: PASS. One closed `dogfood-source-review-authority/v1` record, validated by
the repository-policy adapter before reviewer dispatch, plus a v2 selection
binding is the smallest full correction. Existing repair handoff metadata is
equivalent only inside repair and cannot authorize a corrective source stage.
Extending recovery authority alone is insufficient because original dispatch
and queue classification would still select scope separately. Legacy sources
without the new authority retain COMPLETE behavior; unbound DELTA fails closed.

## Contract profile and all twelve pairs

Profile: `contract` under `quality-contract/v2`.

- SCOPE [High] - one scope/inheritance join feeds all existing adapters; no scheduler, launcher or framework is added.
- ROBUSTNESS [High] - exact run, fingerprint, author, candidate, base and inherited sweep reject substitution and stale ancestry.
- DEPTH [High] - repository policy validates authority while flow persists the returned opaque adapter contract.
- READABILITY [Med] - COMPLETE compatibility and explicit DELTA are named types with one selection path.
- TESTS [High] - regressions distinguish scope substitution, stale inheritance, malformed, incomplete, negative, selection and restart.
- OBSERVABILITY [High] - bounded typed refusals remain blocking while usage telemetry stays advisory.
- SECURITY [High] - scope comes only from external authority, never the report being validated.
- PERFORMANCE [Low] - bounded immutable JSON reads replace no unbounded operation.
- ROLLOUT [High] - v1 legacy bindings and immutable configurations remain valid without rewrite.
- CONSISTENCY [High] - write-once intent, terminal and binding conventions are preserved.
- EXPERIENCE [Low] - existing COMPLETE callers need no new configuration.
- LANGUAGE [High] - scope, inheritance, completed sweep, comparison base and selection retain distinct meanings.

## Non-goals

No report normalization, valid-negative replacement, repeated sweep, source
mutation for verdict, receipt rewrite, scheduler, reviewer launcher, recovery
framework, public service, arbitrary evidence-file access, budget reset,
provider mutation, candidate certification or self-promotion.

## Acceptance evidence

1. External authority binds COMPLETE/DELTA and the adopting executor to exact source identity; DELTA also binds a completed independent sweep and comparison ancestry, with malformed or conflicting authority rejected before reviewer launch.
2. Original classification, recovery eligibility and prompt, replacement validation, selected binding, queue acceptance and repair handoff consume that contract; valid negative, incomplete and malformed remain distinct.
3. A genuinely malformed scope-bound DELTA can use the existing single replacement transition with a fresh reviewer on the unchanged head while original IDs, outcomes, usage and inherited sweep remain immutable.
4. Reviewer and recovery intents plus the v2 selected binding persist scope/inheritance, so interrupted launch and completed restart cannot drift scope, relaunch or duplicate effects. Adoption is an external authority record beside old state; old config and receipts are never rewritten.
5. Exact-head independent COMPLETE review, four controller gates and hosted Node 24 Windows/macOS/Linux remain mandatory; compatibility regressions preserve all prior suites and assertions.
6. Only after external activation may a still-applicable real pending malformed DELTA be recovered and restarted; fixtures and standalone receipts do not close ISS-084 or the broader queue goal.

## Verification

Author uses only installed TypeScript `--noEmit` and pure Prettier advisory
checks. The controller runs typecheck, format check, planning check and planning
board check, then exact-head hosted Node 24 on Windows, macOS and Linux after an
independent COMPLETE review. No local test runner, build, native/Git fixture or
candidate-controlled inventory execution is evidence.

## Predicted footprint and review attack surface

Review policy, recovery adapter, queue/flow and repair-policy interfaces;
focused dogfood tests and planning registration. Attack scope selection from
the report, absent/unrelated inheritance, downstream COMPLETE literals,
intent/binding drift, old-state rewrite, classification collapse, participant
substitution, usage loss, duplicate launch and claims of real recovery from a
constructed receipt.
