## Why

ISS-123 made parking the default: `isRunStopReason` in
`scripts/dogfood/supervision.ts` names five run reasons and every other stop
parks the selected issue and continues. Review of the landed change on
2026-09-12 found three consequences.

An environmental failure such as `unstable-executor`,
`controller-executor-mismatch`, `incompatible-git`,
`dirty-controller-executor`, `hosted-observation-unavailable` or
`selected-base-unavailable` would park every runnable issue in turn, each with
a note that names nothing the person can change on that issue, and on Chase
Sets would label each one `status:needs-replan`.

A stop raised before a cycle is active, such as
`selected-issue-identity-drift` from `assertIssue` or
`malformed-repository-candidates`, posts nothing, is not a run reason, and the
supervisor reselects at once: an unbounded tight loop against the GitHub API.

A typed adapter error raised during queue setup, such as
`missing-chase-sets-delivery-skill` from `issueContext`, surfaces as
`queue-internal-error` because `DeliveryBlocked` is not a `QueueBlocked`.

## Done when

- `isRunStopReason` is replaced by an explicit item list. A stop parks the
  issue only when the worker or delivery pipeline produced the reason for
  that candidate: `implementation-attempt-ceiling-exhausted`,
  `gate-retry-exhausted:*`, `reviewer-malformed`, `exit-receipt-timeout`,
  `launcher-failed`, `rebase-conflict`, `hosted-check-failed:*`,
  `hosted-check-log-unavailable:*`, `deploy-not-verified` and
  `source-finding-location-outside-candidate`. Every other reason posts its
  note without parking and exits the supervisor, as a run stop does today.
- A stop raised before a cycle is active exits the supervisor with the reason
  on stderr. The supervisor never reselects after a stop in the same
  invocation unless it parked the issue.
- Errors thrown by an adapter's `selectCandidates`, `issueContext`,
  `branchName` or `park` keep their reason on stderr and in the note. The
  simplest shape is `loadRepositoryAdapter` wrapping those calls so any error
  with a `reason` becomes a `QueueBlocked` with that reason.
- Rule 7 in `docs/loop.md` says a stop caused by the work parks the issue and
  a stop caused by the host or executor exits, each with its note.
- `test/dogfood/supervision.test.ts` proves with a fake adapter that an
  environmental reason exits without parking and without touching another
  issue, that a pre-cycle failure exits after one selection, and that an
  adapter reason survives.

## Out of scope

Automatic retry of environmental failures. Changing which reasons are
actionable in the recovery table.
