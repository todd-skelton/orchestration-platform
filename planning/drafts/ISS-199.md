## Decision

Hunk-only conflict resolution cannot make necessary unmarked preservation edits, while three unequal blobs do not establish a defect. The simplest alternative is to record overlap without changing edit permission, which leaves the demonstrated blocker intact. Choose a seed-bound census in existing refresh data and permit necessary U edits for ordinary unfenced resolution, retaining marked-hunk invariance for K and every separately ruled fence. Accept independent semantic review of those U edits and the bounded census's missed incompatibilities rather than promising a semantic-futility detector. Evidence that preservation needs paths outside this deterministic set requires separate planning, not automatic scope widening, a new stop reason or renewed attempt authority.
