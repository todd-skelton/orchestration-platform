## Decision

Selected issue reads must tolerate short classified transport loss without gaining mutation or recovery authority.
Keeping them single-shot is the simplest alternative but repeats the demonstrated host-stop boundary even when the next read could succeed.
Choose to remove only the learning-note restriction from the existing owning observation retry, retaining its transport classifier, three total attempts and 1-second then 2-second pauses for both read purposes.
Accept up to two extra read calls and three seconds of scheduled waiting per observation, excluding unchanged command duration, and the existing non-parking stop when transport remains unavailable.
The choice is wrong if a non-transport failure retries, a write repeats, an authoritative refusal is bypassed, or retries change retained history or stop semantics.
