## Why

This is the M2 exit evidence: the loop delivers real Chase Sets work while the
incumbent controller is still available as the fallback.

## Done when

- One low-risk Chase Sets milestone, chosen by Todd, is delivered end to end
  through the loop with the Chase Sets adapter: selection, author, review,
  landing through the merge queue, and deploy verification.
- The incumbent controller was not used for those issues; any manual step is
  recorded as a learning note and becomes a new issue.
- Run records are retained and linked from this issue.

## Out of scope

Routine delivery of all Chase Sets work (ISS-111).
