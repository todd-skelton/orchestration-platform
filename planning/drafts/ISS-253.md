The simplest alternative is a transient category in the thrown error, but that is insufficient unless the ordinary stop persists it.
Choose the existing safe GitHub command classifier and DeliveryBlocked diagnostic channel, retained in the existing bounded stop body and bound through native run, publication and merge-intent records, without a new record schema or reader.

  unknown, confirmed merge, completed cleanup and routine repeated entry;
  a retained success is inert, not another request.
