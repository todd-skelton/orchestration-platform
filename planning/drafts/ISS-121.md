## Why

The third ISS-106 run selected ISS-114
[#379](https://github.com/todd-skelton/orchestration-platform/issues/379),
completed its author and independent review, and wrote the repair intent, then
stopped before launching a repair worker with `malformed-acceptance-criteria`;
the stop is recorded on
[#379](https://github.com/todd-skelton/orchestration-platform/issues/379#issuecomment-5636585163).
The queue reads the Done-when section from the registered repository draft as
one verbatim Markdown string. A composed probe produced one 984-character
criterion containing newlines, which the repair policy rejected because its
generic bounded-string check forbids control characters. This is one
controller rejecting its own trusted output, contrary to rule 9.

## Done when

- The `acceptanceCriteria` bounded-string validation is deleted from
  `validateRepairConfig`; other repair-policy validation is unchanged.
- The complete multiline Done-when section produced by the queue survives
  losslessly in the existing encoded repair author prompt; decoding it yields
  the original Markdown and newlines.
- A regression derives the queue from a registered multiline draft, follows
  the genuine failed-review repair path through the repository adapter, and
  observes the repair prompt; it does not substitute a hand-built repair
  config or criteria fixture.
- No parser, replacement limit, sanitization, schema, record, artifact, guard,
  or framework is added. Author, reviewer, worktree, and private-temp
  boundaries are unchanged.

## Out of scope

The ISS-114 attempt-phase restart finding remains part of ISS-114. Other
repair-policy reduction belongs to ISS-116. Changing retries or editing and
recovering the retained ISS-106 run is also out of scope.
