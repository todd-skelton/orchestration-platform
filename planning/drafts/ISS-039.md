## Context and evidence

The incumbent controller discards confirmed over-engineering observed at review
time: its review contract blocks only on correctness/security and its
non-blocking finding count has no consumer. The first consumer has registered
the fix as review-contract/v3
(chase-sets issue 6999, backed by a 2026-08-17 thirty-PR audit: 13 clean, 10
minor, 7 significant, with implementer-side excess small but real). The portable
review module must ship the channel from the start rather than inherit the gap.
Engine receipts already carry an opaque module disposition payload (`ISS-009`);
module packaging is `ISS-011`; consumer mutations travel as adapter plans
(`ISS-013`).

## Scope fence

In: a simplification-finding record inside the review module's disposition
payload — stable S-IDs, a closed five-kind marker set (single-caller
abstraction, surface unrequired by acceptance evidence, guard for an
already-excluded state, rebuild of named prior art, excess indirection), an
executed deletion-test probe, and a bounded remedy; repair-module briefs that
require one disposition per S-finding; accepted-result routing that emits one
idempotent follow-up work-item adapter plan; and round-accounting exclusion.

Out: any change to engine receipt schemas or blocking authority, consumer
rubric wording, retroactive reinterpretation of historical receipts, and
planning-side proportionality gates (owned by planning-module standards).

## Decisions already made

Simplification findings are advisory and never enter disposition or
repair/attempt ceiling accounting; the evidence bar is the executed deletion
test, and taste-level findings are invalid by construction. Review authority is
exact-target, so an accepted result is never edited after acceptance: findings
route forward as new work, mirroring the first consumer's registered
review-contract/v3 design.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. The review module's disposition payload defines simplification findings with
   stable S-IDs, one of the five closed marker kinds, executed probe evidence,
   and a bounded remedy; malformed or taste-only findings refuse. Evidence:
   schema fixtures with per-field negative controls.
2. A nonzero simplification count coexists with an accepted engine receipt and
   changes no disposition, ceiling, or round accounting. Evidence: reducer
   fixtures pairing accepted receipts with nonzero counts and unchanged
   accounting.
3. A repair brief for a rejected result requires exactly one disposition per
   S-finding — applied or disputed-with-reason — and refuses completion on any
   omission. Evidence: repair-transcript fixtures with omission mutants.
4. An accepted result with nonzero findings yields exactly one follow-up
   work-item adapter plan bound to the reviewed subject, idempotent across
   re-runs, with the accepted subject itself never re-opened or mutated.
   Evidence: dry-run/apply plan transcripts and a subject-mutation canary.
