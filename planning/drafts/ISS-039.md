## Context and evidence

The incumbent controller discards confirmed over-engineering observed during
review: its blocking verdict remains authoritative, but its advisory finding
count has no durable consumer. Chase Sets issue `#6999` registered the missing
channel after a 2026-08-17 thirty-PR audit found 13 clean, 10 minor, and 7
significant examples. The portable review and repair modules must carry the
advisory result without importing Chase Sets policy or widening engine authority.
Todd's later Option B decision in Chase Sets decision `#7007`, comment
`5321438227` at `2026-08-17T23:29:22Z`, retains `#6999` as the consumer-policy
and full review-v3 cutover owner. This issue mirrors only the generic reusable
module contract for that first consumer; it neither supersedes nor duplicates
the Chase execution work.

## Scope fence

In: a simplification-finding record in the review module's existing disposition
payload; stable S-IDs; a closed marker-kind set; an executed deletion test or
strictly smaller alternative; bounded remedies; exactly one applied or disputed
repair answer per finding on rejected results; and one idempotent follow-up
adapter plan for accepted results.

Out: engine receipt or PASS/BLOCK semantics, attempt-round accounting, consumer
rubric wording, reopening or mutating an accepted subject, planning-side
proportionality standards (`ISS-040`), package/runtime/contracts changes, and
adapter implementations. The Chase-specific review-v3 cutover and tech-debt
filing policy remain exclusively in `chase-sets/chase-sets#6999`.

## Decisions already made

Simplification findings are advisory only. Their presence and count never alter
PASS/BLOCK, repair eligibility, breaker state, or attempt-round accounting.
Rejected-result repair answers every finding exactly once as `APPLIED` or
`DISPUTED` with a reason. An accepted result remains immutable and routes all
of its findings through exactly one subject-bound, idempotent follow-up adapter
plan; reruns reproduce the same plan identity and never create another item.

The marker kinds are closed: single-caller abstraction, acceptance-unrequired
surface, guard for an excluded state, rebuild of named prior art, and excess
indirection. Taste alone is not evidence.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. The review-module payload defines stable S-IDs, one closed marker kind, the
   deletion-test or smaller-alternative evidence, and a bounded remedy; missing,
   duplicate, unknown-kind, or taste-only records refuse. Evidence: schema
   fixtures with one negative control per field and marker kind.
2. PASS and BLOCK fixtures with zero and nonzero findings retain their original
   disposition, breaker effect, repair eligibility, and attempt-round totals.
   Evidence: paired reducer snapshots differing only in advisory findings.
3. A rejected-result repair transcript answers every S-ID exactly once as
   `APPLIED` or `DISPUTED` with a non-empty reason; omission, duplication, or an
   answer for an unknown S-ID refuses completion. Evidence: transcript mutants.
4. An accepted result with findings emits exactly one follow-up adapter plan
   bound to the reviewed subject and complete finding set. Repeated reduction
   emits the same plan identity, performs no duplicate apply, and never reopens
   or mutates the accepted subject. Evidence: repeat dry-run/apply transcripts,
   destination census, and a subject-mutation canary.
