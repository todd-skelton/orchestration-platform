## Done when

1. **AC1: a planning operation, not implementation authority.** A classified
   brief/slice/design failure can enter ISSUE/SET/EPIC planning respectively
   through the queue/supervisor, with exact failed artifacts, findings, current
   main, decisions and complete lineage/sibling/epic context. The worker may
   edit only proposed drafts in its isolated planning workspace, never code,
   tests, runtime, configuration, GitHub or the executing checkout. Reuse
   native author/reviewer launch and observation with explicit planning purpose;
   do not disguise planning output as accepted implementation or run product gates.
   Terminal malformed/dead/refused planning follows classified recovery,
   preserving its partial and charged native history, not a silent drop.
2. **AC2: finite planning-repair/v1 output.** Require REPAIR_IN_PLACE only for
   outcome/decision/acceptance-preserving precision; otherwise REPLACED with
   fixed-scope successors, or RECOMMEND_NOT_COMPLETING with evidence and no
   closure. Every proposed brief has acceptance, G0, explicit not-built reasons,
   native dependency intent, current-main don't-rebuild evidence including
   siblings, overlapping defect constraints and branch plus exact-head salvage
   with forbidden-reuse instructions. Genuine new requirements go to a new
   slice, never into the original as a purported precision repair.
3. **AC3: changed work hypothesis.** For each stopped lineage require exactly
   one ordered `### recoveryHypothesis` section: `lastFailure:`,
   `priorHypotheses:`, `newHypothesis:`, `level:` with nonempty payloads.
   ISSUE covers the brief; SET names all siblings and answers their findings;
   EPIC re-derives the approach or one bounded design spike. The reviewer
   judges whether what the work does differs from every prior hypothesis.
   Model/effort/time/willingness/new wording alone fails; report the next
   higher level rather than re-entering at the same level. At EPIC, an actual
   accepted-product-scope conflict becomes one product question, not invented scope.
4. **AC4: independent artifact-bound planning review.** The loop launches one
   distinct eligible reviewer after proposal completion and binds its verdict,
   findings and G0 to all exact proposal body hashes and dependencies plus
   native dispatch/terminal identities. Record all author and in-body repair
   participation. Prefer full-history independence, including unused platform
   author ladder rungs; exhaustion selects the best admitted reviewer that did
   not author/repair these exact bytes, disclosing earlier participation.
   No model/effort rename cleans history and no self-review is possible.
   No available admitted distinct reviewer means a named availability blocker,
   never parking or an unrecorded CLI fallback. FAIL returns prescribed repairs
   or higher-level planning; PASS is planning authority only, not code acceptance.
5. **AC5: narrow, replayable handoff.** Retain pending author, proposal,
   pending reviewer, rejected and accepted states in ISS-236's recovery record.
   Resume existing native work, apply no external writes in this slice and
   expose accepted proposals to ISS-238. A completed proposal without an exact
   independent PASS cannot proceed. A changed body invalidates that PASS.
   Production-path fixtures cover both adapters, all three levels, bad/missing
   hypotheses, changing dependencies, stale reviews, reviewer exhaustion and
   repeated post-PASS reads. A bypass of exact-body or self-review validation
   must fail its dedicated one-variable negative test.
