## Context and evidence

After shadow parity, authority must transfer once, at a bounded safe point, with
no interval where both controllers can mutate or neither can recover. See the
terminal shadow report from `ISS-017` and promotion trust model.

## Scope fence

In: cutover plan, incumbent quiescence, fresh export plus deterministic
plan/apply/verify/commit of the `ISS-024` import implementation inside the same
transaction, adapter installation, single-writer authority transfer, first live
cycle, post-cutover observation, abort-before-consumer-mutation, and forward-
recovery-after-consumer-mutation drills.

Out: additional consumers, unrelated policy changes, feature work, and deleting
incumbent history or recovery artifacts.

## Decisions already made

Before the new controller's first mutation, abort may restore incumbent
authority. After first mutation, recovery continues the reviewed successor
state; transitions follow the `Consumer cutover` table. Preflight authority
health includes only rows marked pre-cutover in the timing matrix plus current
release, adapter, credential-census, quiescence, and single-writer receipts.
After transfer, the incumbent remains the read-only comparator defined there.
The import implementation and fixture-proven mapping come from `ISS-024`, but
no earlier export or committed receipt is reusable. `ISS-018` captures a fresh
export after its one quiescence fence, creates and commits one exact import
transaction, and transfers authority without allowing the incumbent to resume
between them. An abort requires a future fresh export/import transaction;
successor authority cannot activate before this transaction is
`IMPORT_COMMITTED`.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. Preflight proves incumbent quiescence, current shadow parity, compatible
   state, healthy external authorities, and one prospective writer. Evidence:
   exact cutover receipt.
2. An executed pre-mutation abort drill restores the incumbent without consumer
   mutation. Evidence: state and authority read-back.
3. An executed post-mutation interruption drill resumes the new controller
   without dual writes or lost terminal events. Evidence: joined journals and
   external read-back.
4. The credential-fenced read-only incumbent comparator correlates seven
   consecutive post-cutover UTC days and at least 100 authoritative decisions
   with zero unexplained differences, no stale incumbent mutation ownership,
   and no recovery-required state. Evidence: correlation report, credential
   census, and next-cycle receipts.
5. Comparator retirement reaches `STOPPED`: the terminal report is durable,
   every comparator credential is revoked, the exact process tree is exited,
   and the successor completes its next routine cycle alone. Evidence: complete
   retirement transition receipts and negative fixtures for each missing step.
6. The successful live transcript has one uninterrupted quiescence fence, one
   export digest, one import transaction, and one authority transfer. Movement
   after export, resuming the incumbent before transfer, or supplying an
   `ISS-024` fixture/prior-run receipt refuses. Evidence: joined timeline and
   source-movement/receipt-reuse mutants.
