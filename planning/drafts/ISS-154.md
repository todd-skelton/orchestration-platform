## Why

Chase Sets M2 run `m2-purchase-limit-20260915T0132` parked #7844 after four
implementation attempts. The terminal record is Chase Sets comment
`5673723725`; its final candidate is
`25b602832b8d33768c6e1aab1b611c48ad4d3f21`. An accepted planning repair and
independent planning PASS are recorded in comments `5674420745` and its linked
receipt, but they deliberately granted no fifth attempt.

The operator then ran the candidate's complete Ordering workspace against real
PostgreSQL through controller v2.71. Comment `5678264608` records 386 executed
tests with zero skips: 384 passed and only the day +0/+1
`purchase-limit-source-retry-concurrency` cases failed. Both failures reach
`purchase-limits.db.test.ts:340` and call the production event store with the
unsupported `fromVersion: 0` before the AC5 cancellation proof executes.

Todd's closed ruling, Chase Sets #4388 comment `5681842811`, raises only the
absolute `cs-7844` lineage ceiling to five. It permits one final platform-owned
Astra/high correction limited to those two reads, requires exact-head real
PostgreSQL zero-skip evidence before the fresh Sol/high review, and parks on any
non-PASS with no attempt six. It is not a count reset, a new-run budget, an
ownership transfer, or a general ceiling change.

The stable executor cannot encode that authority. `LoopConfig.acceptedReplan`
is a singleton string whose validation, prior runtime paths, issue, candidate,
publication, prompt, run, slug, and fifth-attempt identity are all hard-coded to
#7766. Ordinary config rejects attempt ceilings above four. Adding a second
hard-coded constant would repeat the defect and still could not represent an
unpublished candidate or the required operator evidence boundary. This issue
adds the one closed generic continuation needed by the recorded M2 recovery.

## Done when

- **AC1: Replace the singleton with one closed continuation packet.** A
  versioned accepted-replan config binds the repository, issue key and URL,
  prior run and exact attempt-record directory, prior absolute attempt, exact
  candidate head, target run, next absolute attempt, absolute ceiling,
  authority URL, allowed source paths, and any required pre-review evidence
  descriptor. That descriptor names the accepted receipt schema, exact
  workspace/gate/command identity, evidence-bundle paths, expected complete
  suite file/test counts, zero skips, and required case identities. Exact-key,
  type, and path validation happens before setup or worker launch. The packet
  is data, not an issue-specific branch in queue or supervision code. Evidence:
  config/queue tests use at least two different issue and run identities and
  contain no production `cs-7766` or `cs-7844` constant in the generic
  implementation.

- **AC2: Preserve lineage without resetting attempts.** Composition reads the
  named prior attempt record, requires its issue, repository, absolute attempt,
  head, terminal phase, and accumulated participant history to match the
  packet, and requires
  `nextAbsoluteAttempt === absoluteCeiling === priorAbsoluteAttempt + 1`.
  A fresh run name grants nothing by itself. Missing, malformed, mismatched,
  nonterminal, wrong-head, skipped-attempt, renewable-ceiling, or
  already-consumed packets refuse before a worker launch. Replay resumes the
  same continuation and never creates another attempt. Evidence: queue and
  supervision restart tests with exact one-variable negatives.

- **AC3: Support the two recorded candidate shapes.** A prior published
  candidate retains its publication/branch and forward-refresh behavior when
  the packet names that binding. A prior unpublished candidate retains its
  exact commit and starts the authorized continuation without inventing a PR,
  publication receipt, old PASS, or delivery authority. Both carry the prior
  findings and evidence locations into the current author and reviewer
  context. Evidence: separate published and unpublished fixtures through the
  real queue composition path.

- **AC4: Enforce the ruled correction boundary.** The continuation limits the
  implementation delta to the packet's normalized repository-relative
  `allowedPaths`; empty, absolute, parent-traversing, duplicate, widened, or
  out-of-scope paths refuse. The #7844 packet can name only
  `bounded-contexts/ordering/features/orders/api/purchase-limits.db.test.ts`.
  This mechanism does not authorize a product-contract redesign, unrelated
  source edit, incumbent implementation, or attempt beyond the packet.
  Evidence: changed-path gate positives and sibling/untracked/rename negatives.

- **AC5: Pause for required exact-head evidence before review.** When the
  packet declares a pre-review host gate, author PASS records the candidate and
  yields a typed operator-evidence-required stop without launching the
  reviewer, parking the issue, or consuming another implementation attempt.
  Absent evidence remains that same non-parking stop. Structurally malformed,
  wrong-head, wrong-command, duplicate, or contradictory material yields a
  typed evidence-authority stop with no reviewer and grants no new author or
  candidate. A present identity-valid receipt proving nonzero exit, nonzero
  skips, wrong required counts, or missing required case execution is the
  final work/gate failure: it parks at the absolute ceiling without review or
  another continuation and cannot later be replaced by PASS for that
  candidate.

  A successful resume atomically retains the receipt, run metadata, preflight
  log, and raw verifier log under the continuation runtime before reviewer
  launch. One versioned acceptance record binds their SHA-256 digests and the
  parsed repository, immutable head, workspace/gate/command, exit, exact
  file/test/skip counts, and required day +0/+1 identities. Validation and the
  reviewer prompt consume only that retained snapshot; later external-file
  mutation or removal changes nothing. Exactly one fresh reviewer then follows
  the ordinary lifecycle. Evidence: all three evidence classes, immutable
  snapshot replay, exit-1-not-replaceable, and reviewer-before-evidence
  negatives.

- **AC6: Retain ordinary and historical behavior.** Loops without a
  continuation still cap at four and preserve all current selection, repair,
  review, local gate, publication, hosted check, merge, deploy, park, and idle
  behavior. The former #7766 accepted-replan fixture is expressed through the
  generic packet and remains bounded to one attempt; preserved historical run
  files need no migration. The final continuation cannot enter ordinary
  `fixable-review` repair, ISS-152 gate correction, `advancing-attempt`, or a
  chained accepted-replan path. Any source, review, or gate non-PASS records
  terminal failure and ordinary parking; bounded provider/host observation may
  resume only the same in-flight step and cannot mint another semantic author.
  Evidence: existing queue, supervision, refresh, and delivery suites plus the
  converted #7766 regression and explicit no-attempt-six/no-second-repair
  assertions.

## Decisions and operator actions

Todd resolved the only attempt-authority decision in Chase Sets #4388 comment
`5681842811`: absolute ceiling five, exactly one final same-lineage attempt,
and terminal park on any non-PASS. The simplest implementation is one closed
packet consumed by existing queue/supervision seams, not a registry or another
issue-specific branch.

After this issue lands with three-OS green, the operator advances the stable
executor only while both supervisors are absent. M2 remains stopped until the
operator writes the exact ruled continuation config. After author PASS, the
operator runs the retained PostgreSQL verification through the installed Chase
Sets host verifier and resumes only with its exact-head successful receipt.

## External authority

The authority inputs are Todd's immutable ruling URL and the operator-hosted
Chase Sets verifier receipt. The platform verifies the closed fields it
consumes; it does not reinterpret a comment, historical PASS, or failed run as
authority. No provider or production data is involved.

## Review packet

Review should attack run-name budget renewal, skipped absolute attempts,
borrowed prior history, path traversal or widening, published/unpublished
conflation, reviewer launch before host proof, failed or wrong-head evidence,
replay duplication, a hidden second continuation, and any production
issue-specific constant. The omission-revealing proof uses separate
unpublished attempt-4 fixtures: absent or authority-invalid material keeps the
non-parking evidence stop without a reviewer; an identity-valid failing receipt
parks terminally and cannot be replaced; a successful exact-head receipt is
snapshotted once and resumes exactly once into review.
