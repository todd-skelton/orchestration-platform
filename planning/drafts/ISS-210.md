## Decision

The complete Windows suite and existing merge guarantee must survive a shorter critical path.
Keeping one serial runner preserves coverage but retains the measured 41-to-58-minute wait, while two runners have an estimated ideal 28.83-minute test-body floor before setup.
Choose three standard hosted Windows runners for refresh, queue and the canonically discovered remainder, with one unchanged required Windows aggregate.
Accept extra setup minutes and concurrent capacity consumption without claiming a cost reduction.
The choice is wrong if equivalent full execution cannot meet the three-run 30-minute target or preserve useful failure diagnostics within this footprint.
