## Why

Since ISS-104 the loop derives every setup, queue, repair and delivery
"authority" record from `loop.json` and then validates each record against the
fields it was built from. The loop is the only producer, so each check proves
an object equals itself. Rule 9 in `docs/loop.md` says that is a finding.
Roughly 350 lines in `scripts/dogfood/queue.ts` plus matching validators in
`setup.ts`, `repair-policy.ts` and `delivery.ts` exist only for this.

## Done when

- `QueueAuthority`, `itemAuthority`, `participantIdentity` digests,
  `lineageDigest`, `itemsDigest`, `queueFingerprint`, the `queue-config` and
  `queue-adapter-authority` records, `setup.authority`, `repair.authority`
  with its `historyDigest` and `configFingerprint`, and `delivery.authority`
  are deleted from `queue.ts`.
- The validators that consumed them are deleted: the authority branches of
  `validateQueueConfig`, `setup.ts` authority validation, `repair-policy.ts`
  `validateAuthority`, and `delivery.ts` authority validation.
- `validateQueueConfig` and `validateLoopExecutor` each run once per cycle,
  not five and three times.
- The `assertItem` drift re-check in the repository adapter is deleted; item
  drift is checked once when the config is built.
- Workers still never push, publish, merge, or edit the running loop (rule 6):
  the sandbox flags and the executor pin are unchanged.
- Tests that asserted authority shapes are deleted; tests of behavior stay
  green. Net source and test lines go down and the PR body states by how much.

## Out of scope

Changing the record layout (ISS-114). Changing retry (ISS-115).
