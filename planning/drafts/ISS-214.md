## Decision

The immutable original-source review and accepted refresh review differ while
the spent three-launch reservation cannot be rewritten. The simplest viable
alternative is to use the native queue, `refresh.ts` selected directory and
PASS parsing, and `delivery-adapter.ts` source verification rather than a generic
recovery or budget system. Choose separate original provenance and current
refresh authority with one newly authorized reviewer-only launch for this
recorded lineage. This accepts one new grant-consumption record and a narrow
lineage check while preserving the old reservation. The choice is wrong if the
recorded two-hop refresh cannot re-enter without new authority or if replay
admits another launch.

The don't-rebuild check retains the distinct existing surfaces:
ISS-142 already accepts the producer's `retryContext` in reviewed source records
but does not handle a changed review identity. ISS-167 supplies same-attempt
integration continuation; ISS-199 supplies the seed-bound K/U census and fences;
ISS-200 supplies the spent-resolution reservation and three-launch accounting.
ISS-172's G0 publication text and existing PR-body reconciliation remain intact.
ISS-157's `gateStopAuthorization` and `blockedCycleResume` grant gate-stop and
blocked-cycle resume authority, not a reservation-bound reviewer launch.
None supplies post-publication refresh-review re-entry or this one additional
authority-bound launch. Adjacent lineage is not #457 completion.
