## Context and evidence

Final OS certification proves conformance, not semantic acceptability. A
distinct reviewer must examine the exact candidate before any production
credential references are bound or a human grant is requested.
The current advisory `ISS-043` reviewer-calibration report is review-packet
context; it neither grants authority nor replaces exact-candidate review.

## Scope fence

In: execute the certified pinned `bootstrap-review.yml` on the declared review
host/runner; isolated review-role Codex home; exact candidate/review packet;
accepted/rejected receipt; OIDC and host-custody attestation; negative controls.

Out: source changes, credential-set binding, protected-environment approval,
installation, promotion, or treating the reviewer as operator/author.

## Decisions already made

The certification workflow triggers the pinned review workflow by numeric
run/artifact identity. Its self-hosted review runner is bound by `ISS-035`; the
reviewer role and tool surface are those passed by `ISS-023`, with no
implementation, code-host mutation, install, grant, or promotion capability.
The workflow emits `bootstrap-review-receipt/v1` binding candidate/tree/artifact,
certification, workflow/run/attempt, reviewer role/identity, exact prompt/tool/
host evidence, findings, and accepted/rejected authority.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. A distinct authenticated reviewer examines the exact certified candidate and
   complete source/package/test/installer attack surface; accepted or rejected
   authority binds one immutable worker-result/candidate digest. Evidence:
   attested review receipt and raw-to-normalized subject map.
2. Author/build/aggregate/reviewer/operator identities remain distinct by
   numeric/host/credential identity. Evidence: identity matrix and alias canary.
3. Moved candidate, missing certification, altered review workflow/prompt/tool,
   warm/shared home, self-review, omitted package, reused attempt, or one-byte
   substitution refuses or rejects. Evidence: one executed mutant each.
4. The result artifact is OIDC-attested by the pinned workflow and signed by the
   admitted host-custody key; rejection prevents authorization dispatch.

## Verification plan

## Predicted footprint and dependencies

Creates no tracked source. Owns only the exact review execution and retained
attested receipt. Workflow/prompt/schema bytes were frozen in `ISS-020` and
certified in `ISS-019`. Blocks credential binding and authorization/install.

## Operator actions

Make the isolated review-role Codex home available on the declared runner and
observe the run; do not approve, edit, or reinterpret its result.

## Glossary impact

None; independent review and exact subject terms are registered.

## Independent review packet seed

Attack candidate/workflow movement, identity collapse, hidden prompt/config,
review credential crossover, unsigned local results, result reinterpretation,
and authorization after rejection. Inspect the current `ISS-043` calibration
report as context and verify it is neither omitted nor treated as authority.
