## Context and evidence

`ISS-002` defines pure pointer/history/run contracts; `ISS-022` must prove the
kernel exclusion and custody premise. Receipts do not authenticate writers.
Production authority is the selected epoch, canonical custody, a revocable
in-process capability, and the fixed OS lock. The proportionality review
(pressure round 34) removed the sparse trees, node-inventory census, and
materialization coordinator this slice formerly implemented; authority history
is now one hash-chained append-only log.

## Scope fence

In: fixed `installation/state-mutation.lock`, singleton in-process state service,
private WeakMap/nonces and purpose-scoped RPC handles, eleven-kind registry,
generic value/proposal/conflict/tip/tombstone/archive storage,
run-current META_LEAF/checkpoints, linear authority-history appends and
full-chain verification, deterministic mutation/reconciliation, authority
rotation, exact-byte CAS/readback, crash recovery, and canonical
unsupported/unknown results.

Out: record schema ownership, process policy, broker/release production,
distributed/network locks, leases/PIDs/timeouts as authority, and any fallback
not selected by a new replan.

## Decisions already made

The exact lock/RPC sequences, eleven pointer kinds, Dv/Dr/Dt graph, linear
authority-history chain, generic META_LEAF storage, single-epoch commit runs,
packet purposes, proposal classification, canonical paths, tombstones, and
epoch crash semantics in `supervisor-contract.md` as amended by
`contract-decisions.md` are mandatory. The old capability performs rotation,
appending the chain record and then performing the authority CAS as its final
action; its run-current journal legitimately rests at CAS-armed. All old
handles revoke at selection, and each fresh new-epoch run, until the rotation
run's terminal resolution is selected, performs its lock-held full chain walk
and, on success, writes that terminal resolution before any other mutation as
an idempotent create-once write. Bootstrap destination owner,
anchor, genesis, and reinstall are external ISS-020 operations.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. Cross-process races choose one selected tip and classify every competing
   proposal from actual evidence; same mutation+bytes retries idempotently and
   same instance/different bytes refuses. Barrier-controlled tests cover every
   mutation boundary and source/path collision.
2. Historical and mutation callbacks have different runtime handle classes;
   copied/serialized/proxied/foreign/stale handles refuse. Every callback
   revokes on completion, release, crash, custody movement, or rotation.
3. Ordinary commits select acyclic run-current checkpoint cores before/after
   target CAS, validate authority/custody, and terminalize exactly. Every fault
   boundary yields selected prior/successor or explicit recoverable ambiguity;
   no syscall outcome is guessed.
4. Rotation under the old capability proves the new reviewed active release and
   helper/profile/ABI/custody, resolves every pending proposal among the other
   ten kinds, requires zero unrelated PENDING/UNKNOWN, appends the exact
   chain record binding predecessor digest and next safe-integer ordinal, and
   performs the authority CAS as its final action with verified readback,
   resting at CAS-armed while old handles revoke at selection. Each fresh
   new-epoch run, until the rotation run's terminal resolution is selected,
   performs the lock-held full chain walk, verifies the selected head, and
   idempotently create-once writes that terminal resolution before any other
   mutation; the successor run recomputes the rotation target and mutation
   identity from the selected successor authority value and the head chain
   record and constructs the rotation run's journal path without
   enumeration. A seeded wrong-path or wrong-derivation mutant refuses, and
   a crash after the walk but before the terminal-resolution write resumes
   on the next fresh run. A crash between append and CAS resumes only
   via the same transaction under the old capability re-driving the same
   CAS; the pending record is the sole permitted head-plus-one excess.
   Rotation-v-target races have one linear order; candidate self-rotation,
   rollback, wrong custody, mixed epoch, chain fork, and ordinal gap refuse.
5. Reservation selection reuses one winner per predecessor triple, resolves UUID
   conflicts, and enforces RESERVED→CONSUMED→TERMINAL→TOMBSTONE with chained
   safe-integer ordinals that refuse above the bound. The CAS-selected
   reservation is durable before process creation and carries the descriptor
   inputs; the descriptor exists only as LIVE and binds it.
6. Terminal removal archives first and selects durable tombstone; crashes on
   every side of archive/tombstone preserve one authority. Bare absence after
   genesis and unauthorized archive selectors refuse.
7. Every record class is FULL_REQUIRED: no compaction path exists or ever
   touches the authority-history chain, loss of any required record is
   `UNKNOWN` and blocks mutation, and no degraded-audit mode exists.
8. Unsupported filesystems, lock helpers, or custody guarantees refuse before
   mutation; no PID, age, lease, timeout, symlink, unsigned lock, or silent
   fallback is accepted.
9. Chain verification walks every record from genesis while holding the lock,
   constructing each record's canonical ordinal-derived path with no directory
   enumeration: injected, missing, reordered, truncated, or digest-mutated
   records and a head whose ordinal or digest mismatches the walked result
   all refuse. A head-plus-one record is accepted only when its ordinal is
   head plus one, its predecessor digest equals the head record digest, and
   its rotation identity and successor authority facts equal the selected
   armed rotation intent, the CAS-armed run-current journal plus its
   create-once intent record; when no armed intent is selected, any
   head-plus-one file refuses, and head-plus-two is absent. Scale
   runs reference the ISS-006-published 1000-record golden and budget on all
   three OSes.
