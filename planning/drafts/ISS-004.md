## Context and evidence

Leases, journals, and promotion records require crash-safe behavior on three
different filesystem and locking models. See `docs/architecture/vision.md` and
the state contracts from `ISS-002`.

## Scope fence

In: atomic replace, create-once records, advisory/exclusive locks, compare-and-
swap, durable append, bounded read, crash recovery, and canonical error results.

Out: distributed locks, network filesystems, process ownership, or release
policy.

## Decisions already made

Correctness follows `docs/architecture/contract-decisions.md` and the `State
mutation` table in `docs/architecture/state-machines.md`. `ISS-022` selects a
Node-only or bounded native-helper prerequisite; required guarantees cannot be
weakened here.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. Concurrent writers yield one authorized winner with no torn readable state.
   Evidence: stress test plus deterministic barrier-controlled races.
2. Crash injection at every mutation boundary recovers to predecessor or
   successor, never a third state. Evidence: fault-injection state matrix.
3. Lock ownership cannot be borrowed by descendants or unrelated processes.
   Evidence: negative child-process tests on all OS targets.
4. Capability detection reports unsupported guarantees before mutation.
   Evidence: simulated unsupported-filesystem fixture.
