## Why

The reviewer must currently return a twelve-pair quality packet with weighted
verdict codes, bounded finding counts and cross-bound evidence rows, parsed by
about 180 lines in `scripts/dogfood/repair-policy.ts`. Most "malformed review"
stops are the reviewer failing that schema, not failing to review. A verdict,
a list of findings and one "is there a simpler way" answer is what the loop
acts on.

## Done when

- The reviewer report has `verdict` (PASS or FAIL), `findings` (each with
  `file`, `line`, `severity` of blocking or note, and `text`), and `g0`
  (a string), plus the existing run, role and head keys. The `outputSchema`
  in `dispatch-adapter.ts` enforces it.
- `parseReview` and `classifyReview` in `repair-policy.ts` validate only that
  shape. The twelve-pair tables, weight rules and profile checks are deleted.
- `sourceReviewerReportPrompt` and `reviewerReportPrompt` in
  `repair-adapter.ts` describe the new shape and the G0 question; the quality
  packet text is deleted.
- A blocking finding makes the verdict FAIL; notes never block.
- The PR body built by delivery no longer embeds a quality packet.
- Existing repair tests are updated to the new shape; no fixture keeps the old
  twelve-pair form.

## Out of scope

Retry on an unparsable report (ISS-103). Changing who reviews or the
independent-reviewer rule.
