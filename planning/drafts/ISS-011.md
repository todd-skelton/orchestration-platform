## Scope fence

Todd's ruling on Decision #275 chose option C and split this issue. What remains
here is the landed module-semantics half; acceptance items 4, 5, and the
row-dependent half of 7 moved to `ISS-055`.

In: the neutral `planning` and `review` modules at their exact static

runtime dispatch implementation. Also out, moved to `ISS-055`: the four-row
`modules/manifest.json` and its row grammar, the `delivery` and `repair`
entrypoints, emitted module artifacts and the host-neutral bundle layout, and
the row-dependent half of the registry generator. `modules/manifest.json`,
`modules/build/generate-registry.mjs`, and `modules/.generated/registry.ts` stay
byte-stable at their `ISS-000` placeholder bytes under this issue.
