## Decision

Separate completed deterministic evidence from timing qualification. Keeping
local census/mutant runs at 30 s repeats the failed attempts even without a
local full-suite gate. Instead, local evidence-only focused invocations use
`--testTimeout 180000`; they prove exact counts and assertion kills, never the
30 s bound. Vitest 4.1.10 is pinned at `package.json:27`; its CLI accepts this
numeric flag (checked with `pnpm exec vitest run --testTimeout 180000 --help`
and the installed `vitest/node` `parseCLI`, without executing tests).

The committed test and `vitest.config.ts:13-14` keep the unchanged 30,000 ms
test/hook limits. No source, script or hosted workflow carries the evidence
override. Only the required hosted checks on the final PR head qualify the
full suite and 30 s bound. A local timeout is inconclusive, not a defect or
evidence for or against qualification, regardless of hosted status. Missing
deterministic evidence remains missing; hosted green cannot substitute for it.

   the same final candidate tree record identical exact counts and per-source

   a timeout is not a completed census. Do not add a tolerance or time assertion.

   executor exactly once (8 children). Every other assertion, replay and

   exactly the removed validations. Compare complete before/after censuses with
   the same counter boundaries and CLI override (nine validations reduced to
   one implies 64 fewer children on the historical census basis).
3. **Local regression proof.** Each row asserts its post-change exact count. A
   mutant restoring per-call validation, with the counter and exact expectations
   unchanged, reaches and fails that count assertion in each row. Use the same
   evidence-only timeout, retain expected/actual counts and assertion output,
   then restore the candidate byte-for-byte. A timeout or unrelated error is
   not a kill. The two candidate runs in AC1 must cover the restored final tree.

   expectations are unchanged. The counter is pass-through: other tests in

own invocations sequentially; do not wait for a quiet host or reserve a

commands and 30 s limits, without the evidence-only override. Ubuntu/macOS
run full suites; the three Windows shards collectively cover the full suite.
Missing, pending, failed, cancelled, skipped, ambiguous or stale-head hosted
checks do not qualify. An earlier head's green does not qualify a later head.

If a local command times out at either 30 s or the raised evidence timeout,
record `local-timeout-inconclusive` with head/tree, command, row, exit code and
observed timing. It is neither a candidate defect, a local PASS, a census,
a mutant kill, nor timing evidence either way. Do not infer host-load causality
from timeout alone. No retry-until-green, source timeout change or reduced-load
window follows. If it prevents complete counts or assertion kills, report those
items as unproved, not waived or failed assertions. Hosted status is evaluated
separately: local timeouts cannot fail or satisfy AC5, and hosted green cannot
supply the missing AC1-3 evidence.
