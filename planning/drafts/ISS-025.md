## Context and evidence

Durable circuit breakers are part of controller state, but project thresholds
and recovery policy cannot leak into the engine. The generic lifecycle is in
`docs/architecture/state-machines.md`; policy ownership is settled in
`docs/architecture/contract-decisions.md`.

## Scope fence

In: generic breaker reducer, affected opaque capability holds, policy-version
binding, recovery transaction/probe, receipts, replay, and restart behavior.

Out: project-specific thresholds, provider retry algorithms, deployment policy,
or silently converting unknown authority into closed.

## Decisions already made

Adapters supply trip/recovery facts and policy versions. The engine owns the
state transition and blocks the affected opaque capability while open,
recovering, or unknown. Different adapter policies do not alter engine schemas.

## Bounded literal prerequisite proposal

The sixth literal group in `contract-decisions.md` proposes the existing
breaker-receipt family and closed inline opening, transaction, probe and
recovery evidence for the first ISS-041 consumer. Per-capability checkpoints,
one phase per capability/cycle, whole-reduction UNKNOWN on config/policy change
and no automatic UNKNOWN exit are explicit replans of previously unspecified
mechanics. All six lifecycle states and retained holds survive; NO_TRIP never
clears a hold. ISS-013 owns the paired project recovery observation literals.

This does not implement this issue's reducer, persistence, replay, probe or
restart acceptance. Complete public event/journal history, source/probe
admission and production authority remain gated. Genesis for the quarantined
fixture requires actual new-root/lease/initial-census evidence; an empty array
or production root replacement is never authority. Independent review and the
packet's complete edge/refusal, reuse, changed-policy and removal-mutant matrix
are required before implementation. Footprint is this note, the shared ledger,
minimal lifecycle clarification and other named ownership notes/round 387;
no new family/issue/edge, code, provider or executed evidence is claimed.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. Every listed breaker transition and no unlisted transition reduces
   deterministically across restart. Evidence: generated total transition tests.
2. Malformed history, stale policy, moved open receipt, contradictory probe, or
   interrupted recovery cannot clear a hold. Evidence: one negative fixture per
   authority field and crash boundary.
3. Two adapters with different trip/recovery policies drive the unchanged
   lifecycle through typed facts. Evidence: contrasting adapter transcripts.
4. A successful recovery reaches `CLOSED_RECOVERED`, then a documented next
   routine cycle reevaluates current policy before capability use. Evidence:
   receipt/replay/day-after test.
