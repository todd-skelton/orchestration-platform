## Decision

The ISS-160 conflict-successor park must stay terminal for ordinary `ready`
re-entry, and any further attempt must stay inside the retained four-attempt
and 64-launch ceilings with host-interpreted authority and complete retained
history. The simplest alternative is to let `ready` admit the next unused
attempt automatically after any failed conflict successor. Instead,
generalize the existing closed `terminalAttemptAdmission` declaration so a
host-authorized packet admits exactly one ordinary successor at absolute
attempt N+1 only after a failed conflict successor at attempt N with findings
`[]` whose stop is the demonstrated controller/admission gap, and give the
composition stop a self-describing diagnostic that names this route. The
downside accepted is a second admission kind with an unpublished-terminal
variant and one more host-authored packet per lineage, until ISS-239/241
retire one-off instruments. The choice is wrong if a label, headroom or
unchanged packet ever admits a successor, if the admission accepts a lineage
whose terminal was not a conflict-successor park, or if the pre-admission
refusal or a replay charges an attempt or launch.
