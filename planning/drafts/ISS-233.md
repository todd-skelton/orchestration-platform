## Decision

An unchanged verification-only grant must reach its retained stop without admitting any different stop or weakening admission.
The simplest alternative is to remove or rewrite earlier history, but that destroys retained accounting and exceeds host recovery authority.
Choose a bounded correction separating historical-cycle applicability from strict granted-stop validation in the existing scan and callers.
Accept the existing retained-state reads and reservation lifecycle, with no new grant field, schema, recovery instrument or migration.
This choice is wrong if a target mismatch becomes skippable, an older matching stop can replace the latest stop, or the existing path already passes the same-key regression.
