## Context and evidence

Fixture parity is insufficient for live authority timing and operational
topology. The new platform must observe the same live inputs without performing
consumer mutations. See the adapter SDK, self-hosting release, and parity
fixture.

## Scope fence

In: pinned platform install, read-only consumer adapter, concurrent incumbent
and shadow observation, normalized decision comparison, discrepancy taxonomy,
bounded observation window, delivery-rate and latency delta recording, and
daily report.

Out: merge, deploy, issue, branch, or controller-state mutations by the shadow;
automatic discrepancy acceptance; production cutover.

## Decisions already made

The incumbent named in `docs/planning/first-consumer.md` remains authoritative.
The shadow receives no mutation credentials. Eligibility, unavailable limits,
duration/count gate, latency thresholds, and authority timing follow that
document; discrepancy states follow `docs/architecture/state-machines.md`. This
issue consumes and reports against the comparison window and thresholds
published in `docs/planning/first-consumer.md` (seven-day/100-decision gate,
unavailable limits, latency thresholds); `ISS-018` references the same
publication.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

1. The shadow proves zero mutation capability before live observation. Evidence:
   credential census plus mutation-canary refusals.
2. Every eligible incumbent cycle has one correlated shadow result; unavailable
   correlations remain within the published limit and none hides a mutation
   decision. Evidence: correlation completeness verifier.
3. Every authoritative difference has a stable disposition and regression
   fixture. Evidence: discrepancy ledger validation.
4. The published seven-day/100-decision window completes with zero unexplained
   authority differences and latency below the published p50/p95 thresholds.
   Evidence: terminal comparison report and pre-window baseline.
5. The terminal comparison report records incumbent-versus-shadow
   delivery-rate and decision-latency deltas over the same window, with the
   incumbent's concurrent-lane count, as non-gating evidence feeding the
   parked same-host concurrency decision in `EPIC-KERNEL`. A missing
   recording fails this criterion; an unfavorable delta alone does not.
   Evidence: throughput section of the terminal comparison report.

## Verification plan

`pnpm run shadow:preflight -- --consumer first`

`pnpm run shadow:compare -- --consumer first --check`

## Predicted footprint and dependencies

Create only `adapters/first-consumer/src/shadow/`, shadow correlation tooling, and
discrepancy fixtures. Shared adapter manifest/exports/composition are owned by
`ISS-028`. Blocked by adapter SDK, self-hosting, final certification, parity
fixture, and adapter scaffold. No platform-core, shared-manifest, or glossary
edit.

## Operator actions

Provide a read-only code-host token and start/end approval for the observation
window. No mutation-scoped credential may enter the shadow environment.

## Glossary impact

None; shadow and discrepancy terms are registered already.

## External authority probe and evidence timing

Capture code-host, CI, queue, and deployment observations concurrently with the
incumbent at each lifecycle decision point; post-hoc API reads do not establish
parity.

## Independent review packet seed

Attack accidental mutation credentials, missing-cycle correlation, differences
hidden as unavailable, live timing sampled after the incumbent decision,
throughput deltas omitted or silently promoted into a window gate, and
shadow load changing incumbent behavior. Use credential census, correlation
coverage, and discrepancy ledger.
