## Scope and observed failure

Extract the remaining useful-cycle behavior needed when an independently
launched source reviewer exits successfully but its final verdict transport is
malformed. The accepted queue preserves ordinary PASS/FAIL terminals and
complete fixable-review repair, but malformed transport leaves no durable
disposition or authorized source-to-replacement join. One bounded replacement
must inspect the unchanged candidate without rerunning the author, inventing
lost findings or weakening delivery evidence.

ISS-081/#343 is registered under EPIC-KERNEL/M2 and blocked by ISS-078/#338.
Pilot `iss081-review-report-20260909` starts semantic implementation 1/4 at
`d141313c80a20ea5346cca8181b7e58f6ae32bc3`. The original source participant
`01a08726-fbeb-7db0-883e-cc5c1cc0cc9c` ended in a mechanical launcher exit
without a completion result; this run is the single same-configuration retry,
native admission 2/8. No prior outcome, usage or spend is rewritten.

## G0 and smallest complete shape

G0: PASS. Add one typed malformed terminal, one policy-neutral immutable
source-review binding and one private recovery adapter composed by the existing
bounded queue. Reuse accepted flow, participant, repair and delivery paths.
This is smaller than a parallel engine or project-specific controller.

Profile: `contract` under `quality-contract/v2`.

- SCOPE [High] - All seven criteria map to existing flow/queue/repair/delivery
  boundaries; verification-failure repair remains ISS-082.
- ROBUSTNESS [High] - Malformed, identity-mismatched, negative, incomplete,
  pre-launch-unknown, terminal-unknown and completed states discriminate; there
  is one replacement and no reviewer-shopping loop.
- DEPTH [High] - A pure binding contract is separate from filesystem, launcher
  and repository policy; accepted DB3 adapters remain the only component paths.
- READABILITY [Med] - Original review, selected review, malformed transport,
  incomplete report and accepted verdict have distinct names.
- TESTS [High] - Hosted tests cross real flow/queue/repair/delivery interfaces
  and retain every existing meaningful assertion.
- OBSERVABILITY [High] - Typed bounded reasons expose malformed, conflicting,
  unknown and substituted state without raw traces, private output or env data.
- SECURITY [High] - Exact fingerprint, author, head, original reviewer and
  fresh selected reviewer bind before delivery; telemetry grants no authority.
- PERFORMANCE [Low] - The malformed-only path adds one participant and finite
  state reads; no concurrency, cache or timeout policy changes.
- ROLLOUT [High] - Legacy two-participant state remains valid and new state is
  write-once; fixtures cannot promote or close the real-use obligation.
- CONSISTENCY [High] - Intent-before-effect, exact keys/heads and immutable
  lineage match accepted DB3 contracts; no receipt is rewritten.
- EXPERIENCE [Low] - No UI changes; valid incomplete work remains visible.
- LANGUAGE [High] - Malformed transport, valid negative, incomplete, selected
  review, replacement and supersession retain separate meanings.

## Acceptance evidence

1. Successfully completed reviewer events with unparsable or structurally
   invalid final verdicts record `malformed`; valid negative/incomplete reports,
   wrong run/role/head, failed launch and unknown terminal remain distinct and
   never become replacement eligibility or PASS.
2. Only a pre-existing external `dogfood-review-recovery-authority/v1` bound to
   the malformed source-review case consumes one replacement. Candidate head,
   configuration fingerprint, source author and original reviewer remain exact;
   the selected reviewer is fresh and the author/source checkout is never
   changed or rerun.
3. Write-once intent precedes launch, attempt identity precedes observation and
   terminal precedes selection. Intent without attempt and unknown result do not
   redispatch; completed restart performs no component or provider mutation.
4. The replacement receives complete scope, contract profile, G0 and all twelve
   ordered quality pairs. Encoding never truncates JSON or drops a required
   field, finding or note; evidence that cannot fit the admitted 2000-character
   boundary stays `complete:false` and cannot be accepted or replaced again.
5. `dogfood-source-review-binding/v1` explicitly joins the original source
   run/state/fingerprint/author/head, original malformed review and selected
   passed-or-failed review. Repair and delivery select it only through that
   immutable binding; absent binding preserves the accepted original path.
6. Original/selected terminals, participant ordinals/outcomes, every known or
   unavailable usage measure, findings, notes, implementation-attempt count,
   native ceiling and spend lineage remain lossless and immutable. No reset,
   invention, truncation or original-record overwrite is allowed.
7. Independent exact-head review, four controller static gates, actual hosted
   Node 24 Windows/macOS/Linux and external stable promotion are required. A
   naturally encountered post-activation malformed review and completed
   restart remain real-use closure; fixtures must not manufacture that proof.

## Verification

Controller-owned hosted cases distinguish malformed transport from valid
negative/incomplete/identity/unknown states; prove one fresh exact-head
replacement through real adapter interfaces; cover restart without redispatch;
reject a second malformed review; mutate every binding field; preserve reports
and per-measure usage; drive selected PASS to delivery and selected complete
FAIL to accepted repair; and prove completed restart repeats no effects.
Existing 271 cases across 11 suites and all assertions remain. Genuine Git cases
keep finite `30_000` per-test deadlines. The controller runs typecheck, format,
planning and board gates after independent source review, then hosted Node 24 on
three OSes. This author runs no local test runner/build/native Git fixture and
claims none passed.

## Predicted footprint

Only private dogfood terminal/flow/dispatch/review policy and adapter, bounded
queue, selected-review reads in accepted repair/delivery adapters, focused
existing dogfood suites, supervised documentation, this issue, EPIC-KERNEL/M2
roadmap and pressure round 487. No public package, compiler, workflow,
environment, credential, native permission or unrelated adapter change.

## Review attack surface

Attack malformed-versus-invalid-authority confusion; wrong run/role/head;
summary truncation/loss; valid incomplete replacement; second replacement;
reused author/original reviewer; changed/dirty worktree; intent without identity;
unknown observation; binding substitution/extras; delivery or repair selecting
the original after supersession; lineage/usage/finding/note/attempt/admission/
spend loss; restart effects; ISS-082 or PR-refresh duplication; fixture proof
presented as real recovery; and worker/candidate self-certification.
