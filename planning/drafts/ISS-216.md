## Decision

The stored stop and posted receipt have different bodies by design, so a
literal comparison of those two fields cannot admit the recorded stop.
The smallest viable alternative is an exact comparison against the body the
poster would emit for that stop, using its deterministic unpark text and a
shared canonical derivation wherever the poster and admission need it.
Choose that exact posted-body derivation while preserving all other ISS-215
identity, authority, history and reservation checks.
This accepts a narrow production-code seam between posting and admission,
without introducing a new packet, historical schema field or recovery route.
The choice is wrong if a different byte sequence, another stop, or a replayed
successor can be admitted, or if a future self stop posted by the same path
cannot round-trip through admission.
