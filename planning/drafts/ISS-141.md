## Why

ISS-110's M2 JPEG run `m2-jpeg-20260914` reached Chase Sets PR #8002,
head `181c1d4c5f1bb3f4330cd4ae50f0fd688de3280e`, but workflow run
`34818999245` failed. The native handoff sliced the last 4000 characters of
`gh run view --log-failed`, retaining only PR Required aggregate boilerplate.
The corrective author could not discover Static Checks, Unit Tests and E2E
diagnostics. The incident is recorded at
https://github.com/todd-skelton/orchestration-platform/issues/367#issuecomment-5660899933
and clarified at
https://github.com/todd-skelton/orchestration-platform/issues/367#issuecomment-5660913866.
The interrupted attempt 4 also contains a legitimate partial fixture-path fix;
it is not a substitute for addressing the other failed jobs.

## Done when

- Keep prompts and terminal reports bounded while making complete underlying
  failed-job logs accessible outside the checkout, associated with the exact
  rejected candidate, publication and failed check/run URLs.
- Initial correction, resumed correction, same-attempt dead-worker retry and
  independent review discover that evidence even when aggregate boilerplate
  exceeds 4000 characters and several jobs fail. Do not import a verdict,
  waive a check or prescribe manufactured product changes.
- A normal stopped executor upgrade can acquire missing logs for the existing
  attempt 4 from its predecessor's publication without editing historical
  findings, config fingerprints or implementation-attempt accounting. Failed
  evidence acquisition stops dispatch instead of launching an uninformed author.
- Preserve the interrupted author's tracked patch and execution trace as
  runtime evidence before the existing clean-base retry, so useful partial work
  can be reapplied and independently verified. Do not change the running loop,
  runtime, product, consumed attempts, retry ceiling or failed verdicts.
- Reproduce the truncated-log failure in regression tests, verify the recovery
  paths, and run typecheck, format:check, planning:check and test. Document the
  bounded recovery and its limits in docs/loop.md.

## Out of scope

JPEG implementation, CI waivers or reruns, deployment or merge, attempt resets,
automatic unparking, a salvage registry, a second writer, legacy controller
ceremony, and M2 completion claims.
