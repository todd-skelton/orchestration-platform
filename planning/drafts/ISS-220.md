## Decision

An admitted queue merge must complete without mistaking lifecycle progress for changed publication identity.
Simply observing merge earlier is smaller but already shipped and still races when pending falls through to checks.
Choose existing `confirmMerge` reconciliation for retained complete green checks and queued merge intent, returning pending without that fallthrough and continuing confirmed merge through cleanup.
Accept waiting until the next ordinary supervisor observation when a merge completes immediately after a pending response, with no new inner polling, sleep or retry.
The choice is wrong if pending still re-enters checks, removal can enqueue again, or confirmation skips cleanup or post-merge observation.

### Options

| Option | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Observe merge earlier but fall through when pending | Already shipped; leaves the observed race before either checks identity read. Reject. |
| Teach checks to return a typed merged result and reconcile it | Can cover both reads, but changes the adapter/failed-log consumer contract unnecessarily. Reject for this bounded queued case. |
| Keep admitted queued delivery in existing merge reconciliation | Select. No OPEN-only read after a pending queue observation; the next ordinary poll sees MERGED or another authoritative state. |

Fresh enqueue still requires exact-head review, local gates and attributed
complete green checks; GitHub's queue owns final admission/merge. Observing
that committed outcome does not authorize a new merge mutation from old green.
