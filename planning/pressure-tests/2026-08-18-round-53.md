## Disposition

The repair coherently deletes the open schema-reference and resource-kind
surfaces, makes catalog keys non-renderable, and adds the intended step-4 and
step-7 equalities. It still does not define one exact digest, one deterministic
catalog lookup, one exhaustive absence rule, or the authority source for the
renderer registry. These are public-contract ambiguities, not implementation
details, so implementation is **not authorized** on this `BLOCK_REPLAN`.

### `OP-R53-001` — `actionCoreDigest` is acyclic in intent but not exact

The decision says `actionCoreDigest` is a domain-separated digest of the
enclosing `module-action-plan/v1` action projection and excludes
`dispatchBrief` and every digest derived from it. Neither the digest-domain
literal nor the projection's exact member census, scalar/nested schemas,
canonical member order, and ordered framing parts are stated. The ABI likewise
says only "acyclic action-core projection." Two implementations can therefore
include different plan facts, choose different domains, or disagree about
whether requested role is in the core while both satisfy the prose. The
step-4 equality cannot discriminate a moved or partially substituted plan until
that projection is exact.

Bounded repair: in the governing ledger, name one exact action-core projection
schema and digest domain; enumerate every member, type, nested schema, canonical
order, and framed part; and enumerate the excluded brief, action-core,
rendered-byte, and template-derived fields. Keep requested-role treatment
explicit whether it is included in the core or bound only by the separate role
equality. Add exact-byte/domain/order, omitted/extra member, coordinated
substitution, and digest-cycle mutants. Do not add a second plan or digest.

### `OP-R53-002` — The finite catalog does not have a unique resolver key

The catalog is bounded, dense, release-reviewed, and raw keys are expressly
not rendered. However, "unique `dispatchCatalog`" does not say what is unique.
A directive is resolved by
`(actionKind, capabilityName, directiveKind, code)`, while `planAccessor` and
`templateId` are not carried by the directive. Two rows may share that lookup
tuple but select different accessors or templates and still be distinct catalog
records. Both rows admit the same directive, leaving the host with two
conforming renderings. This defeats the claimed finite deterministic authority
without reopening an external resolver.

Bounded repair: require catalog-row uniqueness by the exact four-part resolver
key and require that key to select exactly one accessor/template pair. State
whether duplicate action/capability declarations outside the catalog are
forbidden or cross-bound to the same pair census. Add duplicate-key mutants
where only the accessor, only the template, and both differ. Retain the current
1–256 bound and static release-reviewed registry; no new registry or schema
reference is needed.

### `OP-R53-003` — Operator-action absence is optional rather than exhaustive

The ledger constrains an `ABSENT` directive when one exists and forbids its
coexistence with a present operator action. It requires every *other* kind to
have a `PRESENT` directive, but it never requires the operator-action kind to
have either a present directive or the one absent directive. A brief with the
eight other kinds and no operator-action record is therefore valid, contrary
to the claimed explicit absence and ISS-002 AC12. In addition, the ledger calls
the required rows substantive but operationally defines only `PRESENT` plus a
catalog match; it does not state the observable catalog/renderer property that
makes such a row nonempty or substantive.

Bounded repair: require exactly one of (a) one or more unique `PRESENT`
operator-action directives and zero absent records, or (b) exactly one
`ABSENT` operator-action directive and zero present operator-action directives.
For all nine kinds, either define the smallest executable meaning of
"substantive" in the admitted catalog/renderer contract or replace that claim
with the exact weaker property actually intended. Add missing-operator,
present-plus-absent, duplicate-absent, empty-renderer/empty-typed-value, and
per-kind minimum mutants.

### `OP-R53-004` — `templateRegistryDigest` has no authoritative producer

Step 7 is correctly owned by dispatch and now equal-binds its role to the
step-4 request and brief. It also binds a `template-registry` digest, but no
other governing document names the record that produces that digest or an
equality from it to the selected active release, installed host bytes, or
release manifest. The only occurrences of this digest are the decision's
step-7 sentence. Digest-binding an observed or supplied registry prevents later
byte changes but does not prove that the registry is the release-reviewed one.
The module descriptor/catalog path has the needed release-manifest binding; the
renderer side does not yet state the corresponding authority chain.

Bounded repair: either delete the separate template-registry digest if the
active release's already-bound host package identity completely selects the
compiled renderer table, or name its single producer and require exact equality
to the installed active-release manifest/host bytes before rendering. Assign
the producer to ISS-021 and the step-7 comparison to ISS-008/ISS-026 while
leaving ISS-002 responsible only for the pure relation and fixtures. Add stale,
candidate, self-reported, moved-template, and digest-substitution mutants. Do
not introduce filesystem, network, adapter, or dynamic template resolution.

- **Removal and no-prose:** a scoped search found no `resourceKind`,
  `referenceSchemaVersion`, `referenceDigest`, or old `actionPlanDigest` in the
  governing documents, contracts package, or contract tests. Resource records
  now expose only access and an opaque digest. Action/capability/code/accessor/
  template/schema/resource keys are expressly non-rendered. No schema resolver,
  path, network lookup, diagnostic namespace, sparse tree, inventory,
  coordinator, retention/compaction kind, or superseded v2/v3 authority export
  was reintroduced.
- **Exact records and bounds:** the dispatch envelope and its three nested
  schemas have exact literals and member censuses. Action and catalog tokens
  are bounded to 64 characters; directive and footprint arrays are bounded to
  256; digests and enums are closed. The unresolved exactness is limited to the
  action projection/digest, catalog lookup key, operator-action cardinality,
  and renderer-registry authority above.
- **Engine/adapter boundary:** opaque resource identity and the expanded
  vocabulary oracle preserve the engine/adapter fence. Raw catalog values do
  not enter worker text. The removed resource class is not needed by ISS-011,
  ISS-021, or ISS-008 as currently scoped.
- **Cross-binding and self-certification:** the brief now equal-binds action,
  capability, subject, descriptor, and action-core facts at step 4, and role at
  step 7. Selected installed modules and external stable release review remain
  the intended authority; neither a worker nor candidate is granted mutation or
  promotion authority by the brief. Findings 1 and 4 must be closed before the
  equalities can prove exact plan/renderer selection rather than only bind
  caller-chosen representations.
- **Producer/consumer and dependencies:** ISS-011 remains the runtime
  module/catalog producer, ISS-021 the renderer producer, ISS-008 the dispatch-
  plan/rendered-byte owner, and ISS-026 the cycle composer. ISS-002 may own the
  closed schemas and pure cross-binding validators without a new dependency
  edge, provided the missing projection and renderer authority are specified as
  upstream contracts rather than implemented by ISS-002 in downstream packages.
  No roadmap or registration change is justified by these repairs.
