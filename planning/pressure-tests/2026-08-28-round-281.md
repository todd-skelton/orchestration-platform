One lifecycle contradiction is still accepted as `UNKNOWN` instead of refused.
`coherentParserChild` accepts a child with zero exit and close events, zero
errors, no stderr overflow, no timeout, and no post-signal expiry when
`outputAccepted:false`, `normalizedBytesMatch:false`, and
`normalizedDigest:null`. The stable observer cannot return that row: its first
race completes only on a full terminal pair, an error/overflow failure, or the
execution timeout; after a non-terminal completion, a missing terminal pair can

no failure trigger and no post-signal expiry is the same impossible state.

terminal pair unless a recorded failure/timeout triggered signaling and the
post-signal deadline expired, and add decisive zero-event and partial-event
mutants. This does not require retaining bytes, adding a writer/registry, or
changing PASS authority.
