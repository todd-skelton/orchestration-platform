## Direct post-wait deletion oracle is exact

Before each direct helper call, the fixture resets terminal state, selects
clock mode 9 or 10, and prepositions `fixture_terminal_tick_call` to one. The
real helper is then invoked as:

```c
terminal_wait_before_deadline(ADMISSION_FIXTURE_PROCESS, 100U)
```

Its own clock reads are therefore:

```text
pre-WAIT_OBJECT_0: 5,099  # elapsed 4,999
post-WAIT_OBJECT_0: 5,100 or 5,101
```

The fixture process wait is signalled, and the direct call must return zero in
both cases. Removing the post-wait `terminal_remaining_before` call instead
returns one immediately, so the test fails before any later worker precheck can
consume the boundary value. This directly kills the Round-215 survivor.

The existing full `run_active_terminal` modes 9 and 10 remain independently
useful: after detecting those exact boundary crossings they terminate/join,
reprove Job zero, release both buffers, and produce only canonical empty
TIMEOUT. The direct and composed oracles therefore prove both the local check
and its trusted cleanup consequence without coupling them.
