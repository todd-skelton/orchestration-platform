## Clean Round-213 production repairs

The current production relations are correct and should remain:

- `terminal_remaining_before` rejects backward time and treats elapsed time
  `>= bound` as terminal timeout;
- `terminal_wait_before_deadline` checks before a wait and again after
  `WAIT_OBJECT_0`, so a signalled wait is not intrinsically proof that it
  completed in time;
- `terminal_job_zero_before` checks the clock before the Job query and after
  every query, including a successful zero observation;
- after `GetExitCodeProcess` proves a non-`STILL_ACTIVE` code, a final stable
  clock check prevents selection of EXITED at or after the boundary;
- a final-clock timeout reterminates/reproves the exact Job and workers before
  producing canonical empty TIMEOUT; backward time refuses;
- `terminal_close_workers` makes at most two adjacent close attempts for each
  retained thread and pipe, clears the exact owner only after a successful
  attempt, and retains persistent ambiguity for hard abort;
- the worker's earlier pipe-close failure remains visible to stable main, and
  the stable runtime owners remain null after transfer; and
- both buffer-free siblings are attempted, with failure latching hard abort and
  resource ambiguity while production retains the failed pointer.

No new timer, worker, authority field, journal kind, ACL, cwd/environment
member, public export, candidate verdict, receipt, or promotion path was added.

## Clean per-owner close matrix

The fixture now maps twelve exact close cases:

- transient and persistent failures for stdin, stdout, and stderr pipe owners;
- transient and persistent failures for stdin, stdout, and stderr thread
  owners; and
- a bounded call oracle of two calls for transient owners, three total calls
  for a persistently failing pipe (worker attempt plus two stable-main
  attempts), and two calls for a persistently failing thread.

For every role it verifies all sibling owners are null, a persistently failing
selected owner remains nonnull, all three runtime pipe fields remain null after
transfer, and transient cases clear the selected owner. This kills the
Round-213 role-skipping and unbounded-retry mutants.

Hard-abort cases assert resource ambiguity and no fixture persist, cleanup, or
restore calls. The already reviewed production `serve` branch exits 70 before
revocation, and the real forced-join diagnostic exercises tracked RECOVER.

## Blocker 1: post-wait deadline check lacks a crossing oracle

The new post-`WAIT_OBJECT_0` check in `terminal_wait_before_deadline` is correct
production code, but none of the fixture clock schedules make that check alone
cross the boundary:

- fault modes 2 and 4 present 5,000/5,001 ms at the pre-wait clock read;
- mode 3 keeps all later reads at 4,999 ms;
- mode 5 crosses from 4,999 to 5,000 in the post-Job-zero check; and
- modes 6–8 cross or go backward only at the final post-exit-code check.

Deleting the post-wait `terminal_remaining_before` call and returning success
directly for `WAIT_OBJECT_0` therefore leaves every current result unchanged.
That is an exact surviving deletion mutant for a requirement explicitly added
by Round 213.

Smallest repair: add independent root or worker schedules where the pre-wait
clock is 4,999 and the post-signalled-wait clock is exactly 5,000, then 5,001.
Both must select timeout, reach trusted kill/join/Job-zero proof, and never make
EXITED usable. Retain the existing pre-wait, post-Job, final-exit, and backward
schedules.

## Blocker 2: failed buffer pointer retention is not asserted

The buffer matrix now injects stdout-only, stderr-only, and both-buffer
`HeapFree` failure. It correctly asserts that both frees were attempted and
that the successful-free count is respectively one, one, and zero. Thus the
simultaneous faults are genuinely reached.

However, `fixture_terminal_fault_case` never compares the two
`active.workers[*].output` pointers after those attempts. Its validity decision
uses only result, hard-abort, resource ambiguity, attempt counts, and successful
free count. The later fixture cleanup conditionally frees whatever pointer is
still present, but that occurs after validity was computed and is not itself an
ownership assertion.

A production mutant that unconditionally sets an output pointer to null after
failed `HeapFree` still produces all expected counts and hard-abort flags and
passes the matrix while losing the exact ambiguous allocation owner.

Smallest repair: for stdout-only failure require stdout to equal its originally
captured allocation and stderr to be null; for stderr-only require the inverse;
for simultaneous failure require both exact original pointers retained. Also
retain the one-attempt-per-buffer and no-mutation assertions. No additional
free or cleanup mechanism is needed.
