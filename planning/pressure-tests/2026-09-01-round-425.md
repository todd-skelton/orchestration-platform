## Proportionality and semantics

Round424 landed the complete worker-result REVIEW preflight SDK boundary and
migrated the smaller `consumeReview` caller. The remaining live duplicate is
step 6 of `consumeFinalReviewCycle`; leaving it inline preserves a weaker
changed-target classification and makes the executable 15-step evidence bypass
the SDK boundary. Migrating that one caller is smaller than another helper,
contract family or release-candidate path.

The final-cycle caller supplies its already admitted module input, action,
fixed host mapping, selected route, fixed current-subject read, deterministic
identity source and existing clocks. Exact current identity remains ELIGIBLE. A
valid changed subject is retained as AVAILABLE and terminalizes step 6 as
REFUSED/TARGET_CHANGED. A malformed or absent current source terminalizes the
same public tuple as REVIEW UNKNOWN/SOURCE_UNKNOWN without a retained subject.
An invalid or reused observation identity refuses before TERMINAL:6. Every
non-eligible result stops before step 7, dispatch, worker effects or review
authority and retains the uncertain fixture session.

an explicit public step-6 binding. Consumer controls distinguish valid changed
evidence from unavailable evidence, prove the retained-subject census, cover
malformed and absent source rows, and prove invalid and prior-facts observation
identity refusal before terminal output. The existing seeded-author dispatch
collision continues to prove that successful step 6 consumes exactly one
identity before step 7.

Review should attack conversion of changed evidence to UNKNOWN, terminal output

authority from preflight consistency. SDK-level thenable, callback admission,
route substitution and await-detachment vectors remain in Round424 and are not
duplicated here.
