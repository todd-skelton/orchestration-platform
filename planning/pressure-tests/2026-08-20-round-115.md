## Cycle, retry, and stable-promotion analysis

The graph is acyclic. `Dsc` excludes the successor value, proposal, receipt,
tip, head, and downstream selection artifacts; proposals can therefore bind
the recomputed `Dsc` without `Dsc` depending on their selectors.

The pre-E0 branches deliberately do not create a
`POINTER_MUTATION_RUN_CURRENT` selection, ordinary commit evidence, or mutation
packet. Making that meta pointer select the authority required to select
itself would create the cycle the exception is designed to avoid. The smaller
shape is direct deterministic CAS under the external destination/bootstrap
lock and immutable transaction journal: retry re-drives the same bytes and
same mutation, while moved input, changed transaction/prior/bytes, conflict,
unknown read-back, or an incomplete tuple refuses. No conflict is resolved by
age or by a second proposal.

After E0, every new mutation of these families requires the exact selected
stable authority epoch and the ordinary run-current/commit protocol.
Historical bootstrap records remain immutable evidence but grant no live
handle. Stable N therefore owns N+1 staging and promotion; neither a candidate
epoch nor the bootstrap producer arm can authorize later promotion.
