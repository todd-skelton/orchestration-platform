the mutation ID, and the transaction-addressed root path needs the transaction
ID. `pointer-current-tip/v1` exposes neither; its `Dp/Dv/Dr` hashes cannot be

exactly that and forbid treating directory enumeration, caller-selected paths,
an unauthenticated index/journal, duplicate family heads, alternate tips,

The BOOTSTRAP archive triple is now structural nullability only. All-null no
longer claims first-ever selection, all-non-null no longer claims which archive
was selected, bare filesystem absence and caller Booleans are rejected, and
selected archive/tombstone versus missing, corrupt, or `UNKNOWN`
`FULL_REQUIRED` history is assigned to the later cleanup-archive composition
ledger and common-CAS locator decision. That is the correct fail-closed
boundary.

The residual phrase “any ... truncation ... refuses” is nevertheless an
implicit completeness/currentness claim. For example, both of these are valid
structural observations under the decided one-to-six bound:

```text
PENDING/NOT_PUBLISHED -> PENDING/PUBLISHING -> PENDING/PUBLISHED
PENDING/NOT_PUBLISHED -> PENDING/PUBLISHING
```

Given only the root and supplied array, the second input may be a genuine
earlier selection or a tail-truncated copy of the first. Root equality,
ordinal-zero genesis, consecutive ordinals, prior common-`Dv`, time monotonicity,
and the closed edge graph are identical in both interpretations. The same
problem exists for supplied `[PREPARED]` versus
`[PREPARED, POST_ACTIVATION]`. Refusing the shorter valid prefix would violate
the stated one-record lower bound and prevent structural validation during a
live intermediate state; accepting it means tail truncation is not detectable
by this slice.

Smallest repair: replace unqualified `truncation` in the history rule and
structural compatibility claims with the exact detectable cases—missing
ordinal zero, an interior omission producing a gap or prior-`Dv` mismatch, or
a supplied history exceeding its bound—and state that suffix completeness and
currentness are not claims of this slice. Defer them with authenticated
selected-current evidence to the later common-CAS locator/composition review.
Do not add a second tip, latest file, directory scan, caller Boolean, or
unauthenticated expected-head digest. This is a wording/scope repair; no new
schema or mechanism is needed.
