# ISS-022 parser-equivalence review — round 275

`BLOCK_REPLAN` at exact commit
`7954c634b4a48ea7bd32e69a2e42748cd298b6fc`.

`executeChild` starts a ten-second timer, but expiry only calls
`child.kill("SIGKILL")` and the probe then waits without bound for `close`. A
failed/refused kill or missing terminal event therefore hangs instead of
producing non-PASS evidence. The timer is also not latched, so a code-zero
close racing an already-fired timeout can still be accepted. The cooperative
happy-path test cannot detect either deletion, nor deletion of the stderr cap
or temporary-bundle cleanup.

Minimal repair: latch timeout/stderr overflow before signaling, bound the
post-signal wait, require the exact one-message/code-zero/no-signal/no-timeout
terminal tuple, and add deterministic timeout, stderr-overflow, terminal-race,
and temporary-root-deletion mutants. Retain the current stable-root bundle
entrypoint, exact eight-row corpus, three-child byte comparison, LF/path census,
and esbuild lock footprint.

Proportionality: the stable bundled child is the smallest credible parser
equivalence check and adds no reducer, writer, PASS authority, helper, or
binary. The repair closes an in-scope hosted-runner hang without expanding the
probe or introducing process-isolation machinery.
