## Scope

One new private module,
`scripts/conformance/hosted-native-lock-preparation.mts`, which, given an
authenticated hosted native-lock plan context and a canonical runner temp:

- allocates the ledger's three mutually external runner-temp roots with the
  fixed leaves `acquisition`, `preparation`, and `case`, resolves each to its
  real identity, requires each to be a direct child of the runner temp, refuses
  any overlap among them or with the stable source root, and refuses a non-empty
  root;
- calls the landed acquisition owner against the acquisition root and reports an
  honest `AVAILABLE`, `UNSUPPORTED`, or `UNKNOWN` state, never mapping an
  unknown capture onto the unavailable branch;
- applies the ledger's `external()` rule to every acquisition output before any
  build input is composed;
- inside the landed authenticated candidate consume/delete window, binds the
  candidate subject to the plan context's `candidateSubjectDigest`, calls
  `prepareNativeLockBuild` with the preparation root as its `runnerTemp`, checks
  that no build row carries a loaded member and that every declared output is a
  retained sealed row, verifies the case root is still empty, and takes the
  post-case census over the archive root; and
- returns the pending preparation, the acquisition census, and the archive
  census.

The candidate root is deleted on every path, because the landed owner owns that
deletion and its cleanup failure is returned as a refusal, never as a successful
build. The archive root is the preparation root and, with no control or case
phase in this slice, the archive-relative mapping is the identity for the only
two permitted prefixes, `build/` and `preparation/`.

The module's exported, package-private surface is
`hostedNativeLockCandidateSourcePath`, `hostedNativeLockRootNames`,
`checkCandidateSubjectBinding`, `checkPreLoadBuildBinding`,
`censusHostedNativeLockArchive`, `prepareHostedNativeLockTransaction`, and their
types. No public package export, schema, dependency, installed tool, production
