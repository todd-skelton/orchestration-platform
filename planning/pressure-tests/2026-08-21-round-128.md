An independent literal census proved that all three field lists are unique and

| Branch | Exact count | Required branch facts |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| CREATED | 7 | core, prebound consume operation, lifecycle/mode/time/schema/transaction |
| CONSUMED | 9 | CREATED identity plus non-null gate root and native-consume receipt |
| REVOKED | 12 | preserved identity, nullable gate/consume evidence, required native-removal receipt and removal operation |

All digest fields use the closed lowercase SHA-256 grammar; operation and

grammar; mode, lifecycle, and schema are closed literals. Opposite-branch,
unknown, renamed, duplicate, null-at-required, cross-type, future-lifecycle,

REVOKED has exactly these three structural cells:

| Cell | Gate | Consume post receipt | Native consume receipt | Admissible predecessor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R0 | null | null | null | CREATED, pre-gate revoke |
| R1 | digest | null | null | CREATED, post-gate revoke |
| R2 | digest | digest | digest | CONSUMED revoke |

The consume pair is all null or all non-null, and its non-null arm implies a
non-null gate. A partial pair or non-null pair with null gate refuses. Parsing
R2 without a prior remains valid structural parsing, while the pure transition

CREATED. This separates record closure from history without inventing a proof
bag or caller Boolean.

The record contains every structural fact available before its own selection
and no downstream fact. CREATED prebinds consume but has no gate or receipt.

and the earlier native-removal receipt, but every REVOKED arm excludes its own
post-selection revoke receipt and later archive. No native receipt bytes,
