## Blocking findings

### `OP-R87-001` — trusted review context does not authenticate the exact candidate

`validateDestinationOwnerSuccessorReviewCoreBinding` proves that the nested
`independentReview.candidateDigest` equals the candidate recomputed from the
supplied core, and it separately equal-binds the opaque `reviewReceiptDigest`
to the trusted expectation. Its closed expectation, however, has no trusted
`candidateDigest` member and the validator receives no authenticated review
receipt record that binds those two values. A caller can therefore replace the
complete candidate with a different internally valid candidate, write the new
candidate digest into the nested review record, and reuse the unchanged trusted
review-receipt digest.

An executable reconstruction began with the candidate's valid fixture, changed
only the selected prior retirement proposal's canonical `proposedAt` from
`2026-08-18T12:00:00.000Z` to `2026-08-18T12:00:01.000Z`, and recomputed its
proposal receipt, selected tip, core prior-retired fields, and nested candidate
digest. This remains a legal retirement proposal: proposal time is
deliberately excluded from the mutation identity, while its canonical receipt
bytes and the exact reviewed candidate change. The candidate digest changed
from
`0a7a2788c7882442a98e7cef4085a3ede2ea7c1114bd94715d12b8bd76abd4dc`
to
`1b8bbdf4d87975be49a57c2a5616929ef8e0f28f92ab53577e5ce11316060cf5e`.
The trusted expectation and review-receipt digest remained unchanged, and
`validateDestinationOwnerSuccessorReviewCoreBinding(...)` returned `[]`.

This violates the ledger requirement that the authenticated review receipt is
issued over the exact recomputed candidate and cannot be candidate-produced.
Internal recomputation proves self-consistency, not that the independent review
authenticated those exact bytes.

The smallest repair is to add `candidateDigest` to the non-persisted closed
review expectation, require it to be a SHA-256 digest, and equal-bind the nested
review candidate both to that trusted digest and to the locally recomputed
candidate. Add an independent expected-candidate mutant and the coordinated
legal prior-proposal/tip/core rebuild above. No public schema, persisted record,
path, digest formula, coordinator, or capability is needed.

### `OP-R87-002` — post-selection context does not pin the reviewed `Dsrc`

`validateDestinationOwnerSuccessorPostSelectionBinding` recomputes `Dsrc` from
the supplied core and proves that the supplied receipt and rebuilt successor
owner graph consistently consume that recomputed value. Its closed trusted
post expectation contains the anchor, observation, three readbacks, and
destination-lock/custody observation, but no trusted `reviewCoreDigest`. The
function only parses the core; it does not run the review-core binding against
an authenticated review context or receive a parsed successor anchor whose
canonical bytes could prove which `Dsrc` the trusted anchor embeds.

An executable reconstruction changed only the supplied core's nested
`reviewReceiptDigest` to 64 `f` characters, recomputed `Dsrc`, rebuilt the legal
`ACTIVATE_SUCCESSOR` value/proposal/tip graph, and rebuilt the post-selection
receipt. `Dsrc` changed from
`1e8ef93f6121e90eb2557005ef9a6add3c4a8f688b2bbac6463f5f0e8916d651`
to
`20e48685fde07ee8dadbd98f9597062a4ba422cc4108f05fdd36c83ddccb75aa0`.
The complete trusted post expectation remained unchanged, and
`validateDestinationOwnerSuccessorPostSelectionBinding(...)` returned `[]`.

This proves only an acyclic self-consistent graph around a caller-supplied core;
it does not prove that post-selection consumed the exact independently reviewed
and persisted `Dsrc`. An opaque trusted anchor digest cannot supply that missing
equality without the anchor record and its composition being validated here.

The smallest repair is to add `reviewCoreDigest` to the non-persisted closed
post expectation and require both the recomputed core digest and the receipt's
`reviewCoreDigest` to equal it. Add independent and coordinated regressions
that rebuild the core and all downstream owner/post digests while retaining the
original trusted `Dsrc`. No public schema, anchor implementation, new record,
or cyclic upstream edge is needed.
