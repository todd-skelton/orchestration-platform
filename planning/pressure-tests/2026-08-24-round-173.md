## Rejected named-ACL experiment

The rejected experiment was not safe to land as authority-bearing code:

- account and UID absence accepted arbitrary nonzero `id`/`getent` exits, so an
  NSS/helper failure could masquerade as authoritative absence;
- `useradd` and `setfacl` cleanup intent was recorded only after reported
  success, so timeout or partial completion could leave an untracked account or
  grant;
- named `setfacl` mutation neither suppressed nor proved ACL-mask
  recalculation, and deletion did not restore the prior complete ACL;
- the launch cleared supplementary groups and enabled `no_new_privs` but did
  not explicitly clear every inherited, ambient, and bounding capability;
- pathname custody compared inode alone rather than device plus inode and
  complete ancestor identity;
- `/proc` returned numeric PIDs which were later passed to root `kill`, leaving
  a PID-reuse race capable of targeting an unrelated process;
- ambient helper lookup and non-detached command results left helper identity,
  mutation outcome, and absence ambiguous; and
- injected command/census seams could bypass the Linux platform refusal.

Deleting named ACLs is the smallest repair. Repairing their masks, snapshots,
restoration, ambiguous partial mutations, and path custody would retain more
authority machinery than the exact two-principal access relation requires.

## Authorized reduced Linux design

The rewrite may implement only this bounded design:

1. Allocate one high numeric UID and a matching high numeric private primary
   GID that are both absent and have never been used by this stable authority.
   The private group has exactly the candidate account as its sole non-root
   member. Creation verifies the exact UID, GID, login shell, primary group,
   complete supplementary-group census, and stable-runner inequality.
2. Before creation and after teardown, independently prove the account name,
   private-group name, numeric UID, and numeric GID absent. User and group
   deletion are separate required mutations: neither successful `userdel` nor
   deletion of one namespace may stand in for proof that the other is absent.
3. Use POSIX DAC only. A fresh external execution root and the exact candidate
   artifact/RPC-runner files become `root:<privateGid>` with directories `0510`
   and files `0550`; candidate scratch is `<uid>:<gid>` mode `0700`. No other
   account belongs to the private group. Other bits are zero. The candidate
   receives no stable checkout, materialization, provider, evidence, or helper
   path.
4. The stable parent retains already-open identity-checked handles. Every
   privileged ownership/mode transition verifies the expected path, complete
   ancestor custody, device plus inode, type, owner, group, and mode before and
   after mutation. Teardown restores the exact original owner/group/mode before
   identity-checked path cleanup and proves candidate access revoked.
5. Resolve the admitted helpers through exact absolute paths: `/usr/bin/sudo`,
   `/usr/sbin/useradd`, `/usr/sbin/userdel`, `/usr/bin/id`, `/usr/bin/getent`,
   `/usr/bin/setpriv`, `/usr/bin/env`, and `/usr/bin/python3`, plus any separately
   justified private-group create/delete helpers required by the final
   implementation. Each helper is a regular root-owned, group/world-nonwritable
   file whose device, inode, mode, and UID are captured and rechecked around
   authority use. Unknown, missing, moved, writable, or substituted helper
   identity refuses.
6. Admit only each helper's exact documented not-found exit/stdout/stderr shape
   as absence. Timeout, signal, malformed output, unexpected diagnostic,
   unknown exit, NSS refusal, or unreadable result is not absence. Detach every
   command/helper result as a hostile exact closed record before semantic use.
7. Register cleanup intent before every privileged mutation. A reported
   timeout, throw, terminal ambiguity, or partial result always triggers an
   independent state probe and bounded reversal; it never skips cleanup because
   the forward command did not report success. Stable administrative commands
   wait for terminal process/pipe closure rather than treating a kill request as
   a terminal mutation result.
8. Launch through the verified absolute helpers with empty candidate
   environment, exact UID/GID, cleared supplementary groups, `no_new_privs`,
   and explicit clearing of inheritable, ambient, and bounding capabilities.
   Before importing candidate code, the stable RPC runner verifies exact
   real/effective/saved/filesystem UID and GID facts, the complete group census,
   and zero capability fields. Candidate code cannot invoke `sudo` or any stable
   privileged helper.
9. A tracked stable-only Python helper, invoked only through the verified
   absolute `sudo` and Python identities, scans all four `/proc` UID fields,
   opens pidfds, revalidates the process identity, signals only through
   `signal.pidfd_send_signal`, and repeats until quiescent absence. Numeric PIDs
   alone are never later signalled. Unavailable pidfds, unreadable `/proc`,
   identity disagreement, signal ambiguity, or residual process refuses.
10. The production factory hard-refuses when `process.platform !== "linux"`.
    Tests may substitute platform state around the production guard; no injected
    runner, census, or helper seam may turn a non-Linux host into Linux authority.

The stable isolated-walk coordinator continues to own the fixed challenge,
captured monotonic launch-to-terminal-response interval, hostile semantic parse,
three fresh lifecycle cycles, normalization, and teardown-before-result order.
The Linux adapter supplies only opaque principal lifecycle authority. Neither
surface is exported from the package root, and no Linux detail enters the nine
portable core records.

## Trust and removal sweep

- Stable protected-main code alone selects the candidate artifact, RPC runner,
  helpers, expected result, timing, and lifecycle adapter. Candidate output is
  hostile data and cannot supply PASS, duration, UID/GID, helper, cleanup, or
  evidence authority.
- Unique-group DAC is equivalent to the existing exact artifact/RPC access
  obligation for this two-principal boundary. Named ACLs, ACL-mask snapshots,
  generalized grant registries, containers, and durable account management are
  deleted.
- UID and GID uniqueness are separate facts. A fresh account with a stale or
  shared group, a deleted user with a residual group, numeric reuse, an extra
  group member, or stable/candidate identity equality refuses.
- Helper identity is stable authority, not ambient PATH state. Candidate code
  receives neither helper paths nor privilege and cannot widen or replay a
  stable mutation.
- Device-plus-inode and ancestor custody bind pathname mutations. Inode-only,
  basename-only, post-open substitution, moved-root cleanup, or recursive
  deletion through an unverified ancestor refuses.
- Pidfds close the census-to-signal PID-reuse race. Complete four-UID-field
  census, terminal root exit, repeated quiescent absence, DAC revocation,
  identity-checked cleanup, user/group deletion, and final numeric/name absence
  are all required before any interval can contribute to a result.
- Same-principal fallback, Node permissions, `vm`, textual scanning, self-report,
  helper widening, and off-Linux simulation remain non-authority.

## Required executable evidence

The Linux rewrite is not reviewable as authority-bearing without deletion tests
for at least:

- exact user, group, numeric UID, and numeric GID absence outcomes, including
  unknown exits, NSS/helper refusal, malformed output, timeout, and signal;
- UID/GID reuse, extra group member, supplementary group, stable identity
  equality, and every nonzero capability field;
- every owner/group/mode bit, extra grant path, helper identity member, device
  and inode substitution, ancestor movement, and failed restoration;
- forward mutations that complete while their command reports timeout/error,
  with cleanup intent deleted one relation at a time;
- root-process exit, inherited pipe, detached/forking descendant, all four UID
  fields, PID reuse, pidfd open/revalidation/signal failure, and residual census;
- scratch/artifact/RPC cleanup ordering, user-versus-group deletion ordering,
  final dual name/numeric absence, and every cleanup failure; and
- production construction on a non-Linux platform despite injected test seams.

The tests remain simulations until the exact adapter also succeeds and its
negative probes execute on the supported hosted Linux image. A simulated PASS
cannot become a job receipt or aggregate.
