## Direct attack results

- **Exact ORDINARY tags:** the three legal framed outcomes are exactly raw
  `00` for SELECTED, raw `01` for LOST_CONFLICT, and raw `02` for
  UNKNOWN_TERMINAL. Replacing any one with another branch-local tag changes the
  declared outcome meaning and must refuse; `03` through `ff`, an empty value,
  a multi-byte encoding, enum text, and a caller-supplied number are outside
  the fixed one-byte census. The implementation surface already provides a
  raw-fixed frame part, so the implementation can select constants directly
  without adding a tag field or numeric input.
- **Exact rotation tags:** AUTHORITY_ROTATION independently maps raw `00` to
  RESUMABLE, raw `01` to SELECTED, and raw `02` to UNKNOWN. Importing the
  ORDINARY meanings into this branch refuses even though the byte values are
  intentionally reused. The already-fixed branch tag, disjoint closed branch
  records, disjoint branch parts, and final canonical union bytes prevent a
  rotation outcome from being interpreted as the ORDINARY outcome sharing its
  byte.
- **Digest recomputation boundary:** both branches frame their fixed outcome
  byte only after the common identity/old-authority/packet-authority evidence
  and their branch-specific evidence. The outcome is also present in the
  closed canonical union bytes. A swapped byte therefore cannot describe a
  valid recomputation of those bytes, and enum text or a different-width
  integer cannot substitute for a raw one-byte part. The acceptance matrix now
  requires byte goldens and negatives for swapped tags, cross-branch meanings,
  text, other widths, caller-selected values, and every other byte. The current
  tree intentionally has no `Dcommit` digest implementation yet; these are
  implementation acceptance obligations, not evidence falsely attributed to
  the pre-repair executable suite.
- **Historical and positive packet arms:** HISTORICAL_READ, every ORDINARY
  outcome, rotation RESUMABLE, and rotation SELECTED require both outer
  `currentAuthoritySelection` and `authorityHistoryBinding` non-null. The
  selected authority value and complete history binding must agree on `G`,
  head ordinal, and head record digest. Either one null, a stale head, another
  `G`, a truncated/forked/reordered history, or coordinated non-current values
  refuses before packet use.
- **Rotation UNKNOWN packet arm:** UNKNOWN requires UNKNOWN/null packet
  authority, the empty authority registry slot, null
  `currentAuthoritySelection`, and null `authorityHistoryBinding` together.
  Either non-null outer member, either one-null combination, a selected slot,
  or KNOWN packet authority is contradictory evidence and refuses. The arm
  exposes neither historical-read nor mutation capability, so null history
  cannot be used as an alternate history assertion or recovery grant.
- **Unchanged outcome/slot and history rules:** ORDINARY SELECTED still binds
  the selected target, LOST_CONFLICT the recomputed real-winner conflict
  composition, and UNKNOWN_TERMINAL the empty target slot. Rotation still
  binds old/successor/empty authority slots for RESUMABLE/SELECTED/UNKNOWN.
  Positive arms retain the complete dense history walk from GENESIS zero to
