## Blocking findings

### `OP-R92-001` — proposed-genesis `globalIdentityDigest` is bound to the wrong digest domain

The normative preimage is `G`, computed under
`state-mutation-global-identity/v1` from installation, project, state root,
custody instance, canonical authority path, and authority `Dp`. The distinct
anchor member `globalBootstrapIdentityDigest` is computed under
`global-bootstrap-identity/v1` from the external bootstrap identity projection.

`validateBootstrapAnchorUseIntentBinding` instead requires
`proposedGenesisInput.globalIdentityDigest` to equal
`anchor.globalBootstrapIdentityDigest`. The committed fixture repeats that
wrong equality. A conforming proposed-genesis input carrying `G` therefore
refuses, while an input carrying the unrelated external identity is accepted.
This also prevents the downstream `Dsc`/genesis-position preimage from being
the exact authority-history input required by the incorporated ledger.

The smallest repair is to replace that equality with a trusted or recomputed
exact `G` boundary and add one cross-domain mutant. Do not merge the two digest
domains or add a new identity record.

### `OP-R92-002` — the selected ACTIVE triples are not closed under coordinated substitution

The composition recomputes the anchor and owner tip/value/proposal digests and
checks their sibling digest links, but it omits identity equalities needed to
prove that each supplied graph is the selected graph:

- `anchorTip.anchorDigest` is never required to equal the recomputed `Dba`;
- `ownerTip.destinationDigest` is never required to equal the selected
  `Ddest`; and
- `ownerProposal.destinationDigest` and its canonical position are never
  required to equal the owner value, anchor, and intent destination.

Consequently a caller can substitute a foreign anchor digest in the anchor
tip, recompute only that tip and the intent's active-tip member, and retain all
other records. Similarly it can coordinate a foreign destination through the
owner proposal/tip and their recomputed digests and mutation ID while retaining
the original owner value and anchor destination. The local mutation-ID
recomputation does not close this because the candidate does not invoke the
existing full owner mutation binding, and the mutation identity itself does not
assert proposal destination equals successor-value destination.

The smallest repair is to reuse the existing bounded owner mutation semantics
or add the omitted exact identity/position equalities, plus one coordinated
anchor-tip mutant and one coordinated owner proposal/tip mutant. No coordinator,
inventory, or new public record is justified.

### `OP-R92-003` — successor use eligibility does not validate `Dsrp` context

The final normative order requires a successor's exact `Dsrc`, successor
anchor/owner selection, readbacks, custody observation, and downstream `Dsrp`
to validate before anchor ACTIVE, use intent, E0, or context issuance. The
candidate accepts no `Dsrp` record or validated post-selection composition.
It merely requires two caller-supplied expectation scalars: a non-null SHA-256
`successorPostSelectionReceiptDigest` and a
`successorPostSelectionReviewCoreDigest` equal to the anchor's `Dsrc`.

The committed successor fixture demonstrates the gap: it supplies an arbitrary
64-hex receipt digest, no post-selection receipt, no value/proposal/tip
readbacks, and no destination-lock/custody observation, yet validation returns
`[]`. Thus existence, exact `Dsrc`, path identity, selected owner triple,
readback, and custody context are not proven before the intent becomes
admissible.

The smallest repair is a private validated successor-post expectation or reuse
of the existing `validateDestinationOwnerSuccessorPostSelectionBinding`
composition, with genesis remaining exactly null and successor requiring the
exact validated `Dsrc`/`Dsrp` context. `Dsrp` must remain downstream and must not
enter the intent schema or digest.

### `OP-R92-004` — reviewed-installer bytes and review are not equal-bound to the anchor

The nested `bootstrap-reviewed-installer/v1` is only compared canonically with
another caller-supplied expectation record. Separately, a caller-supplied
`expected.reviewedInstallerDigest` is compared with the anchor. No equality
connects the nested installer's artifact or review receipt to the anchor's
`reviewedInstallerDigest` or `independentReviewReceiptDigest`.

The committed valid fixture makes the separation observable: its nested
`installerArtifactDigest` and `reviewReceiptDigest` differ from both anchor
members and validation still returns `[]`. Helper fields, transaction,
destination, state root, and custody instance are equal-bound; the reviewed
installer is not. The smallest repair is the exact ledger-prescribed installer
and review equalities plus independent substitution mutants, without adding a
new persisted digest or allowing the candidate to certify itself.
