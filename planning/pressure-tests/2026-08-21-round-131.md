## Graph order, time, and trust boundary

```text

prior state  -> native removal Dnr -> selected REVOKED  -> revoke post receipt -> archive
```

`Dnr` may contain prior `Dnc`; selected REVOKED may contain `Dnr`; neither
native receipt contains its selecting state proposal/tip, a downstream
